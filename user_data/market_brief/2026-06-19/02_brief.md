### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**Semiconductor Super-Cycle Enters Manufacturing Phase** `AI Compute` → ongoing

The session's dominant story was the convergence of three catalysts across the chip complex: Trump's Truth Social confirmation that **Apple** has agreed to work with **Intel** on U.S. chip design/manufacturing, Apple CEO Tim Cook's "100-year flood" memory shortage warning, and Intel's 18A-P node entering risk production.

- **INTC** +9.9%; Trump said Apple, Nvidia, and Tesla's TerraFab will all use Intel foundry. U.S. government's 9.9% stake (purchased at $20.47/share in Aug 2025) now worth ~$52B vs $8.9B cost. Intel also hired former SK Hynix CEO Seok-Hee Lee as EVP of advanced packaging/back-end manufacturing.
- **MU** +5.4% ahead of Jun 24 earnings ⚠️. Deutsche Bank raised PT to $1,500; Stifel to $1,500; Citigroup to $1,200; Wedbush to $1,300. Consensus Q3 EPS est $19.74 vs $1.91 YoY; revenue est $34.4B vs $9.3B YoY.
- **SNDK** +8.0%; **WDC** +3.5% (5D +44%); Apple's Cook said DRAM/NAND prices quadrupled, iPhone Pro memory component cost may jump from ~$39 to ~$145. TechInsights estimates iPhone Pro starting price could rise to $1,299 from $1,099.
- **MRVL** +9.9%; KeyBanc raised PT to $385; Nikkei reported plans to use TSMC's 1.4nm A14 process for next-gen AI chips. S&P 500 inclusion effective Jun 22 ⚠️. Tower Semiconductor announced 5M+ coherent photonic IC shipments to Marvell.
- **TSM** +6.1%; signed 10-year advanced packaging deal with Amkor in Arizona. Fell to #7 globally by market cap after SpaceX IPO.
- **ALAB** +10.1%, **ENTG** +11.6%, **LSCC** +7.2%, **UMC** +8.9%, **MKSI** +8.1% — broad semicap/materials/foundry rally.
- BofA Fund Manager Survey: 80% of global fund managers now call "long global semiconductors" the most crowded trade in history, up from 73% in May.
- UBS trading desk warned clients to "reduce risk meaningfully" in tech, calling the AI trade "extreme and increasingly binary."

**Oil Collapse & Iran MOU** `Macro / Commodities` → new

The U.S.-Iran 14-point Memorandum of Understanding was signed at Versailles, sending crude to its lowest since the conflict began and erasing the entire energy war premium.

- WTI fell ~2.2% to ~$75/bbl, touching its 200-day moving average (~$73.54) for the first time since the war began. Brent ~$78.66. Crude now down ~38% from April peak.
- National average gas fell below $4/gallon for the first time since late March (AAA).
- BofA cut 2026 Brent forecast to $82 from $93; sees $70 in 2027. Goldman expects Persian Gulf exports to normalize by end-July. IEA projects 5M bpd global surplus by 2027.
- MOU key terms: U.S. lifts naval blockade within 30 days; Iran allows free commercial transit for 60 days; U.S. terminates all sanctions; $300B reconstruction plan (with "regional partners"); Iran reaffirms no nuclear weapons, agrees to down-blend enriched uranium under IAEA supervision.
- VP Vance warned Israel against undermining deal; said "not a cent of American money goes to Iran." Iran's Supreme Leader issued written permission for MOU.
- Polymarket: 55% chance Hormuz traffic returns to normal by Jul 31; 53% chance of ≥1 Fed rate hike by year-end.
- Energy stocks hammered: XLE back to pre-war levels; CRK -40%, NOG -35%, VG -35%, BTU -28%, PBF -25% from March highs.
- Airlines rallied on lower fuel: **AAL** +3.4%, **CCL** +3.6%, **RCL** +3.8%.
- Tanker stocks diverged from oil: **FRO** +3.5% 5D, **STNG** +0.7% — market pricing freight rates, not crude.

**Fed Hawkish Pivot Under Warsh** `Macro` → new

Chair Kevin Warsh's first FOMC meeting held rates at 3.50%-3.75% unanimously but delivered a significantly hawkish shift in the dot plot and scrapped forward guidance.

- Median year-end 2026 rate projection raised to 3.8% from 3.4% in March. 9 of 18 officials now expect ≥1 hike before year-end.
- Warsh declined to submit his own dot, calling it "not helpful." Announced 5 task forces reviewing: communications, balance sheet, data sources, productivity/jobs in AI era, and inflation framework.
- "This committee will deliver price stability" — inflation has been above 2% target for 63 consecutive months.
- Citadel Securities warns "second-round effects" will force September hike; inertial Taylor Rule implies 75bp of hikes this year.
- CME FedWatch: 72.2% chance rates unchanged in July; 63.3% chance of hike by September.
- Dollar hit 13-month high. 10Y yield 4.45%, 2Y 4.17%.
- Mohamed El-Erian called press conference a "masterclass in central bank communication"; Claudia Sahm criticized Warsh for refusing to participate in SEP exercise.
- Markets sold off sharply Wednesday (S&P -1.21%, Nasdaq -1.34%) then rebounded Thursday (S&P +1.08%, Nasdaq +1.91%).
