### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**Semiconductor Selloff & AI Capex Debate** `AI Compute / Memory` → ongoing

The sharpest two-day **SOXX** decline since March 2025 (-12.9%) hit every corner of the chip complex, dragging **MU** -5.5%, **SNDK** -14.1%, **LRCX** -7.3%, **KLAC** -5.3%, **AMAT** -3.8%, and **AAOI** -15.8% on Thursday alone. The selloff compounds Wednesday's carnage when **GLW** fell 13.2% and **CRWV** plunged 13.9%.

- **Michael Burry** disclosed shorts on **NVDA** at $198.09, **TSLA** at $416.22, **CAT** at $1,060.98, **AMAT** at $729.40, and **SOXX** puts rolled to March 2027; called the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index extension above its 200-day MA "historically high" and "the beginning of the end."
- **MU**: Burry calls MU a "destroyer of capital" with 42-year median ROIC of 4%, negative FCF 48% of the time; stock 200-day MA extension "never as extended as in 2026, not even during the dot-com peak." BofA Mohan reiterated Buy on **SNDK**, raised PT from $2,100 to $2,500, modeling $9.1B June-quarter revenue vs $8.35B consensus. Bernstein raised SNDK PT to $3,000.
- **SOXX** RSI exited overbought for the first time since late March after 12 straight weeks above the threshold—longest streak of the current cycle.
- **Micron** still commands the most analyst bullishness: Cantor Fitzgerald street-high PT $2,000; Barclays $2,000; Raymond James $1,500.
- Cleveland Fed's **Beth Hammack** warned AI infrastructure demand is "insatiable" and could keep inflation elevated, potentially requiring higher rates.
- Leveraged ETF daily rebalancing flows hit record $50B (4x since start of 2026), now 1.60% of S&P 500 futures volume—amplifying end-of-day volatility.

**Rotation Into Blue Chips & Defensives** `Macro / Sector Rotation` → ongoing

The Dow Jones hit a fresh record above 52,900 on Thursday, outperforming the Nasdaq 100 (-1.9%) by 2.6 percentage points. XLV (Healthcare) +2.2%, XLP (Consumer Staples) +1.7%, XLF (Financials) +0.7% led sectors. Gold +2.3% to ~$4,124/oz; silver +3.2% to ~$61.

- June nonfarm payrolls: 57K vs 110K est (100K FactSet); prior two months revised down by combined 74K. Unemployment rate edged to 4.2% (vs 4.3% est) but labor force participation fell to 61.5%, lowest since March 2021.
- CME FedWatch: July hike probability collapsed to ~20% from ~64% a day earlier; Polymarket prices 10% chance of July hike.
- Fed's **Warsh** at ECB Sintra: inflation "too high" but expectations have "moderated" since May swearing-in. Fed's **Daly**: "I'm a gradualist"; can't give forward guidance; oil coming down is "good news."
- WTI crude fell to ~$68/bbl, fully erasing the Iran war premium. Citi forecasts Brent $60 by year-end. Gasoline national avg $3.82/gal; DOJ/FTC sent letter to state AGs urging investigation of elevated pump prices.

**Meta Cloud Pivot & Neocloud Pressure** `AI Cloud Infrastructure` → new

**META** surged 8.8% Wednesday on Bloomberg report of "Meta Compute" cloud business to sell excess AI capacity, then gave back 4.9% Thursday as investors digested capex implications and Zuckerberg admitted AI bets "haven't come to fruition yet."

- Zuckerberg internal town hall: "trajectory of agentic development over at least the last four months hasn't really accelerated the way we expected"; reorganization "wasn't as clean as it could have been."
- BofA analyst Post: every GW of AI infra made available externally could generate ~$20B annual revenue; cumulative capex ~$850B through 2030 implies ~19 GW of capacity.
- **CRWV** -13.9% Wed, -0.3% Thu; **NBIS** -17.0% Wed, +2.1% Thu. Rosenblatt (Buy, $250 PT on CRWV) said Meta likely cannot resell CoreWeave-leased capacity.
- Nebius Q1 2026: revenue $399M (+684% YoY), raised 2026 capex guidance to $20-25B from $16-20B; 45% adjusted EBITDA margin on AI business.
- Samsung Foundry reportedly in talks with Meta for MTIA Gen-3 chip manufacturing ($6.5B+ deal); Anthropic also reportedly exploring custom chip with Samsung on 2nm process.
- Meta's "Watermelon" AI model reportedly matches OpenAI GPT-5.5 on key benchmarks per Alexandr Wang.

**Enterprise AI Data Sovereignty** `Enterprise AI / SaaS` → ongoing

**PLTR** +5.2% after DA Davidson upgraded to Buy from Neutral, raised PT $165→$175, calling current valuation a "gift."

- CEO **Alex Karp** on CNBC: "Something has gone completely wrong" with OpenAI/Anthropic token-based pricing; enterprises increasingly switching to open-weight models.
- David Sacks endorsed Karp's thesis, citing Anthropic's Claude Design launch after a board member served on **FIG** board; Figma shares down ~50% YTD while Anthropic valuation surged.

- Guggenheim upgraded **CRM** to Buy/$228 (from Neutral), arguing 73% of software names trade at or below a "zero growth" multiple of 5.4x recurring revenue—worst since the GFC.
- **ADBE** +4.1% after HSBC upgraded to Buy/$308; weak jobs data eased rate-hike fears for long-duration growth names.
