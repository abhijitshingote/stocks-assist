### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**Meta Compute Fractures the AI Trade** `AI Cloud / AI Infra` → new

**META** +9.5% on 2.5× average volume after Bloomberg reported the company is developing "Meta Compute"—an initiative to sell excess AI computing capacity to external developers, potentially offering both hosted model access (like AWS Bedrock) and raw GPU capacity (like CoreWeave). The initiative is led by Santosh Janardhan, Daniel Gross, and Dina Powell McCormick. Meta declined to comment; plans could change.

- The report triggered the session's sharpest rotation: software (**IGV** +4.2%, **FDN** +2.6%) surged while semiconductors (**SMH** −4.5%) and neocloud names cratered.
- **NBIS** −15.7%: directly threatened because Nebius has a multi-year capacity agreement with Meta worth up to $27B; Meta entering commercial cloud could make it both customer and competitor.
- **CRWV** −12.7%: Meta selling raw compute directly challenges CoreWeave's core GPU-as-a-service business.
- **GLW** −13.0%: Corning's AI premium (including $6B Meta connectivity deal) repriced as investors questioned whether hyperscaler capex flowing to third parties would slow.
- Samantha LaDuc (LaDuc Trading) warned the move creates a "butterfly effect" across the entire AI buildout stack—optics, photonics, semis, energy, industrials.
- **MAGS** (Roundhill Magnificent Seven ETF) +2.8%, validating a rotation back into cash-rich mega-caps from specialized infra plays.
