## Tape on Monday May 18, 2026

SPY -0.08%, QQQ -0.43%, SOXX -2.49%, IGV +1.19%, XLK -1.08%; the broad tape was flattish-to-soft, but wafer fab equipment (WFE) and process-control were hit hard as investors faded the AI-capex trade amid war headlines, oil volatility, and renewed rate-sensitivity in long-duration growth.

**Key macro/color (FACTS & CHATTER):**  
- Charles Schwab’s Monday open note flagged **war-related headlines**, an intra-day **pullback in oil and 10Y yields**, and a market shifting focus from “war + oil” back to **Fed path and Treasury supply**. *Source: Schwab market update, May 18, 2026 (FACTS).*  
- Pre-market commentary (Benzinga, PreMarket Playbook May 18) highlighted talk of a **temporary waiver on Iran oil sanctions**, knocking crude off morning highs and muddling the “war/oil” trade; net effect was a more **selective de-risking**, with higher-beta semis sold while software and some tech defensives were bought. *Source: Benzinga YouTube, May 18 (MARKET CHATTER).*  

The result: SOXX underperformed badly vs SPY/QQQ as investors took profit in AI-driven capex beneficiaries and rotated into software (IGV +1.19%).
