SPY -0.08%, QQQ -0.43%, SOXX -2.49%, IGV +1.19%, XLK -1.08%; semis and opticals traded heavy as investors rotated out of AI hardware and into software on mounting concerns about overbuild in AI data‑center capex, while broader indices were largely flat and rates/oil eased on tentative progress headlines around the Middle East war.
