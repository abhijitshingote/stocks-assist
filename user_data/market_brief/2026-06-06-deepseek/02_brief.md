### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**AI Semiconductor Rotation** `AI Compute / Memory` → ongoing

The most forceful rotation of the year hit semiconductors on Thursday and extended into Friday as **Broadcom** (not in screener) delivered a Q2 beat but merely reaffirmed its FY27 AI semiconductor target of >$100B, disappointing overheated expectations. The miss coincided with a May jobs report that printed 172,000 nonfarm payrolls (consensus 85,000) and upward revisions of 93,000 to March/April, sending the 2-year Treasury yield up 10 bps to 4.15% and pricing a near-certain December rate hike. The combination triggered aggressive profit-taking across the AI complex.

- **MU** -14.2%, **MRVL** -13.2%, **ARM** -12.9%, **AMD** -10.9%, **SNDK** -12.5%, **DELL** -8.1%, **STX** -9.0%, **GLW** -11.5% — all slid as the **iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX)** dropped >10% over two sessions.
- **NBIS** -13.9% and **CRWV** -9.1% underperformed within neocloud; NBIS also saw a $221 million insider sale from Nvidia director Mark Stevens.
- **COHR** -10.6%, **ALAB** -11.4%, **HPE** -9.0%, **STM** -9.8% fell in sympathy.
- Analysts: Bernstein’s Rasgon called Broadcom the AI guidance driver; Goldman Sachs reiterated Buy and lifted PT to $525; BofA raised to $530.
- CME FedWatch: 96% chance of at least one hike by the Dec. 9 FOMC meeting.
- **IONQ** -13.2% dropped amid the broader risk-off move and Quantinuum’s IPO fade from open.
- Next catalyst: **MU** earnings June 24 ⚠️.

**Defensive Rotation** `Staples / Healthcare / Utilities` → new

The sharpest sector divergence in months saw defensive stocks rally as tech plunged: **XLP** +2.2%, **XLV** +1.5%, **XLU** +1.2%. The moves were driven entirely by the macro rotation, not by company-specific news.

- **PG** +4.2%, **KO** +3.5%, **PGR** +4.6%, **WELL** +4.7%, **KMB** +5.4%, **KVUE** +5.3% — all defensive mega/large caps saw strong absolute gains.
- No individual earnings or guidance catalysts were present for these names.

**Space Sector Correction** `Space` → ongoing

Hopes for a rapid S&P 500 entry for SpaceX were dashed when S&P Dow Jones Indices declined to alter eligibility rules, removing a major passive-flow catalyst ahead of the June 12 IPO. The decision, combined with Morningstar’s $780B base-case valuation (vs. $1.77T IPO price), hit the entire sector.

- **RDW** -16.9%, **TSAT** -18.4% — among the hardest hit in the screener universe.
- Planet Labs (**PL**) fell ~25% despite a strong Q1 beat, after filing a $1.5B at-the-market equity offering.
- Redwire had reversed an earlier week gain on a strawberry-growing contract; momentum reversed sharply.

**Earnings Standouts** `Idiosyncratic`

- **BBCP** +30.5%: Concrete Pumping Holdings Q2 EPS $0.04 (est $0.01), revenue $106.8M (est $96.7M); raised FY26 revenue to $410M–$425M from $390M–$410M.
- **MRLN** +18.7%: Completed Critical Design Review for its C-130J autonomy program with USSOCOM; milestone validated architecture for large-aircraft autonomy.
- **ABM** +8.2%: Q2 adj EPS $0.90 (est $0.87), revenue $2.29B (est $2.21B); reaffirmed FY26 adj EPS $3.85–$4.15; data center, aviation segments strong.

- **CMG** +3.8%: JPMorgan upgraded to Overweight, PT $35.
