### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**Semiconductor / Memory Selloff & MU Earnings** `AI Compute / Memory` → ongoing

The dominant story: Tuesday's ~10% KOSPI crash — triggered by reports SK Hynix may be shifting DRAM capacity away from HBM — cascaded into U.S. memory and semiconductor names. **MU** fell 13.2% to $1,052, **SNDK** −14.1%, **MRVL** −8.7%, **ARM** −9.9%, **AMAT** −6.4%, **INTC** −5.0%, **AMD** −4.5%. All are bouncing 2–5% in Wednesday pre-market ahead of **MU** Q3 earnings after the close.

- **MU** Q3 consensus: EPS $20.76 (vs $1.91 YoY), revenue $35.75B (vs $9.3B YoY — 284% growth). Has beaten both lines for 8 consecutive quarters but closed lower post-earnings in 6 of 8.
- Options imply ~14% move in either direction for MU this week.
- Freedom Capital Markets' Jay Woods: "HBM is the growth story, but guidance is the stock story." Recommends taking profits; re-entry target $955 (20-day MA).
- BofA Securities (Vivek Arya): raised **MU** PT $950→$1,500, Buy; raised total semi industry sales forecast to $2.7T by 2030; HBM market to ~$246B by 2030 (from ~$35B in 2025).
- Futurum Equities (Rolf Bulk): **MU** PT $1,500, Buy. CEO Daniel Newman: "The era of memory as a commodity is over. Memory is strategic."
- BofA also raised PTs on **AMAT** ($540→$720), **LRCX** ($330→$480), **MRVL** ($240→$365), **CRDO** ($252→$340), **ARM** ($335→$460 Neutral), **INTC** ($135→$160), **ALAB** ($240→$450 Neutral).
- Wedbush's Dan Ives called the selloff a "gut check moment," not a demand signal; AI revolution "in the 3rd inning."
- SOX index has recorded 9 single-day gains of 5%+ in the last 60 sessions — a volatility pattern last exceeded during the dot-com bust.
- **SK Hynix** plans Nasdaq ADR listing July 10; targeting up to ₩45.45T ($29.4B) raise — would surpass **BABA**'s $21.8B 2014 record ADR listing.
- **QCOM** agreed to acquire Modular Inc (financial terms undisclosed; expects to issue up to 19.2M shares); reportedly in talks with ByteDance for custom chip design services. BofA maintains Underperform, PT $165→$195.
- Netherlands lobbying US to drop chip export curbs targeting **ASML** sales (Bloomberg).
- Next catalyst: **MU** earnings tonight after close ⚠️; **SMCI** earnings est. Aug. 4 ⚠️
