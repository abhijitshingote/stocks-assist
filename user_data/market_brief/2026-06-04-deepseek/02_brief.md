### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**AI Compute Demand** `AI Compute` → ongoing

**MRVL**, **AVGO**, **HPE**, and **DELL** reported or signaled unprecedented hyperscaler AI spending, while TSMC warned of structural capacity limits.

- **MRVL** +7.2% (5D +52.9%) after Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang explicitly stated Marvell is “the next trillion-dollar company” at Computex, citing its custom ASICs and optical interconnects as central to AI data centers. Nvidia committed a $2B investment under the NVLink Fusion partnership.
- **AVGO** Q2 FY26 AI semiconductor revenue reached $10.8B (+143% YoY); full-year 2026 AI revenue now guided to ~$56B. Total Q2 revenue $22.19B missed $22.27B estimate. Co-announced a $35B AI XPU financing platform with Apollo and Blackstone. Deutsche Bank raised AVGO PT to $515 from $430. Stock fell 13.8% after hours.
- **HPE** Q2 FY26 adjusted EPS $0.79 vs. $0.53 est.; AI systems backlog hit $6.3B. Raised FY26 adjusted EPS outlook to $3.35-$3.45 (prior $2.30-$2.50). Loop Capital upgraded to Buy, PT $75. Truist raised PT to $69.
- **DELL** previously reported fiscal Q1 AI server revenue $16.1B (+757% YoY); multiple analysts (Goldman Sachs, Mizuho, Bernstein) raised targets to $500. ⚠️
- **TSMC** CEO C.C. Wei said at annual shareholders meeting that production capacity will be constrained “for a very long time,” hinted at price hikes, and noted 3nm chips could see a 15% price increase in 2H 2026.
- **INTC** rose 4.5% as CEO Lip-Bu Tan stated all CEOs are calling requesting more AI CPUs, reaffirmed TSMC as a “very trusted partner,” and unveiled new AI inference hardware at Computex.

**Cybersecurity Spending** `Cybersecurity` → ongoing

**CRWD** and **PANW** posted beat-and-raise quarters fueled by AI threat acceleration, but both stocks sold off post-print as elevated expectations met reality.

- **CRWD** Q1 FY27 revenue $1.39B (+26% YoY) beat $1.36B est., adjusted EPS $1.10 beat $1.07 est. Raised FY27 net new ARR guide by >$50M. Announced 4-for-1 stock split (record Jun 25, split-adjusted trading Jul 2). CEO George Kurtz described a “Mythos moment” where AI and cybersecurity collide. Stock fell 10.5% after hours.
- **PANW** Q3 FY26 revenue ~$3.0B (+31% YoY) beat $2.94B est.; NGS ARR grew 60% to $8.1B. Raised FY26 full-year to $11.42B-$11.43B. 6 analysts raised price targets (Needham to $350, Rosenblatt to $355). Stock slipped 3.4% in premarket Wednesday.

**Energy & Geopolitics** `Macro` → ongoing

U.S.-Iran hostilities intensified and the House challenged executive war powers, while macro data signaled sticky inflation and resilient labor.

- U.S. House voted 215-208 to pass a War Powers Resolution directing Trump to withdraw troops from hostilities against Iran without congressional authorization. 4 Republicans joined Democrats. The measure faces Senate and likely presidential veto.
- U.S. Central Command carried out “self-defense strikes” on Iran’s Qeshm Island. Iran’s Foreign Minister claimed self-defense strikes on U.S.-used sites.
- WTI crude rose 2.5% to >$96/bbl. EIA reported a 7.97M barrel crude inventory draw (est. 2.9M draw).
- ISM Services PMI for May came in at 54.5 (est. 53.8), the highest in three months; Prices Paid reached 71.3.
- ADP Employment Change for May was +122K (est. +118K). Factory Orders for April rose 4.8% (est. 4.6%).
- Dallas Fed President Logan said the Fed may need to hike interest rates this year, stating “monetary policy is not restraining the economy.”
- Fed Beige Book reported slight-to-moderate economic activity, sustained price increases, and signs of “weakening consumer spending.”

**Memory & Storage Cycle** `Memory` → ongoing

- **SNDK** +4.0%, **WDC** +4.0%, **MU** +0.1% continued to benefit from AI-driven high-bandwidth memory (HBM) demand. JPMorgan raised its global memory market forecast to $1.7T by 2028.
- **MU** Q3 FY26 earnings expected June 24; estimates call for EPS $19.29 on revenue $33.88B. ⚠️

**📡 Undercurrents** *(developing, not yet dominant)*
- **SpaceX IPO** priced at $135/share; JPMorgan to hold a rare retail-investor pitch event. Space ETF AUM surpassed $5B for the first time.
- **Anthropic** confidentially filed for IPO; Polymarket gives 89% chance of listing by year-end.

- **Alphabet’s** upsized $84.75B capital raise includes $40B ATM, $15B convertible preferred, and $10B Berkshire Hathaway placement – potentially testing market liquidity before mega-IPOs.
