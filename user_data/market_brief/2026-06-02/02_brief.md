### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**NVDA Computex Reshapes the PC and AI Infrastructure Map** `AI Compute` → new

Jensen Huang's Computex 2026 keynote was the session's single most impactful event, radiating across semiconductors, software, cloud, and PC OEMs.

- **NVDA** +6.3% regular session. Unveiled **RTX Spark** superchip (Blackwell GPU + Arm-based N1X CPU co-designed with MediaTek), calling it "the most efficient PC chip ever." 30+ laptop models, 10 desktop models this fall from Dell, HP, ASUS, Lenovo, Microsoft Surface.
- **Vera CPU** introduced: Arm-based, designed for agentic AI inference; customers exploring include OpenAI, Anthropic, SpaceXAI, CoreWeave, Oracle Cloud.
- Huang: "Nvidia has really become an infrastructure company."
- **ARM** +16.4% to $410 — royalty on every N1X CPU shipped. Barclays raised PT to $360 Overweight; Mizuho to $425 Outperform; Wells Fargo to $410 Overweight. Jim Cramer: "obviously additive" for ARM.
- **INTC** -9.2% — RTX Spark directly challenges x86 dominance in consumer PCs. Intel separately revealed Crescent Island GPU targeting AI inference with LPDDR5 memory/air cooling (lower cost than HBM/liquid). Barclays PT raised to $100 Equal-Weight; Mizuho to $128 Neutral.
- **QCOM** -8.9% — Dragonfly AI data center launch overshadowed by NVDA Spark's 100+ TOPS performance on Windows-on-Arm.
- **AMD** +0.9% but -3.4% premarket. Extended AM5 support through 2029 at Computex. Cathie Wood sold $56.2M AMD, bought $67.3M NVDA on Monday. Barclays PT raised to $665 OW; Mizuho to $615 OP; TD Cowen to $600 Buy.
- **CDNS** +8.5% — Unveiled autonomous AI chip design agent with NVDA at Computex.
