This validates the demand thesis underpinning the year's top-performing stocks and sets up **MU**'s June 24 earnings as the week's most important single-stock event.

- **MU** +4.3% premarket; earnings June 24 ⚠️; consensus EPS $19.66 (vs $1.91 YoY), revenue $34.47B (vs $9.30B YoY)
- Deutsche Bank raised MU PT to $1,500 (from $1,000); Citigroup raised to $1,200 (from $840); TD Cowen raised to $1,500 (from $660) — all Buy
- Perplexity CEO Aravind Srinivas predicted MU could surpass META in market cap within 6-12 months if HBM remains the binding constraint
