### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**Hyperscaler Cloud Acceleration Validates AI Capex** `Cloud / AI Compute` → ongoing

**MSFT** +15.3% delivered the session's defining catalyst: Azure revenue +43% YoY (fastest since 2022), crossing $100B annual run-rate for the first time. Commercial RPO surged 84% to $678B. CEO Nadella emphasized "every model is substitutable" — Azure now offers 11,000+ models from OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Mistral, and Microsoft's MAI. Copilot seats passed 30M. Capex guided to $175B (down from $190B prior, due to useful-life accounting change from 15 to 25 years). **AMZN** extended the narrative after the bell: Q2 revenue $200.6B vs $196.5B est, EPS $5.75 vs $1.82 est. AWS grew 37%, its fastest in 18 quarters. CEO Jassy said 2026 capex raised to $220B (from ~$200B) on higher memory costs, capacity "still not enough" through 2027, and 2028 demand is "striking." Both AI and custom chip businesses exceeded $25B annualized run-rates.
