### Meta: AI capex surge, restructuring, and new social surfaces

- **$125–$145B 2026 AI capex vs investor skepticism**  
  [confirmed] Meta’s Q1 revenue grew **33% Y/Y** to over **$56B** with gross margin near **82%**, ad impressions up **19%**, and average ad prices up **12%**. ARPU hit **$15.82**, with North American ARPU nearly doubling in three years. Despite these fundamentals, the stock is cited as down ~6.5% YTD as management lifted 2026 capex guidance to **$125–$145B**, roughly double last year’s pace and far above the **~$28B** spent in 2023. Zuckerberg reiterated a strategy of scaling AI products first and monetizing later, leaving investors focused on timing and magnitude of AI revenue.
