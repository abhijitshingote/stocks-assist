### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**Semiconductor Rout Deepens — SOXX/IGV Ratio Posts Worst Month Ever** `AI Compute / Memory` → ongoing

The chip-to-software rotation that began in early July accelerated Friday, with SOXX now down ~20% MTD vs IGV — the largest monthly divergence on record going back to 2001. TSMC's decision to raise 2026 capex to $60–$64B while posting record Q2 profit ($40.2B revenue, +33.7% YoY) paradoxically deepened the selloff as investors questioned whether hyperscaler spending can sustain at the pace already priced in. Moonshot AI's release of Kimi K3 (2.8T-parameter open-weight model priced at ~$12/million tokens) added a new overhang on GPU demand assumptions.

- **AMD** -5.7%; BNP Paribas raised PT to $600 (from $460) on higher multiples, not higher estimates — next demand catalyst expected late Q3
- **MU** -6.2% (5D -14.3%); U.S. House Committee urged Trump admin to ban Chinese memory chips; Alger's Ankur Crawford called MU her top single-stock pick, citing cash flow potential of 30–50% of market cap over 18 months; short interest elevated; MU forward P/E now 19.3x vs 2-year avg ~25x
- **ALAB** -9.1% (5D -24.1%); insider sales by Board Chairman Alba and Director Dyckerhoff under 10b5-1 plans; next earnings Aug 4
- **UMC** -10.3%; swept up in foundry selloff
- **DELL** -4.0% (5D -12.2%); AI server margin and capex concerns persist from Wednesday's 13% decline
