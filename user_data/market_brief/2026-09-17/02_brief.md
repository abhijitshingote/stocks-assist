### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**Semiconductor & AI Infrastructure Rebound** `AI Compute / Memory` → ongoing

The Monday AI-safety selloff has fully reversed. **INTC** +11.2% leads mega-caps on Reuters reporting SK Hynix is in early talks to manufacture memory at Intel's delayed Ohio fabs — either via lease or JV with cloud firms. Melius sees INTC at $200 in two years citing potential Apple/Tesla foundry wins; Tigress raised PT to $145. **AMD** +7.8% on Piper Sandler OW initiation ($600 PT; $2.2T compute market by 2030). **ARM** +8.7% after CEO Haas told CNBC demand has "never been stronger" and supply constraints could persist for years. **MRVL** +6.6% expanded GFS silicon photonics manufacturing deal for AI optical networking. **MU** +5.4% and **SKHY** +5.4% rally on Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan's AI Infrastructure Summit warning that memory prices have surged 5-7x and the shortage could worsen in 2027. **SMCI** +13.6% on broad sector sympathy.

- INTC: SK Hynix exploring lease or JV at Ohio fabs; nothing finalized; SK clarified "no specific plans or arrangements" confirmed.
- INTC: Melius Buy $165 PT, sees $200 in 2 years if 14A wins Apple/Tesla/hyperscaler; Tigress raised PT $118→$145.

- MU: Reports fiscal Q4 Sept. 30 ⚠️; analysts expect EPS $31.30, rev $50.78B.
