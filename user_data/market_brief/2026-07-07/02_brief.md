### 🧭 Narrative Threads

The chip complex staged a broad recovery from last week's sharp selloff, with the **VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH)** up 3.6%. Memory names led as investors positioned ahead of two catalysts this week: **Samsung**'s Q2 earnings Tuesday and **SK Hynix**'s Nasdaq ADR debut Friday.
