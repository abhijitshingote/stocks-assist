- SpaceX said the low-band spectrum enables indoor signal penetration; Musk called it "the last critical piece of the spectrum puzzle" and "an earthquake."
- FCC approved 15,000 Starlink Mobile satellites earlier this week; spectrum deal subject to final FCC approval.
- Gene Munster (Deepwater) says SpaceX will "eventually" make a smartphone; bigger problem for Samsung than Apple.
