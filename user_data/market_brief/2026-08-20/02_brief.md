### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**mRNA Cancer Vaccine Breakthrough — First Phase 3 Win** `Pharma / Oncology` → new

**MRK** +12.7% and MRNA +177% (Wednesday) on the first positive Phase 3 readout for an individualized mRNA cancer therapy. **TEM** +22.8% as the Merck/Moderna result validates its pending $1.5B Personalis acquisition. **TWST** +20.5% after Anthropic named it evaluator for AI protein design. **ILMN** +8.2% and **TXG** +10.6% ride the broader genomics/precision-medicine wave.

- INTerpath-001 enrolled 1,137 resected stage IIB–IV melanoma patients; intismeran autogene + Keytruda met primary (RFS) and key secondary (DMFS) endpoints vs Keytruda alone at pre-specified interim analysis.
- First Phase 3 win for any individualized neoantigen therapy and any mRNA-based cancer treatment; first Phase 3 to show improvement over Keytruda monotherapy in adjuvant resected melanoma.
- Merck/Moderna split costs and profits 50/50; Merck paid $200M upfront (2016) + $250M option exercise (2022).
- Morgan Stanley upgraded **MRK** Equal-Weight → Overweight, PT $116 → $179; raised **MRNA** PT $39 → $89 (Equal-Weight maintained).
- MRNA pre-consensus Hold rating, avg PT was $52.95 — stock now trades at ~$155 PM (down ~11% from Wed close of $174.38). Prior analyst high was $110.
- Nine Phase 2/3 trials in INTerpath program across melanoma, NSCLC, bladder, renal cell carcinoma; Phase 1 in pancreatic, gastric, lung cancers.
- BTIG raised **TEM** PT to $80, maintains Buy. Personalis provides sequencing platform Moderna uses to identify neoantigens for each patient.
- **TWST** hit 52-week high; Scorpion Capital published short report calling AI drug-discovery narrative "speculative delusion" — stock showed no negative reaction.
- BioNTech (BNTX) +22% Wed on peer effect; developing autogene cevumeran (BNT122), similar mechanism, in Phase 2 for colorectal and pancreatic cancers.
- **LLY** +5.2% partly on Amplitude Therapeutics taRNA vaccine collaboration for infectious diseases; separately, Novo Nordisk testing lower-dose Wegovy pills (WSJ).
