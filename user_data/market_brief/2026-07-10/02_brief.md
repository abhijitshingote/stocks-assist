**AI Memory & Storage Supercycle — Meta Capex Leak, Micron $250B, SK Hynix Debut** `Memory / AI Infra` → ongoing

The session's dominant thread. Meta's in-house "Iris" AI chip starts manufacturing in September (developed with Broadcom, fabricated by TSM).
