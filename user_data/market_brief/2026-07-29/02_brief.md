**AI Semiconductor / Memory Rout Deepens** `AI Compute / Memory` → ongoing

The Nasdaq 100 entered official correction territory Tuesday, down >10% from its early June high. The epicenter remains AI memory and semiconductor stocks, which collectively wiped out **>$2 trillion** in market cap since late-June highs. China's **ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT)** debuted +466% on Shanghai's STAR Market Monday, becoming China's most valuable listed company and reigniting fears of commodity DRAM oversupply. Separately, reports surfaced that China has begun mass-producing domestic DUV lithography equipment.
