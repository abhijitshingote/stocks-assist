- Patients treated with Lifyorli + nab-paclitaxel: 35% reduction in risk of death vs nab-paclitaxel alone (HR 0.65; p=0.0004)
- Median overall survival: 16.0 months vs 11.9 months for nab-paclitaxel alone
- No biomarker testing required
- Taxane-free interval ≤6 months subgroup HR: 0.60
- Patients who received taxane in most recent treatment regimen HR: 0.67
