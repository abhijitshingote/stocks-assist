# Semiconductors — benzinga

_kind: sector · elapsed: 181.3s_

Semiconductors

Semiconductors outperformed broader tech on Friday with **SOXX +2.33%**, **XLK +1.00%**, **IGV +1.65%**, **QQQ +0.42%**, and **SPY +0.39%**, but leadership rotated sharply beneath the surface as AI‑infrastructure capex and stock‑specific catalysts drove a wide dispersion in single‑name moves. Across the stack—GPUs/CPUs, foundry, memory, analog/RF, and equipment—the common thread was positioning for a still‑early AI buildout, even as some of the biggest winners like **NVDA** consolidated.

On the GPU/CPU and platform side, **Nvidia (NVDA -2.27% to $215.23)** lagged the sector despite delivering what Benzinga repeatedly characterized as a “crushing” or “blockbuster” fiscal Q1: revenue of **$81.6B–$81.615B, up ~85% Y/Y and above ~$79–$78.8B estimates**, data center revenue of **$75.2B (~92% of total)**, and **EPS $1.87 vs. $1.76–$1.77 est.**.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52750657/nvidia-crushes-earnings-but-ai-chip-supply-crunch-looms][https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52763701/consumer-tech-news-may-18-22-meta-cuts-8000-jobs-nvidia-posts-strong-earnings-more] The company paired that with an **$80B share repurchase authorization** and a **25x dividend increase** (quarterly dividend lifted from **$0.01 to $0.25**, or **$1.00 annual**, roughly in line with Apple’s **$1.08**).[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52750657/nvidia-crushes-earnings-but-ai-chip-supply-crunch-looms][https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52762325/amc-entertainment-nvidia-arm-holdings-and-more-5-stocks-investors-couldnt-stop-buzzing-about-this-week] Guidance called for **Q2 revenue of $91B**, which some coverage suggested could be conservative given China H200 constraints and only **10 Chinese companies** currently allowed to buy, and NVDA reiterated **~75% non‑GAAP gross margin** guidance.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/05/52762982/nvidia-stock-nears-key-price-as-huang-pitches-200-billion-cpu-market][https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52750657/nvidia-crushes-earnings-but-ai-chip-supply-crunch-looms] Management framed the quarter as still **supply‑constrained**, with a “tight supply of memory chips” and the new **VeraRubin** system described as **“supply‑constrained for the foreseeable future,”** underscoring that growth is being capped by HBM and broader supply chain tightness rather than demand.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52750657/nvidia-crushes-earnings-but-ai-chip-supply-crunch-looms] 

Beyond the print, Benzinga’s industry screen highlighted NVDA’s scale and profitability versus the Semiconductors & Equipment group: **ROE 33.06% vs 7.29% industry**, **EBITDA $71.0B vs $2.72B** (26.1x higher), **gross profit $61.16B vs $3.32B** (18.4x), and **revenue growth 85.23% vs 45.1%**.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52770405/investigating-nvidias-standing-in-semiconductors-amp-semiconductor-equipment-industry-compared-to-c] Valuation remains unusual for a mega‑cap: P/E **32.98**, well below the industry **161.46**, but with P/B **26.68** and P/S **20.78** above peers’ **13.56** and **18.62**, and a debt‑to‑equity ratio of **0.06** that Benzinga reports as lower than NVDA’s top four peers and cited as evidence of a “stronger financial position.”[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52770405/investigating-nvidias-standing-in-semiconductors-amp-semiconductor-equipment-industry-compared-to-c] The company’s market cap is now **> $5.2T**, exceeding the total market caps of Taiwan’s **TAIEX ($4.95T)** and India’s **NSE ($4.92T)**, and one piece notes NVDA supplies GPUs for **52% of all tracked sovereign AI infrastructure projects worldwide**, powering projects like the Abu Dhabi Sovereign AI Cloud, Poland’s AGH Cyfronet Helios, and Japan’s ABCI 3.0.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/05/52769931/nvidia-surpasses-taiwan-and-india-stock-markets-as-analysts-eye-10-trillion-valuation][https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52752173/nvidia-powers-52-of-sovereign-ai-projects-so-whos-really-sovereign]
