### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**IBM Preliminary Q2 Miss — Enterprise IT Spending Disruption** `Enterprise IT / SaaS` → new

**IBM** -22.6% pre-market after CEO Arvind Krishna released a shareholder letter disclosing preliminary Q2 results that missed on both lines. The shortfall was concentrated in Z mainframe and related Transaction Processing software as enterprise clients shifted capex toward servers, storage and memory in the final weeks of June to secure supply-constrained infrastructure. **NOW** fell ~8% in sympathy pre-market after rising +2.3% Monday, given its deep partnership with IBM. **MSFT** also fell ~3% pre-market amid broader enterprise software pressure, compounded by its own layoffs (~4,800, with up to 5,700 more) and a shift to in-house MAI models replacing OpenAI/Anthropic in Excel and Outlook.

- **IBM** prelim Q2 revenue $17.2B (+1% YoY) vs $17.86B est; adj EPS $2.93 (+5% YoY) vs $3.022 est; GAAP EPS $2.27 (-2% YoY)
- Software revenue +5%; Consulting flat; Infrastructure -7%; Red Hat +11%
- Distributed Infrastructure +37% (best in reported history); backlog ~$500M exiting Q2
- Z mainframe still 130% program-to-program vs z16; "numerous large deals failed to close"
- Lightwell AI security platform launched July 8; $5B commitment, 20,000+ engineers
- $1B CHIPS incentives + $1B IBM cash for Anderon quantum wafer foundry; >$10B quantum investment over 5 years
- H1 2026 operating cash flow $7.8B; free cash flow $4.8B
- Morgan Stanley maintains Equal-Weight, raises PT to $293 (from $267)
- Full Q2 results and outlook on July 22 earnings call ⚠️
