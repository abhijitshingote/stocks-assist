### Marvell (MRVL) – AI data‑center beat, aggressive multi‑year guide, rich expectations

- **Quarter and guidance:**  
  - **Q1 FY27:** MRVL reported **record revenue of $2.418B (+28% Y/Y, +9% Q/Q)** and non‑GAAP EPS of **$0.80**, **$0.01** above the midpoint of guidance and modestly ahead of consensus ($0.80 vs $0.79; $2.418B vs $2.404B).[https://www.benzinga.com/news/earnings/26/05/52824218/marvell-tech-q1-adj-eps-0-80-beats-0-79-estimate-sales-2-418b-beat-2-404b-estimate][https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call]  
  - **Q2 FY27 guide:** Revenue was guided to **$2.7B (+12% Q/Q, +35% Y/Y)** at the midpoint, or **$2.565B–$2.835B**, with non‑GAAP EPS **$0.88–$0.98** vs a **$0.90** consensus.[https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/05/52824374/marvell-tech-sees-q2-adj-eps-0-88-0-98-vs-0-90-est-sees-sales-2-565b-2-835b-vs-2-599b-est][https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/05/52829008/snowflake-nebius-salesforce-marvell-technology-and-astrotech-corp-why-these-5-stocks-are-on-investors-radars-today]  
  - **Segment mix:** Data‑center revenue, now **76% of total**, was **$1.83B**, up **11% Q/Q** and **27% Y/Y**, with management projecting the segment to grow **~50% in FY27** and **~55% in FY28** after **46% growth in FY26**.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call]  
  - **Interconnect & switching:** MRVL lifted FY27 **interconnect** growth expectations to **>70% Y/Y** (from ~50%) and now sees **scale‑out switch** revenue exceeding **$600M in FY27** (2x FY26) and reaching **> $1B annualized in FY28**, supported by 800G and 1.6T PAM4, 400G‑per‑lane, and coherent‑light 1.6T roadmaps.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call]  

- **Multi‑year AI roadmap and financial targets:**  
  - CEO Matt Murphy said **AI‑related bookings continued to accelerate** and MRVL “significantly raised its revenue outlook for fiscal 2027 and fiscal 2028.”[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/05/52829008/snowflake-nebius-salesforce-marvell-technology-and-astrotech-corp-why-these-5-stocks-are-on-investors-radars-today]  
  - The company now expects **FY27 revenue to grow ~40% Y/Y to nearly $11.5B**, with **FY28 revenue targeted at ~$16.5B (+45% Y/Y)**, about **$1.5B** above its prior FY28 projection.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call][https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/05/52826602/marvell-tech-ceo-says-now-expect-overall-revenue-in-fiscal-2027-to-grow-about-40-yoy-to-nearly]  
  - **Custom silicon:** FY27 custom revenue is expected to grow **>20% Y/Y** and then **more than double in FY28**, with management reiterating a **> $10B custom‑revenue target in FY29** based on an implied **$55B TAM and ~20% share** and **>50‑program** opportunity set across all U.S. hyperscalers.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call][https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/05/52827183/marvell-technology-ceo-says-now-expect-custom-revenue-to-more-than-double-yoy-in-2028-custom-busines]  

- **Optical, interconnect, and CXL/NIC positioning:**  
  - MRVL already ships **data‑center interconnect (DCI) to all five major U.S. hyperscalers** and expects DCI module revenue to grow from **~$500M in FY26** to **~$1B annualized in FY28**, as scale‑across AI networks adopt secure **1.6T ZR/ZR+ modules** based on a **2nm coherent DSP** sampling this year.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call]  
  - The company has shipped **>1M DCI modules** powered by silicon photonics and accumulated **>15B device‑hours** of field data; it expects **scale‑up optics revenue next fiscal year to be >2x** its prior **~$150M** outlook as CPO/NPO ramps with its **third‑gen 6.4T light engine** and Celestial AI’s photonic fabric.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call]  
  - MRVL highlighted **>10 XPU‑attach programs**, including **CXL and NIC** products, and said memory‑architecture challenges and inference KV‑cache needs are increasing demand for **CXL‑based memory‑expansion and NIC solutions**, positioning it as an end‑to‑end provider of **PCIe switches, PCIe retimers, CXL switches, and memory expanders**.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call]  

- **Nvidia partnership, Polariton deal, and 5G/6G AI RAN:**  
  - MRVL detailed three pillars of its **Nvidia** partnership: (1) an **optics partnership** extending its DSP/TIA/driver franchise into **silicon photonics**; (2) **NVLink Fusion**, enabling MRVL custom chips and networking silicon to interface directly with Nvidia infrastructure; and (3) **AI RAN**, integrating MRVL’s **Octeon base‑station processors** with Nvidia GPUs so telcos can run **5G/6G radio plus AI workloads on the same platform**.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call]  
  - MRVL acquired **Polariton**, a plasmonics‑based silicon‑photonics developer whose **>1 THz modulator bandwidth** (up to **10x** higher than current solutions) is aimed at pushing MRVL’s interconnect roadmap to **3.2T and beyond**.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call]  
  - Management also cited **UA Link, ESUN, and NVLink** scale‑up switching solutions, with each Tier‑1 engagement described as a **multi‑billion‑dollar lifetime revenue opportunity**, underscoring the size of emerging AI‑cluster fabrics.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call]  

- **Profitability, capacity, and capital returns:**  
  - Q1 non‑GAAP gross margin was **58.9%**, GAAP gross margin **52.1%**, with non‑GAAP opex **$577M** and non‑GAAP operating margin **35%**; MRVL targets exiting FY28 at the **upper end of a 38–40% non‑GAAP operating‑margin range** as opex grows mid‑ to high‑teens.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call]  
  - The company plans **~$1B of supply prepayments in FY27**, starting Q2, to secure long‑term capacity; Q1 operating cash flow was **$639M**, inventory **$1.4B**, debt **$4.96B**, with gross debt/EBITDA **1.44x** and net debt/EBITDA **0.32x**.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call]  
  - Capital returns included **$200M of buybacks** and **$54M of dividends** in Q1.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call]  

Despite this fundamentally strong setup, MRVL’s shares finished **-3.31%** on the verified tape as the market digested the prior rally and rich AI expectations into the print, even as Benzinga’s trend metrics still characterized the stock’s price trend as strong across time frames with a “good growth score.”[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/05/52831354/stock-market-today-sp-500-dow-futures-decline-as-us-and-iran-exchange-strikes-snowfalke-salesforce-costco-in-focus]
