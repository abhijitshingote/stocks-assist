# Wireless & Mobile — benzinga

_kind: theme · elapsed: 103.3s_

Wireless & mobile chip stocks traded **mixed-to-weaker** on May 27, with most RF/handset names selling off alongside semis while AI‑levered connectivity plays held up better or gained.

The **tape** showed broad pressure across RF, baseband and display/input names versus slightly negative tech benchmarks: **QCOM -7.80% (close $228.12)**, **SWKS -5.61% ($78.46)**, **QRVO -4.28% ($103.38)**, **HIMX -4.62% ($20.55)**, **SYNA -2.24% ($142.30)**, **INDI -0.19% ($5.12)**, and **MRVL -3.31% ($200.76)**, against **SPY -0.01%**, **QQQ -0.11%**, **SOXX -1.07%**, **IGV -1.04%**, **XLK -0.43%**. AVGO and MXL were notable outliers, with **AVGO +0.33% ($424.05)** and **MXL +4.25% ($99.78)** outperforming as investors favored names tied more directly to datacenter and connectivity over traditional handset/RF exposure.

On the **decliner side**, several moves were news‑linked rather than purely macro. **QCOM -7.80%** reversed recent AI strength despite a Bloomberg‑reported win at ByteDance, which plans to buy “millions” of Qualcomm AI ASICs for its data centers, extending QCOM beyond smartphones into AI infrastructure.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52816461/what-is-going-on-with-qualcomm-stock-on-wednesday] Benzinga framed the drop as profit‑taking after a sharp AI‑chip rally and broader semiconductor weakness, even as Communication Services gained intraday; options flow still showed a bullish call sweep in longer‑dated $270 strikes, and the next major catalyst is late‑July earnings with Street modeling EPS down YoY on slightly lower revenue.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52816461/what-is-going-on-with-qualcomm-stock-on-wednesday][https://www.benzinga.com/insights/options/26/05/52818901/10-information-technology-stocks-whale-activity-in-todays-session] **SWKS, QRVO, SYNA, HIMX and INDI** saw no name‑specific headlines in this packet and traded lower in sympathy with a weak SOXX/SMH tape and rotation away from less AI‑levered consumer/mobile chains.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/05/52818706/dow-record-high-chip-stocks-tumble-oil-slides-iran-deal-whiplash-markets-wednesday]

**MRVL -3.31%** was the key fundamental story: the stock sold off after a very strong **Q1 FY27** and a major AI‑driven guide‑up, illustrating a classic “sell‑the‑news” dynamic after an extended AI run. Marvell reported **record Q1 revenue of $2.418B**, up roughly **28% YoY / 9% QoQ**, with non‑GAAP EPS **$0.80** modestly above guidance and Street.[https://www.benzinga.com/news/earnings/26/05/52824218/marvell-tech-q1-adj-eps-0-80-beats-0-79-estimate-sales-2-418b-beat-2-404b-estimate][https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call] Data center contributed about **76% of revenue** and grew high‑20s percent YoY, with data‑center revenue at **$1.83B** up double‑digits sequentially.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call] For **Q2**, management guided revenue to **$2.7B ±5%** (about **35% YoY** at the midpoint) and non‑GAAP EPS **$0.88–0.98** versus a ~$0.90 consensus, with GAAP EPS **$0.32–0.42**.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call][https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/05/52824374/marvell-tech-sees-q2-adj-eps-0-88-0-98-vs-0-90-est-sees-sales-2-565b-2-835b-vs-2-599b-est]

More importantly for the sector, **Marvell sharply raised its multi‑year AI roadmap**. It now expects **FY27 revenue to grow ~40% YoY to nearly $11.5B**, with data‑center revenue up roughly **50%** and **interconnect >70%**.[https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/05/52826602/marvell-technology-ceo-says-now-expect-overall-revenue-in-fiscal-2027-to-grow-about-40-yoy-to-nearly][https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call] For **FY28**, Marvell now targets **~$16.5B** revenue (about **45%** growth), lifting its prior outlook by **$1.5B**, with data center expected to accelerate to roughly **55%** growth and custom silicon revenue more than doubling.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call][https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/05/52826627/marvell-technology-ceo-says-now-expect-fiscal-2028-revenue-to-reach-approximately-16-5b-expect-q2-da] Interconnect is a major driver: Marvell is already shipping DCI to **all five major US hyperscalers**, is targeting **$1B annualized DCI revenue in FY28** (vs. about $500M in FY26), expects scale‑out switching revenue to exceed **$600M in FY27** and track to **>$1B annualized in FY28**, and reiterated a **> $10B** custom‑revenue target for FY29 on 10+ XPU‑attach programs and new Tier‑1 wins.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call]

That AI push is underpinned by both product and ecosystem moves. Marvell detailed an expanded **Nvidia partnership** spanning optics and **silicon photonics**, NVLink Fusion integration for its custom and networking silicon, and **AI RAN** work that lets its Octeon base‑station processors run 5G/6G alongside AI workloads on a combined Nvidia platform.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call] It also announced the acquisition of **Polariton**, a plasmonics‑based silicon‑photonics firm whose modulators have demonstrated **>1 THz** bandwidth, which Marvell plans to use to extend its DCI/coherent optics roadmap to **3.2T and beyond**.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call] On capital and capacity, Q1 cash from operations reached a record **$639M**; Marvell repurchased **$200M** of stock, paid **$54M** in dividends, reported gross debt of **$4.96B** (net leverage low), and plans roughly **$1B** of supply prepayments in FY27 to secure AI capacity, while targeting the upper end of its **38–40%** non‑GAAP operating‑margin model by FY28 as opex grows far slower than revenue.[https://www.benzinga.com/insights/news/26/05/52827733/full-transcript-marvell-tech-q1-2027-earnings-call]

Despite those numbers and pre‑print enthusiasm—MRVL had rallied into earnings, was **17.9% above its 20‑day SMA and 112% above its 200‑day SMA** with an RSI above 75 and a cluster of fresh target hikes (HSBC to **$300**, Susquehanna to **$230**, Morgan Stanley to **$172**, Cantor to **$190**)[https://www.benzinga.com/trading-ideas/movers/26/05/52809580/marvell-earnings-due-after-the-bell-what-analysts-and-charts-are-signaling]—the stock ultimately finished **down 3.31%**. It had surged **6.17% pre‑market to $221.12** and traded up over **8% after hours to $215.06** following the print, but regular‑session selling into a crowded AI trade and broader semi softness saw investors fade the guidance and multi‑year AI upgrade.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/05/52800359/marvell-technology-surges-over-6-in-pre-market-ahead-of-q1-earnings][https://www.benzinga.com/markets/earnings/26/05/52824993/marvell-shares-climb-after-q1-results-beat-estimates-details]

On the **advancer side**, investors gravitated to AI/datacenter and connectivity leverage. **AVGO +0.33%** modestly outperformed SOXX and XLK while trading around several product and AI headlines and an upcoming June 3 earnings print.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/large-cap/26/05/52822633/whats-going-on-with-broadcom-stock-wednesday-5] Broadcom announced a collaboration with Samsung on a **fixed‑wireless access (FWA) platform** that combines its **BCM6776 Wi‑Fi 8 SoC** with Samsung’s **B1320 5G modem** for global broadband operators, and launched three new **Wi‑Fi 8** gateway chips (BCM6772/6774/6776) integrating processor, Wi‑Fi radios and Ethernet to lower cost and power for mesh and multi‑gig routers.[https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/05/52805319/broadcom-partners-with-samsung-electronics-over-new-optimized-reference-platform-for-global-fixed-wi][https://www.benzinga.com/markets/large-cap/26/05/52822633/whats-going-on-with-broadcom-stock-wednesday-5] On the AI side, Broadcom highlighted a **strategic partnership with FuriosaAI** to co‑develop a next‑gen inference platform for large‑scale agentic AI workloads, with sampling targeted for **1H 2028**.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/large-cap/26/05/52822633/whats-going-on-with-broadcom-stock-wednesday-5] Street expectations ahead of earnings call for a major step‑up in scale (EPS and revenue both up strongly YoY), and AVGO remains broadly rated **Buy** with a high‑$400s average target and several recent PT hikes; AVGO’s heavy weights in major tech ETFs add an incremental flow tailwind.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/large-cap/26/05/52822633/whats-going-on-with-broadcom-stock-wednesday-5] Intraday, shares were briefly **down 0.29% at $420.80** before closing higher.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/large-cap/26/05/52822633/whats-going-on-with-broadcom-stock-wednesday-5]

**MXL +4.25%** outperformed sharply on a focused 5G FWA narrative. MaxLinear and **GCT Semiconductor** announced a strategic partnership to build **highly integrated, cost‑effective 5G FWA and converged gateways** for consumer and enterprise, with joint demos planned at **Computex Taipei (June 2–5)**.[https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/05/52801565/maxlinear-and-gct-semiconductor-partner-to-develop-][https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52814072/why-gct-semiconductor-stock-fell-despite-new-maxlinear-5g-partnership] The stock also benefited from a fresh **Buy initiation from Benchmark with a $125 PT**, and intraday data showed shares up mid‑single digits around $98 before closing near $100.[https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/05/52809486/benchmark-assumes-maxlinear-at-buy-announces-price-target-of-125]

Across the **wireless & mobile complex**, the day highlighted an ongoing rotation theme: investors are rewarding RF/baseband vendors with **clear AI‑infrastructure or datacenter connectivity visibility** (MRVL, AVGO, to some extent QCOM via ByteDance ASICs and MXL via FWA/edge) and derating those more tied to traditional handset, display or consumer cycles (QCOM’s handset core, SWKS, QRVO, SYNA, HIMX, INDI). Marvell’s raised FY27–28 targets for interconnect, DCI and scale‑out switching underscore sustained demand for high‑speed SerDes, optical modules and PCIe/CXL attach, which is a constructive read‑through for peers like **AVGO and MXL**, while underscoring how scarce multi‑year AI visibility remains in classic RF chains.
