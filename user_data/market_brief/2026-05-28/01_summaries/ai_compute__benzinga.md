# AI Compute — benzinga

_kind: theme · elapsed: 81.2s_

AI compute was a modest drag on tech on Wednesday, with **SOXX -1.07%**, **IGV -1.04%**, and **XLK -0.43%**, while **SPY -0.01%** and **QQQ -0.11%** were nearly flat; within the group, **NVDA -0.59% ($212.35)**, **AMD -1.60% ($495.55)**, **INTC -2.29% ($120.48)**, **ARM -4.79% ($303.96)**, **CEVA -3.85% ($40.22)**, **AMBA -5.14% ($89.26)**, **SIMO -4.27% ($279.06)**, and **AIP -1.45% ($37.33)** all finished lower, while **ALAB +0.26% ($321.24)** was the lone green name in the set.  

- **NVDA -0.59% ($212.35)** — Nvidia slipped despite a steady stream of upbeat AI-compute headlines, including Tigress Financial reiterating **Strong Buy** and lifting its price target to **$425 from $360**, plus a Benzinga options scan flagging a **bearish same-day call sweep** on the **$212.50 strike** with **536 contracts** and **$31.1K** in premium.[https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/05/52811875/tigress-financial-maintains-strong-buy-on-nvidia-raises-price-target-to-425][https://www.benzinga.com/insights/options/26/05/52818901/10-information-technology-stocks-whale-activity-in-todays-session]  
- **NVDA** was also in focus after Jensen Huang said Nvidia will pour roughly **$150B per year into Taiwan**, up from about **$100B** today and **$10B-$15B** several years ago, while building a new Taiwan headquarters targeted to open by **2030** and near key partners including **TSM**, Foxconn, Wistron, and Quanta.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52799950/jensen-huang-says-nvidia-will-pour-150-billion-a-year-into-booming-ai-hub-taiwan]  
- Separate Benzinga coverage said Huang may join the advisory board of **Tsinghua University’s School of Economics and Management**, with the appointment not yet formally announced and Nvidia declining immediate comment.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52828982/jensen-huang-tsinghua-university-advisory-board-tim-cook-elon-musk]  
- Nvidia’s dividend reset also remained a notable backdrop: the company raised its quarterly dividend from **$0.01 to $0.25 per share**, and Benzinga estimated Huang’s annual dividend income could rise by about **$810.1M** based on his disclosed share count.[https://www.benzinga.com/trading-ideas/dividends/26/05/52812431/nvidia-dividend-boost-will-make-jensen-huang-810-million-richer-every-year]  
- Benzinga also highlighted Nvidia’s supply-chain leverage, citing TSMC leading-edge manufacturing and CoWoS packaging as key bottlenecks for H100/H200/Blackwell GPUs and noting that GPU backlogs had stretched to around **12 months** at points.[https://www.benzinga.com/Opinion/26/05/52819220/jensen-huang-is-quietly-locking-up-infrastructure-that-keeps-nvidia-untouchable]  

- **AMD -1.60% ($495.55)** — AMD weakened even as Benzinga described the stock as being in a “once-in-50-year run,” with shares up **148% so far in the quarter** and near a **52-week high of $506.96**; another report said AMD plans to invest more than **$10B across the Taiwan ecosystem** for advanced packaging and next-generation AI infrastructure.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52811968/amd-stock-is-on-a-once-in-50-year-run-and-it-looks-like-unfinished-business][https://www.benzinga.com/Opinion/26/05/52799950/jensen-huang-says-nvidia-will-pour-150-billion-a-year-into-booming-ai-hub-taiwan]  
- AMD’s Taiwan push was also tied to partnerships with **ASE, SPIL, Powertech Technology, Sanmina, and Wiwynn**, reinforcing the same AI supply-chain theme that benefits Taiwan manufacturing and packaging capacity.[https://www.benzinga.com/Opinion/26/05/52808834/nvidias-taiwan-investment-signals-a-new-ai-supply-chain-reality]  
- OneQode’s plan to deploy **AMD Instinct MI355X GPUs** and eventually integrate **AMD Helios** added another AI-read-through, though it did not stop the stock from closing lower.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52806131/what-is-going-on-with-amd-stock-on-wednesday]  

- **INTC -2.29% ($120.48)** — Intel fell despite ongoing government and strategic-interest headlines, including Benzinga’s note that the U.S. government holds an **$8.9B stake** that had grown to **beyond $50B** by May 18.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52829639/nancy-pelosi-stock-tracker-dell-pentagon-contract-shares-surge]  
- Intel also remained in the AI hardware conversation through a **12nm** joint program with UMC that is still on track, with certification underway at Intel’s Arizona facility and mass production expected in **2027**.[https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/05/52800607/united-microelectronics-stock-hits-52-week-high-heres-why]  
- Another Reuters-sourced Benzinga piece said ByteDance is developing its own CPUs for AI infrastructure, a reminder that CPU demand is being reshaped by custom silicon efforts from hyperscalers and large platforms.[https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/05/52830571/bytedance-developing-custom-cpu-chips-to-support-ai-rollout-sources-say-reuters-exclusive]  

- **ARM -4.79% ($303.96)** — Arm sold off sharply as Reuters reported ByteDance is developing in-house CPUs based on **Arm** and **RISC-V** architectures for AI rollout, underscoring both Arm’s importance to AI inference CPUs and the risk of customers moving toward custom designs.[https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/05/52830571/bytedance-developing-custom-cpu-chips-to-support-ai-rollout-sources-say-reuters-exclusive]  

- **CEVA -3.85% ($40.22)**, **AMBA -5.14% ($89.26)**, **SIMO -4.27% ($279.06)**, and **AIP -1.45% ($37.33)** all underperformed in a weak semiconductor session, but the provided articles do not include company-specific catalysts for those moves.[verified tape]  

- **ALAB +0.26% ($321.24)** was the only listed name to close higher, standing out against the broader chip pullback.[verified tape]  

- The broader macro backdrop remained supportive of the *AI compute* theme even as the tape was soft: Goldman Sachs Research said global data-center power demand could rise **50% by 2027** and as much as **165% by the end of the decade**, with AI’s share of the market rising from **14%** today to **27%** by 2027.[1]
