Memory and interconnect stocks traded **mixed-to-weak** against flat indices on Wednesday, with **MU and UMC** continuing to rerate higher on AI memory and foundry optimism while **GFS and smaller memory/IP names** sold off as the broader semiconductor complex digested Micron’s parabolic move.

Within a flat tape – **SPY -0.01% to $750.45**, **QQQ -0.11% to $729.44**, **SOXX -1.07%**, **IGV -1.04%**, **XLK -0.43%** – the memory & interconnect group saw **Micron (MU) +0.95% to $920.78** and **UMC +5.41% to $22.32** stand out against declines in **GFS -12.13%**, **RMBS -4.76%**, **GSIT -4.74%**, **MRAM -6.90%**, **AMKR -1.83%**, **TSEM -3.41%**, and **ASX -0.36%** (all close-to-close).
