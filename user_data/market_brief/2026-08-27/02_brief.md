### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**NVIDIA Q2 Blowout & AI Compute Supply Chain Lift** `AI Compute` → ongoing

**NVDA** reported Q2 FY2027 revenue of $96.22B (+106% YoY), beating the $92.18B consensus by $4B. Adjusted EPS $2.22 vs $2.10 est. Data center revenue $89B (+117% YoY). Q3 guidance of ~$108B above the $104.2B Street estimate. CFO Colette Kress projected 70% revenue growth for FY2028; CEO Jensen Huang said demand is "much greater than 70%" but supply-constrained. Gross margin held at 75%, guided to ~74% next quarter on rising HBM costs. Commitments jumped from ~$119B to $279B QoQ; inventory rose to $32B.

- NVDA agreed to acquire **Hugging Face** for $12.9B (vs. $150M annualized revenue), its second-largest deal after $20B Groq acquisition.
- Gene Munster (Deepwater): SpaceX now ~5% of NVDA revenue (~$4.8B), up from ~3% QoQ; reclassified into hyperscaler group.
- Michael Burry: NVDA appears "wildly undervalued" on paper at 32x P/E but stock is "treading water." Raymond James called the valuation discount "fundamentally illogical" — trades at <15x est. 2027 GAAP EPS vs S&P 500 at 19x.
- Ross Gerber: ~$10 EPS this year, historical 45x multiple implies ~$450.
- Analyst consensus: 30 analysts, average PT $316, Q3 target $330.
- Huang on CNBC dismissed OpenAI's Jalapeño chip: "Lots of projects get started. Lots of projects get canceled."
- Pre-market: NVDA +5.9% to ~$222.
