### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**Middle East Escalation Drives Oil Toward $100 and Rate-Hike Repricing** `Macro / Energy` → ongoing

The Iran-U.S. conflict escalated sharply Tuesday with CENTCOM destroying five Iranian crude tankers after IRGC ballistic missile attacks on a U.S. Navy warship. Iran subsequently fired missiles at a U.S. base in Jordan (18 of 20 intercepted). Houthis struck Saudi Aramco facilities, injuring 73. Brent crude closed at $99.47 (+1.6%); WTI at $94.58 (+1.7%).

- 10-year Treasury yield topped 4.80% intraday for the first time since October 2023; settled ~4.78%.
- CME FedWatch: 58% probability of a 25bp rate hike at Sept. 16 FOMC meeting, up from ~40% pre-payrolls.
- Former Fed economist **Claudia Sahm** now views a rate hike as "appropriate policy," says she is "no longer confident" inflation returns to 2% without one.
- Market strategist **James Thorne** warns hiking into an energy supply shock repeats the ECB/Fed's 2008 mistake.
- Goldman Sachs raised Brent year-end forecast to $90 from $80; sees $120/bbl if Gulf output remains 4M bpd below pre-war levels.
- National avg gas: $4.15/gal (record Labor Day); diesel: $5.90/gal nationally, nearing $8 in California.
- USO +1.2% Tuesday; +108% YTD. **MPC** +2.4%, **VLO** +3.5%, **PSX** +2.0% — UBS raised PTs on all three.
- PPI due Thursday, CPI due Friday — headline CPI seen at 3.4%, core at 2.4%.
- NFIB Small Business Optimism fell to 98.7 in August from 99.8, missing 99.3 estimate.
- Iran's IRGC Navy warned all tanker crews near Kuwaiti and Bahraini ports hosting Americans to evacuate.
- Trump posted that oil prices "will drop precipitously" once the war ends; predicted $2/gal gas.

**Biotech Rout: Novartis Double Trial Failure Cascades Across Sector** `Biotech / Pharma` → new

**NVS** -14% (worst session ever for ADRs), **AMGN** -9.6% (worst day since Oct 2000), **IONS** -13%, **SRPT** -8%, **DYN** -23%.

- Novartis disclosed pelacarsen Phase 3 Lp(a)HORIZON failed primary endpoint (no reduction in CV death, heart attack, stroke vs placebo despite cutting Lp(a) levels). Read-across hit Amgen's rival olpasiran (siRNA mechanism, different from pelacarsen's ASO).

- BMO Capital downgraded **AMGN** from Outperform to Market Perform, maintained $450 PT.
- **ROIV** +19.7% bucked the trend: Pulmovant's mosliciguat Phase 2 in PH-ILD showed 56.3% PVR reduction (highest ever in any randomized controlled PH trial) and met all secondary endpoints.
- **XLV** (Healthcare SPDR) -2.2%, worst sector of the day; **IBB** -1.8%.
