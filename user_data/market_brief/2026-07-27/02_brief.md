### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**US-Iran Ceasefire Collapses Oil, Lifts Risk Assets** `Macro / Geopolitics` → ongoing

The dominant session catalyst: both the US and Iran halted retaliatory strikes over the weekend. Brent crude fell ~7% to ~$85.61/bbl; WTI dropped ~7% to ~$83.24. S&P 500 futures +0.96%, Nasdaq 100 +1.63%. The de-escalation is fragile — Iran's FM Baghaei confirmed the Strait of Hormuz remains closed and said an alleged Ukrainian attack on an Iranian vessel "cannot go unanswered."

- US suspended its 13-night airstrike campaign; Iran confirmed reciprocal pause contingent on US halt.
- Saudi air defenses intercepted drones from Iraq targeting oil facilities in eastern Saudi Arabia and Riyadh.
- Houthis struck Saudi Aramco installations along the Red Sea; maritime traffic through Bab el-Mandeb slowed.
- AAA national gas average $4.11/gal; analyst warns $5/gal possible if disruptions persist into fall.
- E&P names selling off pre-market: **EQNR** -5%, **PTEN** -6.6%, **KOS** -6.8%, **SM** -4.5%.
- **MPC**, **PSX**, **VLO** flat-to-down 1D but face Canadian tariff risk — Trump imposed 50% duties on ~$20B of Canadian goods; oil exempt for now. Polymarket odds of new US-Canada trade deal in 2026: 9%.
- CME FedWatch: 68.5% probability Fed holds rates unchanged Wednesday; Polymarket odds of a 2026 rate *hike* fell to 65% from 75% as strike pause eased inflation fears.
