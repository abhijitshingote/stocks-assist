## The Week

Oil dominated everything. Iran's second wave of attacks on U.S. Navy ships, Houthi seizure of Yemen's Mocha port, and drone strikes on Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline pushed Brent crude above **$108** and WTI past **$103**. That energy shock fed directly into inflation: August PPI jumped to **5.4%** year-over-year, core CPI printed **0.3%** month-over-month versus **0.2%** expected, and CME FedWatch repriced September rate-hike odds to **91.6%**. The iShares 20+ Year Treasury ETF (TLT) hit its lowest level since **2004** as the 30-year yield touched **5.30%**. Against that backdrop, AI capex momentum remained fierce — Oracle (ORCL) booked **$30B** in new AI cloud contracts, Qualcomm (QCOM) and Amazon (AMZN) struck a **$60B** custom-chip framework, Nvidia (NVDA) weighed a **$10B** anchor investment in Anthropic's potential **$100B** IPO, and Microsoft (MSFT) disclosed plans to triple data-center capacity to **38 gigawatts**. Apple (AAPL) unveiled its first foldable iPhone at **$1,999** and its first **2nm** chip.

## What Moved

### Middle East Conflict & Energy

Iran launched a previously undisclosed **second wave** of missile attacks on U.S. Navy ships within three days, per the WSJ. Iran's IRGC Navy warned tanker crews near Kuwaiti and Bahraini ports hosting Americans to evacuate. Houthis captured Yemen's Red Sea port of **Mocha** and advanced toward the Hanish Islands, tightening control over the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. Saudi Arabia's **East-West oil pipeline** was temporarily shut down after drone attacks in the Riyadh and Medina regions; injuries were reported. Chevron (CVX) CEO said early oil-market buffers have **"played out"** and price risk from the Iran war is now higher. Brent settled at **$108.54/bbl** (+**0.85%** Thursday), WTI at **$103.28** (+**0.78%**). CVX separately disclosed plans to **double** its rig count in Venezuela under a five-year production growth plan.

### Inflation & Rates

August headline PPI rose **0.4%** month-over-month and **5.4%** year-over-year (vs **5.3%** est, **4.8%** prior). Diesel fuel surged **24.1%** on the month, driving more than a third of the goods increase. Core CPI printed **0.3%** month-over-month vs **0.2%** expected; headline CPI was **0.4%** / **3.4%** annually. Polymarket September hike odds jumped to ~**80%**; CME FedWatch hit **91.6%**, up from **59.4%** a week earlier. The two-year Treasury yield spiked ~**5 bps** to **4.50%** after PPI; the 10-year reached **4.90%**, 30-year **5.30%**. TLT fell **1.3%** to **$81.16**, its lowest since May 2004 — down roughly **55%** from its 2020 peak near **$180**. President Trump's pledge of a **$5,000** citizen "dividend," estimated at **$1.2 trillion** with no funding mechanism, added to long-duration pressure. Michigan consumer sentiment cratered to **47.8** vs **51.0** est. Kroger (KR) CEO said a cyclospora outbreak hit Q2 identical sales by **~35 bps** and noted consumers are cutting back amid **$4** gas and SNAP reductions.

### AI Infrastructure & Semiconductors

**Oracle** reported Q1 with **>$30B** in new AI cloud contracts, lifting remaining performance obligations to **$664B** and delivering **850 MW** of additional data-center capacity. Management raised FY27 adjusted EPS guidance to **$8.10** (from **$8.05**, vs **$8.07** est) and revenue guidance to **>$90B** (vs **$89.8B** est).

**Qualcomm–Amazon** expanded their collaboration with a multi-generation custom silicon and optical connectivity framework. Amazon received a 10-year warrant for up to **25 million** QCOM shares at **$161.26**; vesting is tied to up to **$60B** in commercial payments. Analyst Patrick Moorhead called it a "huge QCOM win" supporting its **>$15B** data-center revenue target.

**Microsoft** disclosed plans to build **38 GW** of data-center capacity — roughly **triple** its current power — after shortages forced it to turn away some AI and cloud business.

**Nvidia** is weighing up to **$10B** as an anchor investor in Anthropic's potential IPO. Anthropic is seeking to raise as much as **$100B** at a ~**$2 trillion** valuation, up from **$965B** post-money in May. Annualized revenue run rate surpassed **$65B** by end of July (from ~**$9B** at end of 2025), with a **$190B–$200B** 2028 revenue forecast underpinning the valuation. The IPO is expected before November midterms.

**TSMC** (TSM) posted record August revenue of **NT$514.81B**, up **53.3%** year-over-year, as AI demand remained "extremely robust." Deputy COO Cliff Hou said the company is simultaneously building ~**20 factories** globally (vs 4–5 historically) and still cannot meet demand. Full-year capex guidance: **$60B–$64B**. TSMC and ASML (ASML) launched a joint initiative to develop **12-inch** EUV photomasks, targeting a pilot line by **2031** and production deployment by **2033**.

**SpaceX** (SPCX) closed a compute hosting deal worth **$1.11B/month** starting December 1, adding **>$13B** in ARR. CFO doubled down on the **$100B** ARR target by year-end. Foxconn reported record August revenue of **T$921.8B** (~**$29.15B**), up **52%** year-over-year, and said Q3 will beat expectations on AI demand.

**Memory** now accounts for **50%–55%** of semiconductor industry revenue, up from **20%–30%** historically, per Susquehanna. Micron (MU) Q2 DRAM revenue rose **65.5%** to **$36B** with **23.3%** market share. AMD (AMD) said it expects enterprise AI to become **50%** of its server CPU addressable market.

Vicor (VICR) backlog hit **$380M**, up **145%** year-over-year; the company is acquiring **~1M sq ft** of new fab space in New Hampshire. Analog Devices (ADI) agreed to acquire edge AI chip maker Alif Semiconductor for **$1.35B** in cash.

### Apple Product Launch

Apple unveiled the **iPhone Duo**, its first foldable iPhone, priced at **$1,999** with two cameras. The **A20 Pro** chip on **2nm** technology was announced — the first consumer 2nm silicon. iPhone 18 Pro starts at **$1,199**, iPhone 18 Pro Max at **$1,299**. A new **iPhone Handoff** feature allows a single phone number across two iPhones. Apple Intelligence is **not available in China**; the updated Siri is blocked in both the **EU and China**.

### Enterprise Software & Cloud

**Adobe** (ADBE) raised FY26 adjusted EPS guidance to **$24.45–$24.50** (from **$24.35–$24.45**, vs **$24.36** est) and revenue guidance to **$26.576B–$26.626B** (from **$26.5B–$26.6B**, vs **$26.506B** est).

**Palantir** (PLTR) and **Nebius** (NBIS) entered a strategic partnership making Nebius Palantir's preferred sovereign AI infrastructure partner, bringing Nebius compute and inference endpoints inside the Palantir enterprise perimeter.

### Deals & M&A

**GE Aerospace** (GE) agreed to acquire casting manufacturer CPP for **$11.75B** (**$7B** cash, remainder debt), valued at ~**18x** projected 2027 EBITDA including synergies. Expected to close H2 2027.

**Copart** (CPRT) announced a definitive agreement to acquire **ACV Auctions** (ACVA) for **$10.50/share** in cash (~**$1.9B** equity value), a **~45% premium** to ACVA's unaffected price. ACVA jumped **43.8%** after hours.

**Chime** (CHYM) agreed to acquire **Stride Bank** for **$590M** in cash, giving the fintech a national bank charter. **Shell** (SHEL) sold its RISEC power plant stake to **Constellation Energy** (CEG) for **$715M** and is acquiring **169 MW** Hunlock Creek gas-fired generation in Pennsylvania. **Western Midstream** is nearing a deal to buy Blackstone (BX)-backed **Kinetik** (KNTK). **TPG** is exploring a **~$5B** sale of healthcare payments firm **Lyric** via JPMorgan (JPM). **Google** (GOOGL) completed its Mechanize AI acqui-hire (deal reportedly worth **>$1.5B**), with the former CEO joining DeepMind.

### Quantum Computing

**IonQ** (IONQ) raised FY26 revenue guidance **~60%** to **$450M–$460M** (from **$280M–$290M**), reflecting the SkyWater Technology acquisition completed July 31. **Quantinuum** (QNT) finalized **$100M** in CHIPS Act funding for trapped-ion quantum R&D and manufacturing. **D-Wave** (QBTS) secured up to **$100M** in CHIPS Act funding with the Commerce Department receiving a minority equity stake.

### Biotech & Pharma

**AstraZeneca** (AZN) / **Amgen** (AMGN): Phase 3 DeLLphi-305 interim analysis showed IMFINZI plus tarlatamab met primary endpoint with statistically significant **overall survival** improvement vs IMFINZI alone in extensive-stage small cell lung cancer.

**AbbVie** (ABBV): Atogepant met the primary endpoint in Phase 3 LUNA for menstrual migraine — **0.80 fewer migraine days** vs placebo (p<**0.0001**) — plus all **eight** ranked secondary endpoints.

**Bristol Myers Squibb** (BMY): Arlocabtagene autoleucel (GPRC5D-directed CAR-T) met primary ORR endpoint and key secondary CRR endpoint in quadruple-class exposed relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma (Phase 2 QUINTESSENTIAL).

**Intellia** (NTLA): FDA accepted its BLA for lonvo-z with Priority Review and a PDUFA date of **March 10, 2027** — potential first **in vivo CRISPR** therapy. **Scholar Rock** (SRRK): FDA approved the first therapy targeting muscle loss in spinal muscular atrophy for patients ≥2 years. **Exelixis** (EXEL): FDA extended the zanzalintinib NDA review by **three months** to **March 3, 2027**. **Novo Nordisk** (NVO): China approved Wegovy as the **first and only** GLP-1 RA treatment for MASH.

### Cybersecurity & Operational Disruption

**Boston Scientific** (BSX) disclosed its August 25 cybersecurity incident will have a **material impact** on Q3 and full-year 2026 results. The company said it is **unlikely to meet** previously issued net sales growth and adjusted EPS guidance for Q3 and FY26, with an update planned at the October 28 earnings call.

### U.S.–China Tech Friction

China's Commerce Ministry threatened **"resolute countermeasures"** if the U.S. cracks down on Chinese AI companies under the pretext of combating model distillation. Anthropic left the Information Technology Industry Council over legislation that would curb foreign access to U.S. chips. Alibaba (BABA) launched an e-commerce AI agent priced **50% cheaper** than Anthropic's offering.
