### 🧭 Narrative Threads

**AI-Infrastructure Debt Stress & Neocloud Credit Contagion** `AI Infrastructure` → ongoing

The multi-day AI-infrastructure selloff paused in equities on Oct 15 but credit markets continued to deteriorate. **CRWV** reversed intraday from −8% to close +4.6%, yet its 5-yr CDS exceeded 1,100 bp (implied ~64% 5-yr default probability), its 2032 unsecured bonds traded below 80¢/$1 with ~15.8% yield, and reported debt/lease obligations total ~$52B. **ORCL** 5-yr CDS hit a record ~288–300 bp. CDS on **AMZN**, **GOOGL**, and **META** reached record-wide levels per S&P Global / LSEG.

- Gartner (Oct 14): >80% of neocloud providers may cease independent operations by 2030; cumulative $8.5T revenue needed through 2030 to cover announced capex + 12% ROIC.
- Morgan Stanley est. ~$570B of global AI-related debt in 2026.
- CoreWeave 2026 capex guidance: $35B–$39B; Q2 net interest expense $640M (>2× YoY); H1 FCF approx. negative $10.5B.
- Mizuho cut CRWV PT to $88 from $115; Deutsche Bank maintained Buy / $150 PT.
- D.A. Davidson's Gil Luria assigned a zero-equity-value scenario for CoreWeave if the AI cycle falters.
- BofA Oct Global Fund Manager Survey: 39% named hyperscaler AI capex as most likely systemic credit event; 45% of managers expect AI bubble by 2027.
- Anthropic reportedly told investors Q3 revenue $16.8B (vs $11.6B Q2); Series H $65B at $965B post-money valuation; compute commitments >$518B over 10 years.
- DELL and HPE each fell ~7% Oct 15; **NVDA** −1.8%, **AMD** >−3% during session.
