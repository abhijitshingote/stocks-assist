## CoreWeave, Inc. Class A Common Stock (CRWV) `[AI Infrastructure]`
- CoreWeave shares initially fell approximately 8% on October 15 before reversing higher; the session followed concerns about AI-infrastructure spending, rising borrowing costs, and a Gartner report warning that more than 80% of neocloud providers could fail to survive independently by 2030 (Investor’s Business Daily, Oct 15 13:41 ET).[1]
- D.A. Davidson analyst Gil Luria said CoreWeave could be the first casualty if the AI cycle faltered and assigned a zero-equity-value scenario; the comments were reported from a podcast and were marked [report] (Yahoo Finance, Oct 15 08:28 ET).[3]
- Mizuho cut its CoreWeave price target to $88 from $115; Deutsche Bank maintained a Buy rating and a $150 price target, while Creditsights’ Paul Meeks called the selloff excessive (Yahoo Finance, Oct 15 10:50 ET).[6]
- CoreWeave’s unsecured bonds due in 2032 traded below 80 cents on the dollar, while reported yields on its 2032 and 2031 bonds approached 14.8% and 14.9%, respectively (Yahoo Finance, Oct 15 08:24 ET).[7]
- CoreWeave reported second-quarter revenue of approximately $2.58 billion, up 112.3% year over year, a $104 billion backlog, a $626 million net loss, $640 million of interest expense versus $267 million a year earlier, and $5.74 billion of negative free cash flow; the figures were previously disclosed and cited as background (Yahoo Finance, Oct 15 15:19 ET).[4]
- CoreWeave CEO Michael Intrator sold 78,552 Class A shares at $87.69 on September 30 for $6,888,224.88 under a 10b5-1 plan; the transaction was outside the window and was cited in same-day commentary (Yahoo Finance, Oct 15 15:19 ET).[4]
