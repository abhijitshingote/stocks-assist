## The Real Brokerage Inc. (REAX) `[Real Estate & Brokerage]`
- Citizens JMP initiated coverage with a **Market Outperform** rating and a **$40 price target** on October 14, citing the completed RE/MAX acquisition and the potential for a larger, less-cyclical brokerage platform; the target implied approximately **153% upside** to REAX’s October 12 close of $14.37 (Investing.com, Oct. 15; source published date, time not stated).
- Citizens projected **$178.9 million** in fiscal-2027 adjusted EBITDA versus **$133.5 million** FactSet consensus, a **34% premium**, and identified acquisition costs plus the pace of revenue and EBITDA benefits as variables to monitor (Investing.com, Oct. 15; source published date, time not stated).
- The RE/MAX acquisition closed on **August 24, 2026**; the company has cited approximately **$30 million** in expected cost synergies within three years of closing (Ad-hoc-news.de, Oct. 15; source published date, time not stated).
- The broader Citizens sector initiation said annual residential sales rates were near **multi-decade lows**, affordability remained a problem, and consumer confidence was low; it argued that the hybrid brokerage/franchise model was underappreciated after an average **more-than-40% year-to-date pullback** across four covered names (Business Insider, Oct. 15; source publish time not stated).
- REAX closed Thursday, October 15, at **$15.99, up 16.90%**, with **848,490 shares** traded, according to the available market-gainer record (MetaCap, Oct. 15; source publish time not stated).
- The company announced that its RISE 2026 conference will run **October 19–21** in Las Vegas and said nearly **2,000 agents** are expected; executives are scheduled to unveil **Leo Pro**, the company’s next-generation artificial-intelligence technology (Business Insider, Oct. 15, updated Oct. 16; source publish time not stated).
- No Friday, October 16, 2026 after-hours or pre-market price or catalyst was identified in the available results through **02:03 ET**.
