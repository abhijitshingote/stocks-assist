## KOSPI / KRX [Cause and trading controls]

- The KOSPI opened at 5,673.01, down 5.59%, and closed at 5,554.22, down 454.59 points, or 7.57%, after reaching an intraday low of 5,450.81 on Oct. 15, 2026. (Yonhap, Oct. 15; source publication time not stated)[3]
- The reported drivers were a U.S. inflation shock, expectations for Federal Reserve rate hikes, skepticism about the sustainability of AI-related investment, and selling in semiconductor stocks; the available reports did not identify Samsung-specific news as the primary trigger. (Chosun, Oct. 15; source publication time not stated)[1]
- U.S. September CPI rose 0.6% month over month and 3.7% year over year, versus a 3.6% economist estimate, according to the cited market report. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15; source publication time not stated)[5]
- Foreign investors sold a net 284.7 billion won shortly after the Oct. 15 open, and the KOSPI recorded its seventh consecutive decline. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15; source publication time not stated)[5]
- The Korea Exchange activated a sell-side sidecar at 9:08 a.m. local time, equivalent to 8:08 p.m. ET on Oct. 14, suspending program trading for five minutes after KOSPI 200 futures fell at least 5% for one minute. (Korea JoongAng Daily, Oct. 15 09:41 a.m. local time; 20:41 ET Oct. 14)[6]
- The KOSPI Level 1 circuit breaker was activated at 12:38 p.m. local time, equivalent to 11:38 p.m. ET on Oct. 14, after the index fell 8.00% to 5,528.05 from the prior close of 6,008.81. (Korea JoongAng Daily, Oct. 15; source publication time not stated)[2]
- The circuit breaker halted KOSPI-listed-share trading for 20 minutes; after reopening, orders were matched through a 10-minute single-price auction. (Yonhap, Oct. 15; source publication time not stated)[7]
- The Level 1 circuit breaker threshold was an index decline of at least 8% from the previous session’s close that persisted for one minute; order entry was halted except for cancellations during the suspension. (Korea JoongAng Daily, Oct. 15; source publication time not stated)[2]
- The KOSDAQ circuit breaker was activated at approximately 12:23 p.m. local time, or 11:23 p.m. ET on Oct. 14, at 757.88, down 8.01%; the KOSPI breaker followed about 15 minutes later. (Korea JoongAng Daily, Oct. 15; source publication time not stated)[2]
