## TSMC [2027 demand outlook]

- TSMC CEO C.C. Wei said consumer and price-sensitive end-market segments were being challenged by rising component prices and macroeconomic uncertainty, adding, “As such, we are being prudent in our business planning going into 2027.” (Reuters, Oct. 15, 2026; publication time not provided)[1]
- Wei simultaneously said demand for leading-edge technologies was “extremely robust,” that TSMC had seen “no sign of slowdown,” and that AI-related demand “probably will be higher” than earlier assumptions. (Investing.com, Oct. 15, 2026; publication time not provided)[5]
- TSMC raised its 2026 U.S.-dollar revenue-growth outlook to approximately 44%; third-quarter revenue was $46.72 billion, up 41.2% year over year, and reported EPS was $4.60 per ADR, up 68.7%. (Zacks/Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15, 2026; publication time not provided)[8]
- TSMC guided fourth-quarter 2026 revenue to $52.3 billion–$54.3 billion and maintained 2026 capital spending of $60 billion–$64 billion. (Zacks/Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15, 2026; publication time not provided)[8]
- TSMC said N2-family annual capacity would more than double in 2027, supported by smartphone and high-performance-computing AI demand; A14 risk production is scheduled for 2027 and volume production for 2028. (Yahoo Finance transcript, Oct. 15, 2026; publication time not provided)[9]

## TSM [Stock and analyst action]

- TSMC’s U.S.-listed ADR fell 2% after the October 15 earnings report, despite the 44% 2026 revenue-growth outlook and record quarterly profit. (Reuters, Oct. 15, 2026; publication time not provided)[1]
- Needham analyst Charles Shi raised TSM’s price target to $560 from $530 and kept a Buy rating after the earnings report. (Business Insider, Oct. 15, 2026; publication time not provided)[10]
- TSMC CFO Wendell Huang said spending during 2026–2028 was expected to be “significantly higher” than during 2023–2025. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15, 2026; publication time not provided)[7]
