## INTU `[Deal terms / stock move]`
- Intuit announced a signed agreement to acquire Sequence, a Series A AI-native revenue-management company, on October 15, 2026 at 09:00 ET. Sequence automates quote configuration, pricing, billing, invoicing, collections, and revenue recognition. (Intuit, Oct. 15 09:00 ET)[1]
- Intuit did not disclose the purchase price, Sequence annual recurring revenue, transaction consideration, or expected closing date. (Intuit, Oct. 15 09:00 ET)[1]
- Intuit said Sequence’s team, including CEO Riya Grover, would join Intuit’s Mid-Market product organization; Intuit planned to develop an integrated offering “in the coming months.” (Intuit, Oct. 15 09:00 ET)[1][10]
- Intuit said Sequence would expand Intuit Enterprise Suite and QuickBooks Online Advanced with usage-, seat-, and milestone-based revenue-management capabilities for mid-market businesses. (Intuit, Oct. 15 09:00 ET)[1][7]
