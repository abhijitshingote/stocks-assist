## **AMD** [AI Compute]
- An Oct. 15 article identified Meta’s Muse AI app and OpenAI’s Dots as consumer AI-agent launches and cited AMD’s CPU, GPU and rack-scale opportunity; BNP Paribas analyst Karl Ackerman had raised AMD’s price target to $960, Citigroup analyst Atif Malik to $800, and Mizuho analysts set a $705 target. The article did not provide contemporaneous AMD stock movement. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15 02:50 ET)[1]
- AMD’s Helios open-rack platform was described as supporting CPU-only and GPU configurations for agentic-AI infrastructure, with availability planned for November; the report also cited an alleged $8.2 billion all-stock acquisition of World Labs. [rumor] (MarketBeat, Oct. 15, publication time unavailable)[2]

## **META** [Internet & Platforms]
- Meta’s Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full models, launched Oct. 12, were described as targeting personal and professional AI agents; Muse Wonder was available to developers through the Model API and Muse Code, while Muse Wonder Full was available to Muse Code subscribers. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15 13:16 ET)[3]
- ARK Invest bought 32,311 Meta shares across two sessions, while analysts debated the earnings impact of Meta’s AI spending; the report did not state a Meta stock move for the Oct. 15 session. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15 13:16 ET)[3]

## **NOW** [Software & SaaS]
- ServiceNow introduced EmployeeWorks on Oct. 15, including ToolSmith for nontechnical employees to build workflow-specific AI agents and AI Passport for permissions, compliance and oversight; the stock was reported up 6.14%. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15 17:12 ET)[4]

## **TSM** [Fab & Foundry]
- Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing reported third-quarter net profit of NT$763.06 billion, up 69% year over year and above the NT$731.77 billion FactSet consensus; revenue was NT$1.494 trillion, up 51% year over year. TSMC’s 2026 revenue-growth outlook was “slightly above 40%” before any further update. (Dow Jones, Oct. 15 06:00 ET)[5]

## **ON** [Power & Wide-Bandgap]
- StoneX initiated onsemi with a Buy rating and a $110 price target; the report cited Wednesday’s closing price of $73.36, implying approximately 50% upside. (TheFly via Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15 10:44 ET)[6]
- Onsemi reported AI-data-center revenue was projected to more than double in 2026 and 2027 and exceed $2.5 billion annually by 2030; its investor-day plan also called for 12%–14% annual revenue growth from 2026 through 2030. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15 10:44 ET)[6]

## **NVDA** [AI Compute]
- KeyBanc reiterated Nvidia at Overweight and identified the company’s CUDA software stack as a barrier to entry in cloud and enterprise AI; no new price target or Oct. 15 stock move was provided in the cited report. (CNBC, Oct. 15, publication time unavailable)[7]

## **QMCO / Quantinuum** [Quantum Computing]
- Morgan Stanley upgraded Quantinuum to Overweight from Equal Weight and set a $62 price target, described as approximately 88% upside; no stock move was provided in the cited report. (CNBC, Oct. 15, publication time unavailable)[7]

## Honeywell, NVIDIA and Dell [AI Infrastructure]
- Honeywell Technologies launched Forge Cognition with NVIDIA and Dell Technologies on Oct. 15; the platform combines AI agents and real-time digital twins for facility operations, including equipment monitoring, energy optimization and response coordination. (Ground News, Oct. 15, publication time unavailable)[8]
- Early pilots were reported to reduce unnecessary facility alerts by up to 90% and energy use by as much as 10%; Honeywell stock was reported down 0.82% to $156.88 in an early trading reference. (Ground News, Oct. 15, publication time unavailable)[8]
