## **TRON** `[Crypto]`
- Nasdaq-listed Tron Inc. filed an at-the-market offering of up to **$200 million** of common stock, with net proceeds intended primarily for additional **TRX** purchases; Clear Street will act as sales agent and may receive a commission of up to **3%**. The filing states that no shares must be sold and that proceeds may also support working capital and general corporate purposes. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15 08:36 ET)[1]
- Tron’s disclosed treasury held **718.9 million TRX**, valued at approximately **$237 million**, after a purchase of **149,369 TRX** at an average price of **$0.3347** on Oct. 15. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15 08:36 ET)[1]
- The proposed offering could create approximately **163.9 million new shares** if the company raises the full $200 million at an assumed **$1.22 per share**. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15 08:36 ET)[1]
- Tron’s existing accumulation program targets approximately **$50,000 of TRX purchases per day for 360 consecutive days**. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15 08:36 ET)[1]

## **Evernorth** `[Crypto]`
- Evernorth plans to deploy its **473 million XRP** treasury, valued at approximately **$648 million**, through initial institutional strategies in the **fourth quarter of 2026**. (The Block, updated Oct. 15; publication time not provided)[3]
- **Ripple, Kraken, Coinbase, and BitGo** were named as counterparties for Evernorth’s initial XRP treasury strategies. (The Block, updated Oct. 15; publication time not provided)[3]
- Evernorth said it plans to expand into **lending, credit markets, and on-chain DeFi strategies on the XRP Ledger** as those capabilities become available. (The Block, updated Oct. 15; publication time not provided)[3]
