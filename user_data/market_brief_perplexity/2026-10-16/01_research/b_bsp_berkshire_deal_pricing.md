## BSP [Confirmed deal terms and valuation]
- Bending Spoons agreed to issue Berkshire Hathaway **10 million new ordinary shares at $33 per share**, totaling **$330 million**, in a **private placement**; the transaction was expected to close in Q4 2026 subject to customary conditions. (CNBC, Oct. 14 10:39 ET, updated Oct. 15 16:01 ET)[1]
- The **$33 per-share price was confirmed in the securities filing and company disclosure**; it was approximately **13% below BSP’s $37.86 close on Oct. 14** and approximately **38% below the $53.46 close reported after the news**. (CNBC, Oct. 14 10:39 ET, updated Oct. 15 16:01 ET; Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15 12:38 ET)[1][6]
- The disclosed share issuance and $33 price imply approximately **250 million post-issuance shares** from Berkshire’s reported **4% ownership**, producing an implied post-money equity valuation of approximately **$8.25 billion**. (CNBC, Oct. 14 10:39 ET, updated Oct. 15 16:01 ET)[1]
- BSP rose **41.20% to $53.46 on Oct. 15**, from **$37.86 on Oct. 14**. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15 11:21 ET, updated Oct. 16)[6]
- BSP was reported at **$54**, approximately **40% above Berkshire’s $33 placement price**. (Investopedia, Oct. 14, updated Oct. 15)[2]

## BRK.A / BRK.B [Ownership and transaction status]
- Berkshire’s additional **$330 million** investment followed its **$600 million** investment in Bending Spoons’ July 2026 IPO, bringing its disclosed cumulative commitment to approximately **$930 million**. (CNBC, Oct. 14 10:39 ET, updated Oct. 15 16:01 ET)[1]
- Berkshire’s confirmed post-transaction ownership was **4%** of Bending Spoons. (CNBC, Oct. 14 10:39 ET, updated Oct. 15 16:01 ET)[1]
- The transaction was a **primary private placement of newly issued shares**, not a secondary purchase from an existing shareholder. (Investopedia, Oct. 14, updated Oct. 15)[2]
- Berkshire’s **Class B shares fell 0.13% to $526.35** in the reported session. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15 12:57 ET)[4]
- Another report recorded BRK.B at **$526.35, up 1.11%**, creating conflicting reported BRK.B moves in the available coverage. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 15 11:21 ET, updated Oct. 16)[6]
