## ServiceNow, Inc. (NOW) `[Software & SaaS]`
- ServiceNow announced AI Passport, a runtime authority layer for controlling AI-agent and Model Context Protocol server permissions, plus expanded Super Intelligence Control Tower governance and cost-to-value monitoring at 09:00 ET on October 15, 2026 (BusinessWire, Oct 15 09:00 ET).[1]
- ServiceNow introduced the next generation of EmployeeWorks, including no-code AI-agent creation and ToolSmith, a developer environment intended to reduce tool-building time from days to minutes (BusinessWire, Oct 15 09:00 ET).[10]
- TD Cowen raised its price target from $140 to $165 and maintained a Buy rating; analyst Derrick Wood cited checks indicating ITSM Prime upgrades, cross-selling, Armis security demand, and U.S. federal activity (TD Cowen, Oct 15 08:16 ET).[4]

- ServiceNow’s president, chief product officer, and COO Amit Zavery denied reported acquisition interest in AI customer-service startup Decagon on October 14, stating that the company had no intention of purchasing it and prioritized organic growth [rumor][report] (247WallSt, Oct 15 12:58 ET).[5]
