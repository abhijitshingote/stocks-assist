## Cypherpunk Technologies Inc. (CYPH) `[Cryptocurrency & Digital Assets]`
- Cantor initiated coverage of Cypherpunk Technologies with an **Overweight** rating and a **$0.90 price target** on October 15, alongside coverage of Forward Industries with an Overweight rating and a $7.90 target; the note described digital-asset treasury companies as an investment theme. (Infomarine, Oct 15, publication time not reported)[3]
- Cypherpunk’s Winklevoss Treasury Investments exercised **4,938,801 pre-funded warrants** for common shares at **$0.001 per share** on October 12; WTI held **29,793,414 common shares** and **97,229,007 pre-funded warrants** afterward, subject to a **19.99% ownership limit**. (StockTitan, Oct 14, updated Oct 15, publication time not reported)[4]
- Cypherpunk was reported to control roughly **18% of Zcash’s hashrate**, while Chief Investment Officer **Will McEvoy** said Zcash mining generated about **$1,000 per megawatt-hour** versus less than **$150** for Bitcoin mining; the report said the company had not placed new Zcash-mining-rig orders. (CoinGeek, Oct 14, updated Oct 15, publication time not reported)[9]
- Binance said it would shut its Binance Pool mining unit on **November 10**, while Zcash mining profitability was reported at nearly **three times** Bitcoin’s level; the report identified Cypherpunk as one of the companies pursuing Zcash mining. (CoinGeek, Oct 14, updated Oct 15, publication time not reported)[9]
- CYPH closed at **$3.02**, up **$0.40**, or **15.27%**, on October 15, with **16.33 million shares** traded and a reported market capitalization of **$325.45 million**. (MetaCap, Oct 15, 17:53 ET)[2]
