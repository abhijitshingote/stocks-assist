## Penske Automotive Group, Inc. (PAG) `[Retail]`
- Penske Automotive Group agreed to be taken private by Motown Acquisition Corp., owned by Penske Corporation and Mitsui & Co.; holders outside the buyer group will receive **$212.00 per share in cash**, valuing the company at approximately **$16.3 billion including debt**. The price was an **8.84% premium** to the October 14 close. (Penske Automotive Group, Oct. 15, 2026; CNBC, Oct. 15 17:08 ET)[1][6]
- Penske Corporation and Mitsui will own approximately **64% and 36%**, respectively, after closing; the transaction is expected to close in **Q1 2027**, subject to regulatory approvals and shareholder votes, and is not subject to a financing condition. (Penske Automotive Group, Oct. 15, 2026)[1]
- The special committee of independent directors unanimously recommended the transaction, and the board approved the **$212** offer, increased from the previously announced **$210** proposal. (Morningstar, Oct. 15, 2026)[8]
- PAG shares rose **9.16%** on October 15; approximately **3.24 million shares** traded, more than **7×** average daily volume, and the stock finished at **$212.00**. (Seeking Alpha, Oct. 15 17:03 ET)[3]
- Penske Automotive will issue third-quarter 2026 results before the market opens on **October 28, 2026**, but will not host an earnings conference call; its shares will cease trading on the NYSE after the transaction closes. (Penske Automotive Group, Oct. 15, 2026)[1]
