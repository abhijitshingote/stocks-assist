## CrowdStrike Holdings, Inc. (CRWD) `[Software & SaaS]`
- CrowdStrike shares fell 3.30% on Thursday, October 15, 2026, as cybersecurity stocks retreated amid renewed concerns that AI developers could become competitors to established security vendors; Palo Alto Networks, Zscaler, and Fortinet also declined, while Fortinet fell 3.52% to $184.05. The concern followed reports that OpenAI was in early talks to acquire security startup Trail of Bits, with no announced transaction.[report] (Yahoo Finance, Oct 15; Arabian Post, Oct 15)
- Semafor reported that Perplexity was “giving away” an AI sandbox; CrowdStrike was down 3.3% at 13:18 ET on October 15, according to Cboe data.[report] (Business Insider, Oct 15 13:20 ET)
- Citi analyst Fatima Boolani maintained a Buy rating and raised CrowdStrike’s price target to $295 from $260, citing an improved chief investment officer survey and AI deployment driving additional security investment.[4] (Markets Insider, Oct 15)
- Citi also raised targets on Palo Alto Networks and Zscaler while maintaining Buy ratings; Wells Fargo cited 10 channel-partner discussions and a reseller survey indicating improved cybersecurity spending heading into 2027.[5] (Yahoo Finance, Oct 15)
- CrowdStrike’s October 14 close was $252.26, and the stock was quoted at $241.70, down approximately 3.1%, at 11:13 ET on October 15.[2] (Yahoo Finance, Oct 15)
- CrowdStrike’s August 26 results included quarterly revenue of $1.47 billion, free cash flow of $377.4 million, annual recurring revenue of $5.84 billion, and a $33.2 million GAAP operating loss; the figures were cited in an October 15 valuation discussion.[2] (Yahoo Finance, Oct 15)
- CrowdStrike President and CEO George Kurtz sold 19,900 shares for approximately $5.15 million in transactions executed October 13–14, at prices ranging from $249.11 to $267.51.[10] (Investing.com, Oct 16)
- The available results report no additional CrowdStrike-specific after-hours or pre-market catalyst through 02:03 ET on October 16, 2026.
