## ERIC [Q3 results and stock move]

(Ericsson release, Oct 15, 2026; publication time not stated)

(Light Reading, Oct 15, 2026; publication time not stated)

## ERIC [RAN outlook and component costs]
- CEO Per Narvinger said on Oct. 15 that “the RAN market is broadly flat” and that component costs were increasing in the wake of the AI boom. (Ericsson earnings-call transcript, Oct 15, 2026)
- Narvinger said tactical measures, including product substitution and upselling, had mitigated higher component costs in the short term but would not be sufficient over the long term. (Ericsson earnings-call transcript, Oct 15, 2026)
- Ericsson said structural measures would include portfolio optimization, price increases, supply-chain actions, and efficiency improvements. (Ericsson release, Oct 15, 2026)
- Narvinger said higher component costs would create a headwind to gross margin in the following year, while Reuters reported that Ericsson had previously linked rising memory-chip costs to AI demand. (Ericsson release, Oct 15, 2026; Reuters, Oct 15, 2026)
- Ericsson said higher input costs would continue building over coming quarters and that price increases had already begun, with longer-term cost recovery dependent on contract renewals. (Light Reading, Oct 15, 2026; Ericsson earnings-call transcript, Oct 15, 2026)
- The call transcript cited a 10% potential spectrum-efficiency gain from AI RAN and used SpaceX’s reported $8 billion spectrum purchase as an example of a potential $800 million value at that efficiency level. (Yahoo Finance earnings-call highlights, Oct 15, 2026)
