## Intuit Inc. (INTU) `[Software & SaaS]`
- Intuit announced an agreement to acquire Sequence, a Series A AI-native revenue-management platform automating quoting, pricing, billing, collections, and revenue recognition; financial terms were not disclosed. The transaction is intended to expand Intuit Enterprise Suite and QuickBooks Online Advanced. (Intuit IR, Oct 15 09:00 ET)[1]
- Intuit’s fiscal 2027 revenue guidance was previously reaffirmed at $23.28 billion–$23.51 billion, representing 9%–10% growth; Global Business Solutions revenue guidance was $13.07 billion–$13.16 billion under the revised reporting structure. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 15 11:02 ET)[6]
- The company’s latest reported quarter showed EPS of $4.03 versus a $3.58 consensus estimate and revenue of $4.35 billion versus a $4.27 billion consensus estimate; revenue increased 13.7% year over year. (MarketBeat, Oct 15; publication time not stated)[5]
- Intuit’s quarterly dividend was scheduled for payment on October 16 at $1.38 per share to shareholders of record on October 8. (MarketBeat, Oct 15; publication time not stated)[5]
- CNBC reported Intuit shares up approximately 6% after the Sequence acquisition announcement. (CNBC, Oct 15; publication time not stated)[9]
