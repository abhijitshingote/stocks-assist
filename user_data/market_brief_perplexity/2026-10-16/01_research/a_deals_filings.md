## **CCC** `[Software & SaaS]`
- GTCR and Elliott agreed to acquire CCC Intelligent Solutions in an all-cash transaction with enterprise value of approximately **$6.1 billion**; CCC shareholders will receive **$7.75 per share**. The board approved the transaction, which is expected to close in **Q1 2027** subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals. (GlobeNewswire, Oct 15 08:00 ET)[1]

## **VRNS** `[Software & SaaS]`
- Proofpoint, owned by Thoma Bravo, agreed to acquire Varonis in an all-cash transaction valued at approximately **$6.3 billion**; Varonis shareholders will receive **$49.75 per share**. The transaction was unanimously approved by Varonis’s board and is expected to close in **H1 2027**, subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals. (Proofpoint, Oct 15, time not reported)[4]
- Thoma Bravo committed equity financing and secured debt-financing commitments for the acquisition; Varonis entered the merger agreement with Proofpoint and Vail Merger Sub on **October 15, 2026**. (SEC Form 8-K filing, Oct 15, time not reported)[2]

## **PAG** `[Retail / Automotive]`
- Penske Automotive agreed to be taken private by Motown Acquisition, owned by Penske Corporation and Mitsui, for **$212.00 per share in cash**, implying enterprise value of approximately **$16.3 billion**. The transaction is expected to close in **Q1 2027** after regulatory approvals and shareholder votes. (PRNewswire, Oct 15 10:50 ET)[8]
- The buyer expects up to **$2.2 billion of equity financing** from Penske Corporation and Mitsui and up to **$1.8 billion of debt financing** from JPMorgan, Bank of America and Wells Fargo. (SEC Form 8-K filing, Oct 15, time not reported)[9]

## **FUL** `[Specialty Materials & IP Licensing]`
- H.B. Fuller priced **$850 million** of senior unsecured notes due **2034** at **100% of principal**, carrying a **7.625% coupon**. Proceeds are intended to help fund the Advanced Medical Solutions acquisition, repay existing borrowings and address the company’s **4.000% notes due February 15, 2027**; closing was expected around **October 21, 2026**. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 15 13:27 ET)[5]

## **RIG** `[Energy]`
- Transocean’s amended and restated credit agreement increases revolving commitments from **$510 million to $750 million** and extends maturity from **June 2028 to October 2031**. Effectiveness is subject to conditions including completion of Transocean’s previously announced Valaris acquisition. (GlobeNewswire via AP, Oct 15 17:33 ET)[3]
- Transocean retired its **8.00% senior secured notes due 2028**, with **$165 million** of principal, plus call premium and accrued interest, settled on **October 1, 2026**. (GlobeNewswire via AP, Oct 15 17:33 ET)[3]

## **TCXB** `[Calendar / IPO]`
- TCGX Acquisition Corp. II priced an IPO of **10,000,000 Class A ordinary shares at $10.00 per share**, for gross proceeds of **$100 million**. The shares were scheduled to begin trading on Nasdaq under **TCXB on October 16, 2026**; the offering was expected to close around **October 19, 2026**. (Business Wire, Oct 15, time not reported)[6]

## **FLSC** `[Calendar / IPO]`
- Frazier Life Sciences Acquisition Corp. II priced an IPO of **7,500,000 Class A ordinary shares at $10.00 per share**, for gross proceeds of **$75 million**; the shares began trading under **FLSC on October 14, 2026**, and the offering was expected to close on **October 15, 2026**. (Business Wire, updated Oct 16, time not reported)[7]
