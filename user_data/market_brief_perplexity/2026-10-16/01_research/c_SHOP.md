## Shopify Inc. (SHOP) `[Software & SaaS]`
- Baird raised its Shopify price target from $160 to $190 and maintained an “Outperform” rating; the note cited expected gross-merchandise-volume growth ahead of fiscal Q3 2026 results (Yahoo Finance, Oct 15, 2026; publish time not stated).[1]
- Jefferies maintained a “Buy” rating and a $175 price target, citing third-party data indicating potential gross-merchandise-volume growth above expectations (Yahoo Finance, Oct 15, 2026; publish time not stated).[1]
- Cantor Fitzgerald raised its Shopify price target from $145 to $158 and maintained a “Neutral” rating; the firm forecast potential fiscal Q3 upside in GMV, revenue and operating profit, with sales growth in the low 30% range (The Stock Observer, Oct 15, 2026; publish time not stated).[1][3]
- Shopify’s Shop Pay was integrated with Meta’s Muse AI assistant, enabling purchases through a conversational AI interface (Mitrade, Oct 15, 2026; publish time not stated).[2]
- The software and IT-services sector rose 1.04% on Oct 15, while Shopify’s verified regular-session gain was 2.36%; Meta fell 0.11%, Microsoft rose 1.08%, and Alphabet rose 0.45% (Mitrade, Oct 15, 2026; publish time not stated).[5]
- Shopify was reported at $172.20, down 0.2% in an intraday report discussing takeover speculation involving eBay and Etsy; the report said eBay rose 5% and Etsy rose 7%, while the Shopify move was not attributed to that speculation (Yahoo Finance, Oct 15, 2026; publish time not stated).[4]
