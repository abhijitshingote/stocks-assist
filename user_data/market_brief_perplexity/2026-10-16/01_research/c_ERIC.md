## Telefonaktiebolaget LM Ericsson (publ) (ERIC) `[Communications & Networking]`
- Ericsson reported Q3 2026 revenue of SEK 59.5 billion versus SEK 56.2 billion a year earlier, organic sales growth of 7%, and adjusted EBITA of SEK 10.1 billion versus the Bloomberg consensus of SEK 7.93 billion (Ericsson, Oct 15 2026; Bloomberg, Oct 15 2026). [10][6]
- Q3 diluted EPS was SEK 1.77 versus SEK 3.33 a year earlier; the U.S. consensus was $0.14 per share, while reported EPS was $0.18 (Ericsson, Oct 15 2026; MarketBeat, Oct 15 2026). [3][5]
- Q3 revenue was $5.94 billion versus the $5.59 billion consensus estimate (MarketBeat, Oct 15 2026). [5]
- Ericsson said Networks revenue increased and that organic sales grew in three of four market areas; customer wins included network modernization, differentiated connectivity, and mission-critical networks (Ericsson, Oct 15 2026). [10]
- CEO Per Narvinger said the RAN market was flat, geopolitical uncertainty remained elevated, and component costs would pressure gross margin in 2027 (Ericsson, Oct 15 2026). [10]
- Ericsson announced a new APAC market area combining North East Asia with South East Asia, Oceania and India, effective Jan. 1, 2027, as part of a simplified group structure and executive-team changes (Ericsson/PRNewswire, Oct 15 2026). [2]
- Ericsson said it returned SEK 4.8 billion through share repurchases during Q3 (GuruFocus, Oct 15 2026). [7]
- ERIC opened at $10.65 versus the prior close of $9.58, and was reported at $10.395 with 11,903,527 shares traded; the report attributed the gap-up to the earnings release (MarketBeat, Oct 15 2026). [5]
- ERIC was reported about 12% higher in Nasdaq premarket trading and about 11% higher in Stockholm after the Q3 release (Investing.com, Oct 15 2026). [3]
- No separate after-hours or premarket catalyst published by 02:03 ET on Oct. 16 was identified beyond the Q3 earnings release, operating-structure announcement, and related analyst-consensus comparisons.
