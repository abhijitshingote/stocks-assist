## ICICI Bank Limited (IBN) `[Banking]`
- ICICI Bank rose 4.17% to ₹1,395.00 on Thursday, October 15, while the BSE Sensex gained 2.11% to 74,315.61; IndusInd Bank rose 2.24%, HDFC Bank’s listed shares gained 2.22%, and SBI rose 0.53%.[6]
- The broader Indian market rally lifted private-bank and financial-services shares: the Nifty IT index gained about 1.82%, private banking stocks advanced, and ICICI Bank was the largest positive contributor to the Nifty 50, adding 79.71 points.[2]
- The Sensex recorded its largest daily gain since June 12, 2026, as buying was broad-based across IT, banking, and heavyweight stocks; HCLTech’s September-quarter results and easing crude prices were cited as market factors.[4]
- ICICI Bank and HDFC Bank rose 4.2% and 2.5%, respectively, ahead of quarterly earnings scheduled for the weekend.[3]
- Reliance Industries rose 3.44% and Bharti Airtel gained 3.31% after Reliance Jio Platforms unveiled details of a planned initial public offering; the two stocks were among the other major Nifty gainers.[5]
- ICICI Bank’s U.S.-listed shares closed at $29.44, up 4.14% from $28.27, on October 15, 2026.[10]
- ICICI Bank allotted 113,464 equity shares with a face value of ₹2 each under the ICICI Bank Employees Stock Option Scheme 2000 on October 15, 2026. (Economic Times, Oct 15, time not stated)
