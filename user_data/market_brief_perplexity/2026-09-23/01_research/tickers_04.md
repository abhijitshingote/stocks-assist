## IonQ, Inc. (IONQ) `[Quantum Computing]`
- IonQ announced development and successful testing of what it described as the industry’s first end-to-end, real-time quantum-error-correction decoder operating on a single standard, off-the-shelf CPU; the announcement was made Tuesday, September 22, 2026, after the close (IonQ, as reported by CNBC).
- IonQ said the decoder can identify, correct, and decode quantum-computing errors in real time without slowing execution; the validation covered hundreds of logical qubits and millions of logical operations (IonQ statement, as reported by CNBC).
- IonQ quantum research lead Nicolas Delfosse said validation across hundreds of logical qubits and millions of logical operations provided a path toward commercial-scale fault-tolerant quantum computing (Nicolas Delfosse, IonQ statement, as reported by CNBC).
- IonQ announced that its Superion 256 quantum computer would be installed at Nvidia’s Accelerated Quantum Research Center in 2027 (IonQ, as reported by Investor’s Business Daily).
- IonQ’s announcement led to gains across quantum-computing stocks; Rigetti Computing and D-Wave Quantum each rose more than 5.5% in premarket trading on September 23, 2026 (CNBC).
- IonQ announced a multi-year strategic partnership with South Korea’s SDT to deliver a Superion 256 quantum computer (IonQ, as reported by Investing.com).
