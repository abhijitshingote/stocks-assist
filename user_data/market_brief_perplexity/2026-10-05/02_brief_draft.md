**White House AI Accord & Super Intelligence Force** `AI Policy` → new

Six companies — **NVDA**, **META**, **GOOGL** (Google), Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI — signed voluntary "White House Accord on Super Intelligence" (Sep 29 signing, announced Oct 4). **MSFT attended but declined to sign.**

- Commitments: internal controls, internal evaluation teams, independent external audits, board-level review. No federal enforcement mechanism, penalties, or compliance deadlines.
- Trump created **Super Intelligence Force** led by DNI Jay Clayton; reports to Trump and Chief of Staff Wiles.
- Treasury Sec Bessent: AI companies should self-police; US-China bilateral AI-notification system planned.
- FTC opened industry-wide probe into Anthropic, OpenAI, others over AI-agent consumer harms; compelled testimony planned.
