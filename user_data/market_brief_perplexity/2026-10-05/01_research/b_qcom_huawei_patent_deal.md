## QCOM [Patent agreement]

- Huawei and Qualcomm announced a **multi-year, broad patent-license agreement** covering cross-licenses in **5G, compute, AI and networking**, plus Qualcomm’s purchase of certain Huawei U.S. patents in compute, AI and networking; closing is subject to necessary regulatory approvals. (Huawei/Qualcomm joint release, Oct. 5, 2026; AFP, Oct. 5, 2026 11:29 ET)
- **Royalty rate, upfront payment, total transaction value and exact duration were not disclosed** as of the 12:20 ET cutoff. (Huawei/Qualcomm joint release, Oct. 5, 2026; Reuters, Oct. 5, 2026 06:01 ET)
- Huawei said the agreement is expected to raise the total value of its patent-licensing agreements to **more than $6.9 billion** after completion; the **$6.9 billion is not the value of the Qualcomm transaction**. (Reuters, Oct. 5, 2026 06:01 ET; Patent Lawyer Magazine, Oct. 5, 2026 11:21 ET)
- The agreement is Huawei’s **first Qualcomm patent-license deal covering 5G technologies**. (Reuters, Oct. 5, 2026 06:01 ET)
- Qualcomm agreed to license patents underpinning Huawei’s **LogicFolding** chip-design technology, according to Bloomberg reporting cited by Yahoo Finance. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 5, 2026 09:34 ET)
- QCOM rose approximately **2% overnight** before Monday’s open, according to a market report; no exact 12:20 ET price or percentage was reported in the gathered sources. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 5, 2026 09:34 ET)
- No analyst firm, rating change or price-target change for QCOM was reported in the gathered results during the specified window.

## Export controls and regulatory review [Regulatory]

- The transaction remains subject to **necessary regulatory approvals** and had not closed by the 12:20 ET cutoff. (Huawei/Qualcomm joint release, Oct. 5, 2026; AFP, Oct. 5, 2026 11:29 ET)
- Bloomberg reporting cited in the gathered coverage said the transaction is subject to **U.S. Federal Trade Commission review under Hart-Scott-Rodino rules**. (AskTraders, Oct. 5, 2026 11:26 ET; Yahoo Finance, Oct. 5, 2026 09:34 ET)
- The gathered reports did **not identify Commerce Department or OFAC approval**, an export license, or an OFAC authorization specifically required or obtained for the patent agreement by 12:20 ET. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 5, 2026 09:34 ET; AFP, Oct. 5, 2026 11:29 ET)
- The agreement’s stated licensing fields include **5G, compute, AI and networking**, while U.S. export restrictions have limited Huawei’s access to advanced chips and software since **2019**. (Reuters, Oct. 5, 2026 06:01 ET)

## ARM [Delaware trial]

- Qualcomm and Arm’s **five-day Delaware jury trial began October 5, 2026**, concerning Qualcomm’s allegations that Arm withheld chip-testing tools and publicly disclosed its 2024 license-termination threat. (Reuters, Oct. 5, 2026 10:32 ET)
- Qualcomm is seeking to stop paying Arm royalties potentially worth **billions of dollars for up to five years**; Qualcomm’s Arm agreement runs through **2033**. (Reuters, Oct. 5, 2026 10:32 ET)
- The Huawei–Qualcomm patent announcement did **not change the reported scope, claims, damages theories or settlement status** of the Qualcomm–Arm Delaware trial by 12:20 ET. (Reuters, Oct. 5, 2026 10:32 ET)
