## Accenture plc (ACN) `IT Services & Consulting`

- Accenture shares fell **6.31% on Friday, October 2**, after rising approximately **16% on October 1** following fiscal fourth-quarter results; the pullback was attributed to profit-taking, measured organic growth, acquisition dependence, and valuation concerns. (Yahoo Finance/Benzinga, Oct 2; TradingKey, Oct 2 12:24 ET)
- Accenture reported fiscal Q4 revenue of **$18.68 billion** versus **$18.04 billion consensus** and adjusted EPS of **$3.29** versus **$3.18 consensus**; full-year bookings reached **$84.5 billion** and Q4 bookings reached **$22.2 billion**. (MarketBeat, Oct 2; Yahoo Finance, Oct 1)
- Fiscal 2027 guidance called for revenue of **$76.4 billion–$78.7 billion** and EPS of **$14.39–$14.81**; first-quarter revenue guidance was **$19.0 billion–$19.6 billion**. (MarketBeat, Oct 2)
- Reported growth included approximately **3% organic revenue growth**, while acquisitions contributed materially to overall growth; pricing pressure and cautious discretionary consulting spending were cited as constraints. (MarketBeat, Oct 2; TradingKey, Oct 2 12:24 ET)
- **Truist Financial** raised its price target from **$150 to $220** and maintained **Hold**; **Morgan Stanley** raised its target from **$175 to $195** and maintained **Equal Weight**. (MarketBeat, Oct 2)
- **Wells Fargo** reaffirmed a **Reduce** rating, while **TD Cowen** maintained **Hold** with a **$173** price target. (MarketBeat, Oct 2)
- **Stifel** reiterated **Buy** and raised its price target from **$225 to $242** after the earnings report; analyst David Grossman cited smaller discretionary deals, federal-government activity, and AI-related partners. (CNBC, Oct 1)
- Accenture’s fiscal 2027 outlook and bookings also moved peers on October 1: **Cognizant rose about 8%** and **IBM rose about 3%** as the results addressed concerns about AI reducing demand for IT-services work. (Reuters, Oct 1)
- **Infosys rose 8% to $11.61** and IBM rose **5% to $231.48** in another October 1 report describing the broader consulting-sector reaction to Accenture’s bookings and outlook. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 1)
- Accenture said pricing was lower in several areas during fiscal Q4, reflecting pressure from clients seeking to capture part of the savings from AI-driven efficiencies; the company also said AI opportunities exceeded the efficiency impact on its business. (Reuters, Oct 1)
- Accenture and **Anthropic** committed at least **$1 billion each over five years** to expand AI-safety and testing capabilities under a September partnership; the partnership was cited as background to Accenture’s AI-related strategy, not as a new October 2–5 announcement. (Reuters, Oct 1)
- Accenture announced a **4.9% dividend increase to $1.71 per share**, payable November 13 to shareholders of record October 13. (MarketBeat, Oct 2)
- No separate company-specific after-hours or pre-market catalyst for Monday, October 5 was identified in the available results; one October 5 report continued to describe the move as a post-earnings pullback after the October 1 rally. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 5)
