## Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) `AI Compute`
- AMD closed at **$633.91**, up **$18.18, or 2.95%**, on Friday, October 2, 2026; the shares reached a reported intraday high of **$645.46**. (MarketBeat, Oct 2; updated Oct 5 05:45 ET)
- AI-chip stocks including **Nvidia, Intel, Broadcom, and AMD** rose in pre-market trading on October 2 after **Micron Technology** reported fiscal fourth-quarter results and issued a higher outlook; lower Treasury yields also supported the group. (Business Insider, Oct 2; updated Oct 4 22:33 ET)
- AMD’s planned acquisition of **World Labs**, the AI model and research company led by **Fei-Fei Li**, is valued at approximately **$8.2 billion** in an all-stock transaction; AMD expects the deal to close by the end of 2026, subject to regulatory approval and customary conditions. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 4 15:24 ET; updated Oct 5 02:48 ET)
- Under the World Labs agreement, Fei-Fei Li is expected to become AMD’s **executive vice president and chief scientist**; the purchase price will be paid in AMD shares, with the number of shares based on a **10-trading-day volume-weighted average price** before closing. (Ad-hoc-News, Oct 2; updated Oct 3 20:44 ET)
- **Rosenblatt** reiterated its **Buy** rating and **$700** price target for AMD on September 29, citing the World Labs acquisition’s potential to expand AMD’s AI software, modeling, simulation, robotics, and system-level capabilities. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 4 15:24 ET; updated Oct 5 02:48 ET)
- **Bank of America** reiterated **Buy** on September 25 and raised its AMD price target from **$620 to $720**, citing potential expansion of the server market from approximately **$61 billion to $211 billion by 2030**, with about **$180 billion** tied to AI workloads. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 4 08:24 ET; updated Oct 4 21:06 ET)
- AMD’s World Labs transaction is an all-stock deal that could dilute existing shareholders; the acquisition does not immediately add chip revenue or earnings and introduces integration and execution risk. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 4 15:24 ET; updated Oct 5 02:48 ET)
- **Hewlett Packard Enterprise** secured a **$1.2 billion** order from **Vultr** for AMD Helios AI racks, according to market coverage published during the window. (MarketBeat, Oct 2; updated Oct 5 05:13 ET)
- AMD and **Oracle** previously announced that Oracle Cloud Infrastructure would deploy **50,000 AMD Instinct MI450 GPUs** beginning in the third quarter of 2026; AMD also has an agreement with **OpenAI** for a multi-generation, **6-gigawatt** MI450 deployment and issued OpenAI a warrant for up to **160 million AMD shares** tied to deployment milestones. (Startup Fortune, Oct 4; updated Oct 5 05:08 ET)
- AMD traded at **€566.75**, up **0.70%** from the October 2 close, in Lang & Schwarz pre-market trading on Monday, October 5; the cited report did not identify a new Monday-specific catalyst. (Ad-hoc-News, Oct 5 06:38 ET)
