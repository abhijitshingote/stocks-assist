## **QCOM** — Wireless & Mobile
- Qualcomm and Arm began a five-day jury trial in Delaware on October 5 over Qualcomm’s allegations that Arm withheld chip-testing tools required under contract and leaked information about Arm’s 2024 license-termination threat; Qualcomm is seeking to stop royalty payments potentially worth billions of dollars for up to five years, while Qualcomm’s agreement with Arm runs through 2033. (Reuters, Oct. 5, 06:20 ET)[1]

## **ARM** — Specialty Materials & IP Licensing
- Arm and Qualcomm began a Delaware trial on October 5 concerning Qualcomm’s contract and licensing claims, including whether Arm negotiated in good faith over the next version of its chip technology; Judge Maryellen Noreika is also considering whether Qualcomm can pursue damages under the disputed contract provision. (Reuters, Oct. 5, 06:20 ET)[1]

## **TSM** — Fab & Foundry
- Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. is in early-stage discussions with Elon Musk over a possible role in the proposed Texas “Terafab”; options reportedly include TSMC owning and operating the plant while SpaceX purchases its chips, or SpaceX holding a majority stake, with no final ownership or operating agreement announced as of October 5. (ANI, Oct. 5, 07:49 ET)[8]
- Taiwan’s foreign ministry said on October 5 that its minister was visiting Arizona for the opening of a new de facto consulate; the ministry cited TSMC’s planned **$265 billion** Arizona investment as part of Taiwan-U.S. economic and trade cooperation. (Reuters, Oct. 5, 04:20 ET)[2]

## **STM** — Analog & Mixed-Signal
- STMicroelectronics announced that it will release third-quarter 2026 results before European markets open on **October 29, 2026**, followed by an analyst and media conference call at **4:30 a.m. ET**. (STMicroelectronics, Oct. 5, 00:20 ET)[9]

## **ASML** — Chip Equipment
- A report published October 4 stated that ASML reported second-quarter 2026 sales of **€9.3 billion**, a **54% gross margin**, and full-year 2026 revenue guidance of **€43 billion–€45 billion**, compared with **€34 billion–€39 billion** at the start of the year; the information concerns previously announced results and guidance. (MoneyWeek, Oct. 4, 10:41 ET)[10]

## **MU** — Memory & Interconnect
- Micron’s reported long-term customer commitments increased to **$32 billion** from **$22 billion** disclosed in June; a union at Micron’s Taoyuan facility began a strike-authorization vote running through Tuesday, with the company stating that negotiations were ongoing. [report] (Ad-hoc-news, Oct. 5, 06:20 ET)[11]
- D.A. Davidson reportedly raised its Micron price target from **$2,000 to $2,100** while maintaining a **Buy** rating. (Ad-hoc-news, Oct. 5, 06:20 ET)[11]

## **NVDA** — AI Compute
- Amazon was reported to be seeking outside investors for a proposed **$8 billion** vehicle that would own Nvidia Grace Blackwell systems installed across more than a dozen U.S. data centers and lease them to Amazon; negotiations were ongoing and no transaction had been completed as of October 5. [report] (UOB Kay Hian, Oct. 5, 05:00 ET)[12]

## Samsung Electronics — Memory & Interconnect
- Samsung Electronics was scheduled to release preliminary third-quarter results on **October 8, 2026**; the report cited Micron’s forecast of tighter memory supply in **2027 and 2028** driven by demand for high-bandwidth memory and other AI-infrastructure chips. (Aju Press, Oct. 5, 05:18 ET)[13]
- Samsung presented plans for a Vietnam memory-test facility in Thai Nguyen covering approximately **265,895 square meters**; equipment installation for the first production line is scheduled for **July 2027**, with operations targeted for the fourth quarter of 2027, while a second line is scheduled to begin equipment installation in **May 2028** and operate in October 2028. (SammyFans, Oct. 5, 05:28 ET)[14]

## TSMC / SpaceX Terafab — Fab & Foundry
- Elon Musk confirmed on October 3 that SpaceX’s planned Texas Terafab was holding exploratory talks with TSMC; the first phase is described as requiring **$16.8 billion** of capital expenditure, with initial commercial wafer output targeted for **2028**.
