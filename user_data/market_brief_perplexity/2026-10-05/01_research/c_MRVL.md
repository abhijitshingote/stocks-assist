## Marvell Technology, Inc. (MRVL) `[AI Compute]`
- MRVL closed at **$272.29**, up **1.57%** on Friday, October 2, 2026. The broader semiconductor move followed September nonfarm payrolls of **29,000** versus **84,000** expected and an unemployment rate of **4.2%**, which lowered Treasury yields and supported high-multiple growth stocks; KLA rose **7.98%**, Semtech **7.96%**, AMD **4.18%**, and Marvell **5.92%** in the session, according to the cited market report. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, 21:22 ET)
- Marvell’s **October 6, 2026 Investor Day** was identified as the near-term catalyst, with management expected to present its long-term strategy, growth opportunities, and AI data-center infrastructure roadmap. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, 11:35 ET; ad-hoc-news, Oct. 4, 10:34 ET)
- Piper Sandler analyst **David O’Connor** reiterated **Buy** and a **$270** price target before Investor Day; he forecast fiscal-2031 revenue of **$45 billion** and EPS of approximately **$19**, and said investors may be underestimating Marvell’s “XPU Attach” opportunity. (Business Insider, Oct. 4, 23:52 ET)
- Morgan Stanley analyst **Joseph Moore** raised his price target to **$268 from $246** while maintaining **Hold**, citing expected scale-up connectivity opportunities and the Google agreement; valuation was listed as a concern. (Business Insider, Oct. 4, 23:52 ET)
- Marvell’s fiscal second-quarter 2027 revenue was **$2.7393 billion**, up **36.5% year over year**, and adjusted EPS was **$0.94**; data-center revenue represented **79%** of quarterly revenue. (ad-hoc-news, Oct. 4, 10:34 ET; Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, 13:31 ET)
- Management’s fiscal-third-quarter 2027 revenue guidance was **$3.15 billion, plus or minus 5%**; fiscal-2027 revenue was reported as expected to reach **$12 billion**, compared with **$8.19 billion** in fiscal 2026. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, 13:31 ET; Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, 11:35 ET)
- A July agreement with **Alphabet/Google** covers custom accelerators, storage controllers, network-interface controllers, and memory controllers; Piper Sandler described the opportunity as a **$120 billion** Google deal involving TPU-attach sockets. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, 11:35 ET; Business Insider, Oct. 4, 23:52 ET)
- Marvell presented what it called the industry’s first **2-nanometer optical-interconnect demonstrations** at **ECOC 2026** and expanded its collaboration with **AT&S** for advanced IC-substrate manufacturing capacity for future AI and cloud products. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, 22:06 ET)
- MRVL options volume reached **152,000 contracts**, with a **0.53 put/call ratio** versus a typical **0.62**; implied volatility was approximately **62.08**, and the expected daily move was **$10.66**. (Business Insider, Oct. 4, 11:08 ET)
- Marvell’s principal identified risks included approximately **90× trailing earnings** valuation, concentration in hyperscaler and data-center spending, potential AI-capital-spending reductions, and dependence on **Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing** and other Asian suppliers. (MarketBeat, Oct. 2, 02:49 ET; Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, 13:31 ET)
