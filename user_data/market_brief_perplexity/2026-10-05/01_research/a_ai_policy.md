## **GOOGL / Chegg / Penske Media** — Internet & Platforms
- U.S. District Judge Amit Mehta dismissed antitrust lawsuits filed by Chegg and Penske Media against Google over AI Overviews, ruling that the plaintiffs had alleged an “expectation” of receiving search traffic rather than an agreement with Google; the cases were **Chegg Inc. v. Google LLC**, No. 25-cv-543, and **Penske Media Corp. v. Google**, No. 25-cv-3192. (Economic Times, Oct. 2, 2026, 03:49 ET)[1]
- Judge Mehta wrote that the complaints did not establish an antitrust violation and said remedies for economic harm caused by innovation may require legislative action rather than antitrust law. (Economic Times, Oct. 2, 2026, 03:49 ET)[1]

## White House / U.S. Congress — AI Policy & Safety
- President **Donald Trump** and **31 AI companies** signed a voluntary “White House Accord on Super Intelligence” in which signatories pledged safeguards, internal and external safety audits, independent board-level safety oversight, and regular meetings to establish industry standards. (DC AI Decoded, Oct. 5, 2026, time not specified)[4]
- The White House announced that **Jay Clayton** would lead a newly created **Super Intelligence Force** while retaining his role as director of national intelligence. (DC AI Decoded, Oct. 5, 2026, time not specified)[4]
- Senators **Mark Warner**, **Brian Schatz**, and **Andy Kim** sought unanimous consent for the Artificial Intelligence Risk Management and Security Act; **Sen. Ted Cruz** objected. (DC AI Decoded, Oct. 5, 2026, time not specified)[4]
- Rep. **Lori Trahan** and Rep. **Jay Obernolte** said the White House accord was an important step toward responsible development of advanced AI systems. (DC AI Decoded, Oct. 5, 2026, time not specified)[4]
- Sen. **Mark Warner** said AI posed both national-security and safety risks and called for a safety protocol. (DC AI Decoded, Oct. 5, 2026, time not specified)[4]
- Sen. **Amy Klobuchar** said bipartisan safeguards should address AI risks by the end of **2026**. (DC AI Decoded, Oct. 5, 2026, time not specified)[4]
- Sen. **Raja Krishnamoorthi** called for tighter safeguards on advanced U.S. AI chips and measures to prevent Chinese companies from evading export controls through overseas data centers. (DC AI Decoded, Oct. 5, 2026, time not specified)[4]
