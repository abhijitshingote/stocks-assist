## Armada Acquisition Corp. II (XRPN) `Digital Assets / SPAC`

- Shareholders approved Armada II’s business combination with **Evernorth Holdings** at a September 30, 2026 extraordinary meeting; the transaction is expected to close on **October 7**, subject to remaining closing conditions, with Nasdaq trading under **XRPN** expected to begin on **October 8**. (Evernorth/Armada II, Oct 1 08:00 ET).[10]

- The transaction and related private placements raised **more than $1 billion**, including approximately **$300 million** in expected gross cash proceeds; Evernorth expects to hold approximately **473 million XRP** at closing. (Financial Modeling Prep, Oct 3 12:14 ET).[14]

- XRP was reported near **$1.54**, up **2.8%**, implying a value of approximately **$727 million** for Evernorth’s expected 473 million XRP treasury. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2 05:09 ET).[1]

- The reported Friday, October 2 regular-session move was **+68.25%**, closing at **$39.42** on approximately **9.1 million shares**. (Financial Modeling Prep, Oct 3 12:14 ET).[14]

- A market recap listed XRPN among October 2’s top gainers at **+68.2%**; the same recap identified shareholder approval of the Evernorth combination as the relevant event. (The Desperate Trader, Oct 3 06:59 ET).[5]

- XRPN traded between **$10.10 and $48.59** on October 2, with a reported **4.65 million shares** traded and a price of **$45.52 at 16:39 ET**; this reading conflicts with the verified tape’s **$39.42** close, so the verified tape is used for the regular-session close. (MEXC, Oct 2 12:39 ET).[7]

- Evernorth’s planned treasury is tied to XRP price volatility; reported transaction risks include delayed or failed closing, failure to meet listing standards, changes in laws and regulations, and difficulty executing the strategy. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2 05:09 ET).[1]

- A market report described XRPN as rising from **$23.43** at Thursday’s close and opening Friday near **$28**, while noting that the expected October 7 closing and October 8 listing were prospective and subject to conditions. [report] (BitFinance, Oct 2; publication time not stated).[4]
