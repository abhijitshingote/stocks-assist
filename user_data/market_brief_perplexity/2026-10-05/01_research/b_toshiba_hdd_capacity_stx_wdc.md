## Toshiba HDD expansion `[Scope and timeline]`
- Toshiba plans to invest approximately **¥60 billion ($380 million)** in its Philippines facilities to **double production capacity for AI-data-center HDDs by fiscal 2027**, measured against fiscal 2025 capacity; the investment is its first major HDD-related investment in about five years. (Nikkei, Oct. 2, 2026; Investing.com, Oct. 2, 2026)
- The new production lines are designed for HDDs with **up to 40% higher per-drive capacity**; Toshiba plans **30TB-class drives in 2027**, mass production of **65TB-class drives around 2030**, and eventually **100TB-class products**. (Nikkei, Oct. 2, 2026; Tom’s Hardware, Oct. 2, 2026)
- Toshiba is targeting an increase in its HDD market share measured by storage capacity from **just over 10% to approximately 30% over the medium term**. (Nikkei, Oct. 2, 2026)
- Toshiba’s Philippine expansion is centered on its **Laguna Technopark** facility and includes additional lines for nearline HDD assembly; some equipment is already installed and first drives from the expanded lines have reportedly begun shipping. [report] (Tom’s Hardware, Oct. 2, 2026; HWBusters, Oct. 3, 2026)
- The reports specify a **fiscal-2027 completion target**, but do not give an exact completion month, production-unit target, or named customer contracts. (Nikkei, Oct. 2, 2026; AI Weekly, Oct. 4, 2026)
- The reported plan does not identify named cloud or AI-data-center customers, customer volume commitments, or customer contract dates; a separate report said the added factory could be relevant to buyers negotiating contracts for **2029 or 2031** shipments. [report] (AI Weekly, Oct. 4, 2026; FinanceFeeds, Oct. 3, 2026)
- Toshiba’s production-line expansion is expected to support drives with increased storage density, while additional investment is planned for **65TB-class production in 2030** and later **100TB-class drives**. (Nikkei, Oct. 2, 2026; Investing.com, Oct. 2, 2026)
