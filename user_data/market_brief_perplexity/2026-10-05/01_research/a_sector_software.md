## **PTC** `[Software & SaaS]`
- Schneider Electric agreed to acquire **PTC** for **$205 per share in cash**, valuing PTC’s equity at approximately **$22.6 billion** and enterprise value at **$23.7 billion**; the offer represents a **42.3% premium** to PTC’s previous closing price, with completion expected in **Q3 2027**. PTC rose **37% in pre-market trading** on October 5. (Reuters, Oct. 5, 06:48 ET)[1][4]
- Schneider Electric’s agreement confirmed reports published on **October 4** regarding the proposed acquisition. (Reuters, Oct. 5, 06:48 ET)[1]
