## Azenta, Inc. (AZTA) `[Life Sciences & Biotech]`
- Azenta’s fiscal third-quarter 2026 results were reported at **$0.16 non-GAAP EPS** versus a **$0.10–$0.11 analyst estimate**, and **$161.17–$161.18 million revenue** versus estimates of **$149.31–$150.51 million**; sample-management revenue was **$88 million** and multiomics revenue was **$73 million**. (Investing.com, Oct. 2, 2026, 10:45 ET; MarketBeat, Oct. 2, 2026, 08:22 ET)
- The company’s shares reached a **52-week high of $41.84–$42.00** on Friday, October 2, 2026, while the verified regular-session close was **$38.75, up 12.38%**. (Investing.com, Oct. 2, 2026, 10:45 ET; RTT News, Oct. 5, 2026, 04:22 ET)
- Azenta appointed **Dr. Martin D. Madaus** interim president and CEO effective **August 22, 2026**; the board approved a **$600,000 annual base salary**, a performance bonus equal to **100% of base salary**, and **$1.2 million** of time-vested restricted stock units. (Investing.com, Oct. 2, 2026, 10:45 ET)
- Azenta also approved September 11 retention grants for **four senior executives**, with individual awards ranging from **$500,000 to $1 million** and vesting over **two years**. (The Globe and Mail, Oct. 1, 2026)
- **Needham** reiterated a **Buy** rating and a **$37.00 price target**; the broader analyst consensus was **Hold**, with an average target of **$41.20**. (MarketBeat, Oct. 2, 2026, 08:22 ET)
- **Pomerantz LLP** announced an investor investigation on **October 1, 2026** concerning possible securities-law violations related to the company and certain officers and directors, including disclosures surrounding former CEO **John Marotta’s August 24 resignation**. (PRNewswire, Oct. 1, 2026)
- Azenta’s post-market indication was **$39.45, up $0.70 or 1.81%**, at **7:55 PM ET on October 2**; no additional company-specific announcement before the **October 5, 2026 12:20 ET** cutoff was identified in the gathered results. (Seeking Alpha, Oct. 3, 2026)
