## Everpure, Inc. (P) `[AI Infrastructure]`
- Everpure shares closed at **$140.14**, up **4.48%** on Friday, October 2, extending a six-session winning streak; the stock was reported at **$140.55**, up **0.29%**, at **19:49 ET** in after-hours trading on October 2 (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2 19:49 ET).
- A market commentary attributed the October 2 move partly to continued reaction to Everpure’s **September 23 analyst meeting**, where the company reaffirmed fiscal 2027 revenue guidance of **$5.03 billion–$5.07 billion** and non-GAAP operating income guidance of **$940 million–$960 million** (Quiver Quantitative, Oct. 2 18:31 ET).
