## Palantir Technologies Inc. (PLTR) `[AI Infrastructure]`
- Palantir named Armada its first **Certified Modular Data Center Partner**, combining Palantir’s Sovereign AI Operating System with Armada’s Galleon modular data centers, Armada Platform, and Sovereign AI Grid; financial terms, purchase commitments, customer count, and revenue-sharing terms were not disclosed. (Business Wire, Oct. 1, 22:00 ET; Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, 08:13 ET)[1][4][5]
- The Armada partnership was reported as supporting deployment of open-weight AI models on customer-controlled infrastructure in months rather than through multiyear infrastructure projects; Palantir shares were cited as nearly **1% higher in early Friday trading** before closing at **$188.75**, down **0.68%** on Oct. 2. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, 08:13 ET; Ad-Hoc-News, Oct. 4)[5][6]
- Palantir’s second-quarter 2026 revenue was **$1.935 billion**, up **92.83%** year over year, and EPS was **$0.41** versus a **$0.28 consensus estimate**; U.S. commercial revenue was **$764 million**, up **149%** year over year. (24/7 Wall St., Oct. 2, 08:45 ET; Ad-Hoc-News, Oct. 4)[6][15]
- Management’s previously issued third-quarter 2026 revenue guidance was approximately **$2.16 billion–$2.164 billion**, while full-year 2026 revenue guidance was **$8.150 billion–$8.158 billion**; Palantir had not confirmed its third-quarter earnings date, while Benzinga listed **Nov. 2**. (24/7 Wall St., Oct. 2, 08:45 ET; Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, 08:22 ET)[1][8][15]
- UBS maintained a **Buy** rating and raised its price target from **$220 to $250** on **Sept. 15, 2026**, outside the requested window but directly relevant to the stock’s valuation context. (CNBC, reported Oct. 2)[8]
- ARK Invest sold **20,805 Palantir shares** on Thursday, comprising **14,970 shares** through ARKQ and **5,835 shares** through ARKX; the reported value was approximately **$3.95 million** based on a **$190.40** closing price. (Benzinga, Oct. 2)[14]
