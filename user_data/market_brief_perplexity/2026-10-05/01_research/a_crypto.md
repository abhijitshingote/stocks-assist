## Crypto `[Crypto]`

- **Bitcoin (BTC)** traded at approximately **$86,200**, up **3.7% over seven days**, as of **10:55 ET on October 5**; the available report did not provide a 24-hour percentage change. (LCX, Oct 5 10:55 ET)
- **Ether (ETH)** traded at approximately **$2,727**, up **3.0% over seven days**, as of **10:55 ET on October 5**; the available report did not provide a 24-hour percentage change. (LCX, Oct 5 10:55 ET)
- U.S. spot **Bitcoin ETFs** recorded **$241.1 million of net inflows** during the week ended **October 2**, marking a **third consecutive weekly inflow**; cumulative net inflows reached **$57.8 billion**. (LCX, Oct 5 10:55 ET)
- U.S. spot **Ether ETFs** recorded **$138 million of net outflows** during the week ended **October 2**, following **$690 million of inflows** in the prior week; 2026 year-to-date net inflows were approximately **$1.5 billion**. (LCX, Oct 5 10:55 ET)
- A separate Farside-based report put the week ended October 2 at **$82.9 million of provisional Bitcoin ETF inflows** and approximately **$118 million of Ether ETF outflows**; the figures were described as **not final**. (FinanceFeeds via Ground News, Oct 5 04:29 ET)
- The SEC approved a **Cboe BZX rule change on October 2** permitting the listing of six **3× leveraged ETPs**, including Bitcoin- and Ether-linked products from Volatility Shares; separate **Form S-1 registration approval** is required before trading can begin. (Tokenist, Oct 5 10:09 ET)
- The Senate’s **CLARITY Act** remains stalled after a **49–50 procedural vote on September 15**, below the **60 votes** required to advance; the bill had not become law as of October 5. (LCX, Oct 5 10:54 ET)
- The CFTC’s updated guidance permits eligible assets such as **tokenized Treasuries, corporate bonds and money-market-fund shares** to be held in tokenized form when the blockchain version carries the same legal and economic rights as the traditional asset; the guidance does **not** authorize customer funds to be invested directly in Bitcoin or Ether. (Adalytica, Oct 5 07:15 ET)
- The CFTC guidance also permits regulated firms to use **blockchain and distributed-ledger technology for recordkeeping**, provided records remain authentic, reliable, retained and accessible. (Adalytica, Oct 5 07:15 ET)
- **Supercycle Finance**, led by co-founder and CEO **Michael Terpin**, announced a Bitcoin-treasury program on **October 2** and proposed a **Regulation A offering of up to $75 million**, with most proceeds intended for Bitcoin purchases; the company plans to buy during declines, sell at higher prices and hold U.S. Treasuries and other interest-bearing assets between transactions. (Bitcoin.com News, Oct 4 18:18 ET) [report]
- A report published October 4 said the SEC had approved a temporary **“Innovation Exemption”** for limited trading of tokenized U.S. stocks on selected on-chain venues; the same report said the SEC proposed a rule on October 1 addressing investment firms’ handling and custody of customer crypto assets. (LCX, Oct 5 10:54 ET) [report]
