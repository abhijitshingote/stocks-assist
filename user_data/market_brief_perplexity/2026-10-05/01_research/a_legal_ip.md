## **FSLR** / JA Solar / Corning [Specialty Materials & IP Licensing]
- First Solar filed a patent-infringement lawsuit on October 2 against JA Solar Technology, JA Solar International, JA Solar Vietnam, JA Solar Industrial and American Panel Solutions, a Corning subsidiary, in the U.S. District Court for the District of Delaware; the complaint alleges infringement of U.S. Patent No. 9,130,074 concerning TOPCon crystalline-silicon solar-cell manufacturing methods. The case was pending as of October 5 at 12:20 ET; no import-ban order was reported. (NewsQuawk, Oct. 5 10:16 ET; Now.Solar, Oct. 4 02:00 ET)

## **NVDA** / Greg Lui [AI Compute]
- California businessman Greg Lui was charged by U.S. prosecutors with conspiracy, smuggling and money laundering over allegations that he routed servers containing more than $300 million of Nvidia AI chips to China through Malaysia and Singapore using false documentation; the criminal case was reported on October 2, with no conviction reported by October 5 at 12:20 ET. [report] (NewsQuawk citing Bloomberg, Oct. 5 10:16 ET)

## **ANTHROPIC**, **OPENAI**, METR [AI Infrastructure]
- The Federal Trade Commission opened an industry-wide probe into Anthropic, OpenAI and other AI laboratories over potential consumer harms from AI agents; an FTC official said the agency planned formal information demands and compelled executive testimony, including from Anthropic, OpenAI and METR. The investigation was active as of October 5 at 12:20 ET. (The Business Standard citing Reuters, Oct. 5 08:50 ET)

## Crypto firms / OCC [Crypto]
- The Independent Community Bankers of America sued the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency on October 2 in the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia, alleging that the OCC exceeded its authority by issuing a rule and guidance allowing cryptocurrency companies to apply for limited national trust-bank charters; the suit sought revocation of the rule and related guidance. (Reuters, Oct. 2 08:21 ET)

## **UTHR** / **LQDA** [Pharma]
- A Delaware federal court ruled on September 30 that Liquidia Technologies induced infringement of United Therapeutics’ U.S. Patent No. 11,826,327 by marketing Yutrepia for pulmonary hypertension associated with interstitial lung disease and found asserted claims 1 and 14 valid; the parties had until October 7 to submit a proposed final judgment, so no FDA-withdrawal order had been entered by October 5 at 12:20 ET. (Mugglehead, Oct. 4 10:57 ET)

## **TRI** / ROSS Intelligence [Software & SaaS]
- The Third U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals ruled in Thomson Reuters’ favor in its copyright dispute with ROSS Intelligence, and ROSS said on October 2 that it would seek U.S. Supreme Court review; the litigation was unresolved as of October 5 at 12:20 ET. (LawNext, Oct. 2 19:09 ET)
