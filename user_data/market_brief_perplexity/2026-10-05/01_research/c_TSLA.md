## Tesla, Inc. (TSLA) `[Electric Vehicles]`
- Tesla delivered **486,532 vehicles** worldwide in Q3 2026, versus its company-compiled consensus of **461,974**, while production was **464,391**; the delivery figure was also **2% below** Q3 2025’s **497,099**. (Tesla, Oct 2; CNBC, Oct 2)
- Tesla shares closed Friday, October 2, at **$370.59**, up **4.65%**, after the delivery report; the stock was reported up approximately **5%** during Friday trading. (MarketBeat, Oct 2; CNBC, Oct 2)
- **Model 3 and Model Y** deliveries totaled **478,237**, versus Tesla’s internal forecast of **450,712**. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2)
- European demand helped offset weaker sales in the **United States and China**; Tesla’s Q3 deliveries were approximately **2% below** the year-earlier period. (New York Post, Oct 2; Yahoo Finance, Oct 2)
- Tesla installed **13.7 GWh** of energy-storage products in Q3, below the **15.9 GWh** forecast and up from **12.5 GWh** a year earlier. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2)
- **Morgan Stanley** maintained its **Hold** rating after the delivery report; **RBC Capital Markets** maintained an **Outperform** rating and described the delivery total as “impressive.” (CNBC, Oct 2)
- **Oppenheimer analyst Colin Rusch** said the delivery result could support operating cash flow and gross margins, and identified Full Self-Driving as a potential demand and margin driver. (Investing.com, Oct 2)
- **Elon Musk** said on Saturday that “something may come of” discussions between **TSMC** and Tesla/SpaceX’s **Terafab** project; the discussions were reported as involving possible TSMC participation in operating semiconductor factories in Texas. **[report]** (Yahoo Finance, Oct 5 05:48 ET)
- Tesla shares rose approximately **1% overnight** Sunday after Musk’s comments on the TSMC discussions. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 5 05:48 ET)
- **Rivian Automotive** shares fell approximately **3%** on Friday despite an in-line quarterly delivery report, while Tesla rose approximately **5%** after exceeding its delivery consensus. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2)
- Tesla’s reported **486,532** deliveries exceeded Visible Alpha’s average estimate of **456,896** by approximately **29,636 vehicles**. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 4 19:38 ET)
- **Roadster** reveal timing was reported as rescheduled for **October 15, 2026**, and Tesla’s Q3 financial results were expected around **October 21, 2026**. (MarketBeat, Oct 2)
