## MaxLinear, Inc. (MXL) `Communications & Networking`
- MaxLinear closed at **$105.93** on Friday, October 2, up **14.99%** from **$92.12** on October 1; volume was **4,757,799 shares**. (Seeking Alpha, Oct 2; publication time not stated)
- The October 2 coverage attributed the move to renewed attention on **infrastructure and data-center chips**, including MaxLinear’s optical-networking products used in AI data centers. (Seeking Alpha, Oct 2; publication time not stated)
- MaxLinear had gained **50.7% during September 2026**; the cited drivers were demand for optical-networking chips linking AI data centers, bullish analyst reports, and a rebound from approximately **$60** summer lows. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2, 14:27 CT / 15:27 ET)
- MaxLinear CFO **Steven Litchfield** said on September 9 that optical-chip lead times were **26–28 weeks**, customers were scheduling spring 2027 deliveries, and he did not expect pricing to decline materially through the end of 2027. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2, 14:27 CT / 15:27 ET)
- MaxLinear’s optical-networking market share was reported at **no more than 7%**, with Litchfield targeting roughly a **threefold increase** over the next **2–3 years**. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2, 14:27 CT / 15:27 ET)
- StoneX and Stifel had published price targets of **$125** and **$120**, respectively; S&P Global Ratings had issued a positive credit-outlook action on **September 11, 2026**. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2, 14:27 CT / 15:27 ET)
- MaxLinear announced its third-quarter 2026 earnings conference call for **October 22, 2026, at 4:30 p.m. ET**. (MaxLinear release carried by StockTitan, Oct 1, 16:35 ET)
