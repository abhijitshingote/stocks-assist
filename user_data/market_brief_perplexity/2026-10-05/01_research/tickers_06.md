## Stablecoin Development Corp. (SDEV) `[Digital Assets]`
- SDEV rose **104.37%** to the verified October 2 close of **$7.48**, with approximately **137.6 million shares** traded; a specific October 2 company announcement was not identified (Financial Modeling Prep, Oct. 2; exchange tape).
- A September **S-3/A** filing disclosed that SDEV held approximately **2.315 billion SKY tokens** as of September 13, representing about **10% of SKY’s total supply**, with nearly all tokens staked in the Sky Protocol; this was cited as the company-specific explanation for the stock’s October 2 move (Gate News, Oct. 5 02:00 ET; filing date: September 2026).
- SDEV reported on September 29 that, after inquiries concerning unusual trading activity, it was unaware of material nonpublic information, a pending transaction, or another undisclosed business development explaining the trading (Business Insider, Oct. 2 19:51 ET; SEC report dated September 29).
- SDEV was reported at **$11.52**, up **51.68%**, in premarket trading at **06:34 ET** on October 5; this reading was before the 12:20 ET cutoff (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 5 06:34 ET).
- SKY, the token held by SDEV, was reported to have risen from approximately **$0.80 to above $0.90** during the same period in which SDEV advanced from approximately **$0.84 on September 17 to $7.48 on October 2** (TokenPost, Oct. 4 21:41 ET).
