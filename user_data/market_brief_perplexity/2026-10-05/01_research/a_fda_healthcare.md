## **LLY** [Pharma]
- The FDA expanded Jaypirca (pirtobrutinib) on October 2, 2026, for adults with previously untreated chronic lymphocytic leukemia or small lymphocytic lymphoma without a known 17p deletion; the approval was based on the 282-patient BRUIN CLL-313 trial. (Eli Lilly press release, Oct 2, 15:05 ET)[1]

## **NVO** [Pharma]
- Novo Nordisk said on October 2, 2026, that the FDA review of denecimig for hemophilia A would extend beyond the company’s expected third-quarter decision because remediation at a manufacturing site was still underway; Novo said it had not received a new FDA decision date and continued to target a U.S. launch in the first half of 2027. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2; latest update Oct 4, 10:13 ET)[11]

## **MRK / ESALY** [Pharma]
- The FDA had approved Merck’s Welireg (belzutifan) plus Eisai’s Lenvima (lenvatinib) on September 24, 2026, for adults with advanced renal cell carcinoma with a clear-cell component after a PD-1 or PD-L1 inhibitor; the approval was cited in an October 2 healthcare regulatory roundup. (Optum Rx, Oct 2, 19:44 ET)[5]

## **ABBV** [Pharma]
- The FDA had approved AbbVie’s Juvmo (tavapadon) on September 28, 2026, for Parkinson’s disease in adults; the approval was cited in an October 2 regulatory roundup. (Optum Rx, Oct 2, 19:44 ET)[5]

## **LLY** [Pharma]
- The FDA had approved Olumiant (baricitinib) on September 25, 2026, for severe alopecia areata in adults and patients aged 12 years and older; the approval was cited in an October 2 regulatory roundup. (Optum Rx, Oct 2, 19:44 ET)[5]

## **MRVI / INZY** [Pharma]
- The FDA had approved Atebrioz (zilurgisertib), developed by Mirum Pharmaceuticals and Incyte, on September 25, 2026, to reduce new heterotopic ossification in adults and pediatric patients aged 12 years and older with fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva. (Optum Rx, Oct 2, 19:44 ET)[5]
- In the 63-patient phase 2 PROGRESS study, new heterotopic ossification volume decreased by 3.2 cm³ with zilurgisertib versus a 24.6 cm³ increase with placebo through week 24. (Contemporary Pediatrics, Oct 2; latest update Oct 4, 22:19 ET)[13]

## **EDSA / Edwards Lifesciences** [Diagnostics / Medical Devices]
- The FDA approved the AUTUS Size-Adjustable Valve on October 1, 2026, through the premarket-approval pathway for pediatric patients with congenital pulmonary valve disease; Edwards Lifesciences holds the approval for the device developed by Autus Valve Technologies. (Nova Pharma News, Oct 4, 16:19 ET)[12]
- The pivotal study enrolled 62 children at 12 U.S. sites; implantation succeeded in all patients, and among the first 60 patients at six months there were no deaths, strokes, or treated clot-related events. (Contemporary Pediatrics, Oct 2; latest update Oct 4, 22:19 ET)[13]

## **NVIV** [Pharma]
- Invivyd said its investigational COVID-19 monoclonal antibody was safe and tolerable in a phase 3 randomized clinical trial; the October 2 report did not provide efficacy data, patient count, or statistical results. (KFF Health News, Oct 2, 2026)[6]

## **BHVN / SK Biopharmaceuticals** [Pharma]
- SK Biopharmaceuticals licensed Biohaven’s Kv7 ion-channel platform and opakalim in an August 2026 transaction valued at up to $795 million; the deal was cited on October 2 in connection with an FDA-requested pause in new enrollment in one opakalim phase 3 trial. (PharmaVoice, Oct 2, 09:33 ET)[15]
- The FDA requested that Biohaven pause new enrollment while the company addressed questions about a metabolite observed in rodent studies; the pause could delay a key opakalim readout. (PharmaVoice, Oct 2, 09:33 ET)[15]

## **DBVT** [Pharma]
- DBV Technologies submitted a Biologics License Application to the FDA for the Viaskin peanut patch in children aged 4 to 7 years and requested priority review; the filing was supported by the phase 3 VITESSE trial of 654 children at 86 sites. (Contemporary Pediatrics, Oct 2; latest update Oct 5, 02:19 ET)[13]
- In VITESSE, 46.6% of treated children versus 14.8% receiving placebo met the 12-month responder criterion, and approximately 83% versus 48% increased their peanut-tolerance threshold. (Contemporary Pediatrics, Oct 2; latest update Oct 5, 02:19 ET)[13]

## FDA / Diagnostics [Diagnostics]
- The FDA’s Molecular and Clinical Genetics Devices Panel voted 10–0 on safety, 6–4 on effectiveness, and 7–2 with one abstention on the benefit-risk profile of GRAIL’s Galleri multi-cancer blood test on September 23, 2026; the FDA’s PMA decision was expected in the following months. (MedDevice Startups, Oct 4; latest update Oct 5, 03:11 ET)[10]

## **MDT** [Medical Devices]
- FDA granted Medtronic’s IN.PACT BTK paclitaxel-coated balloon Breakthrough Device designation on October 1, 2026, for below-the-knee peripheral artery disease; the report stated that no drug-coated balloon was approved for that U.S. indication at that time. (MedDevice Startups, Oct 4; latest update Oct 5, 03:11 ET)[10]
