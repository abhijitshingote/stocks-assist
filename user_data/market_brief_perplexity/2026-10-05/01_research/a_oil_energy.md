## OPEC+ `[Energy]`
- Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Kazakhstan, Algeria and Oman agreed on October 4 to keep **November 2026 oil-production targets unchanged** from September levels. (Reuters, Oct 4, 2026, 10:05 AM ET)
- The seven OPEC+ members produced approximately **25 million barrels per day in August**, up **630,000 barrels per day** from July and approximately **5 million barrels per day below** their combined February pre-conflict level. (Reuters, Oct 4, 2026, 10:05 AM ET)
- OPEC+ still had approximately **2 million barrels per day of production cuts** covering most members; its next seven-member meeting was scheduled for **November 1, 2026**. (Reuters, Oct 4, 2026, 10:05 AM ET)
- November production targets were listed as **10.478 million barrels per day for Saudi Arabia**, **9.949 million for Russia**, **4.431 million for Iraq**, **2.676 million for Kuwait**, **1.628 million for Kazakhstan**, **1.007 million for Algeria**, and **841,000 for Oman**. (OPEC+ statement reported by Investing.com, Oct 4, 2026, 05:41 AM ET)

## G7 Emergency Energy Reserves and Export Policy `[Macro / Commodities / Geopolitics]`
- G7 countries agreed on October 2 to release up to **100 million barrels of diesel and crude from emergency reserves** and pledged to refrain from energy-export restrictions after pressure from U.S. President Donald Trump. (Reuters, Oct 5, 2026, 06:20 AM ET)

## Energy Supply and Geopolitics `[Macro / Commodities / Geopolitics]`
