## **ON** / **SYNA** — Semiconductors [Acquisition]

- ON Semiconductor amended its agreement to acquire **Synaptics** for **$123 per SYNA share in cash**, valuing the transaction at approximately **$5.7 billion**, replacing the prior all-stock structure valued at roughly $7 billion; U.S. antitrust clearance had been obtained, with closing expected by mid-2027 subject to Synaptics shareholder approval and remaining regulatory approvals. **ON rose approximately 8% and SYNA rose approximately 14%** in Friday morning trading. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, 10:27 ET; Advfn, Oct. 2, 06:22 ET)
