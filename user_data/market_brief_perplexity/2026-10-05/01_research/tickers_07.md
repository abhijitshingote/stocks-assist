## ChipMOS TECHNOLOGIES Inc. (IMOS) `[Test & Advanced Packaging]`
- ChipMOS’s Taiwan-listed shares reached a record closing high of NT$127 on Friday, October 2, 2026, after rising nearly 10%; the move occurred alongside gains of more than 5% in MPI, WinWay, ShunSin Technology, and Powertech Technology among Taiwan semiconductor-testing and packaging companies (Taiwan News, Oct 2, 20:35 ET).[9]
- Taiwan coverage attributed the sector move to higher demand for memory packaging, packaging-price increases, and tighter capacity; ChipMOS reported August 2026 net profit of NT$433 million, up 117.6% year over year, with EPS of NT$0.62 (Bnext, Oct 5, 06:16 ET).[8]
- ChipMOS’s second-quarter 2026 EPS was NT$1.28, versus NT$0.72 in the first quarter; first-half EPS totaled NT$2.00, while January–August revenue reached NT$19.927 billion, up 30.04% year over year (Bnext, Oct 5, 06:16 ET).[8]
- ChipMOS’s memory-packaging and testing business represented approximately 51% of second-quarter revenue; reported second-quarter gross margin was 17.96%, versus 6.6% in the second quarter of 2025 (International Business Times Australia, Oct 3, 12:37 ET).[1]
- ChipMOS has been adding DDR5 testing capacity, acquired a plant in Taiwan’s Southern Taiwan Science Park, and is expanding into finished-chip testing for smartphone application processors and AI ASICs, as well as optical-bump packaging for silicon-photonics applications (International Business Times Australia, Oct 3, 12:37 ET).[1]
- UBS raised its target price for ChipMOS to NT$166 in August 2026; the target was reported in the October 5 Taiwan-market coverage (Bnext, Oct 5, 06:16 ET).[8]
- ChipMOS’s September 29, 2026 Form 6-K reported second-quarter revenue of NT$7.383 billion, up 28.7% year over year; pretax profit was NT$1.027 billion, up 255.4%, and profit attributable to owners was NT$892 million (Ad Hoc News, Oct 2, 06:28 ET).[3]
- ChipMOS reported August 2026 revenue of NT$2.7857 billion, up 33.3% year over year (Ad Hoc News, Oct 2, 06:28 ET).[3]
- A market-mover review found no company filing or reported company-specific announcement on Friday, October 2, explaining the Nasdaq ADR’s 14.61% gain; the closest filing was the September 29 Form 6-K, and the reported explanation was the broader semiconductor and memory-packaging rally (Paper Trail KR, Oct 1, 22:04 ET).[6]
- The next reported earnings date is November 10, 2026, for the quarter ended September 2026 (JRHISSE, Oct 5, 01:43 ET).[12]
