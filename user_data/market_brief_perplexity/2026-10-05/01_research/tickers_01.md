## Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SPCX) `[AI Infrastructure]`
- SpaceX shares rose **7.35%** on Friday, October 2, after the company completed **three launches in less than 13 hours**: NASA’s **Crew-13** mission carrying four astronauts to the International Space Station, the **Transporter-18** rideshare mission carrying 130 payloads, and the classified **NROL-97** National Reconnaissance Office mission; the Falcon Heavy flight was the first NRO payload launched on Falcon Heavy (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, 17:00 ET).[1]
- Transporter-18 included a Planet Labs satellite carrying Google tensor-processing units for **Project Suncatcher**, Google’s first orbital test of AI chips; the experiment is intended to test whether the hardware can operate reliably in space (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, 11:49 ET).[5]
- SpaceX’s Starship **Flight 14** reached Earth orbit for the first time earlier in the week and deployed **26 Starlink V3 satellites**; one of the vehicle’s six upper-stage Raptor engines shut down prematurely, shortening the mission to about three hours (Aviation Week, Oct. 2, 07:33 ET).[6]
- On Monday morning, October 5, SpaceX scrubbed the planned launch of **21 Space Development Agency Tranche 1 Transport Layer A satellites** during the final minute of the countdown; the next attempt was scheduled for Tuesday, October 6, at **04:17 ET** (Spaceflight Now, Oct. 5, 05:21 ET).[4]
- The Federal Aviation Administration opened environmental scoping for SpaceX’s proposed modification of its vehicle-operator license to permit commercial Starship-Super Heavy operations at **Launch Complex 37**, including up to **76 launches and 152 landings per year**; public comments are due October 14 (Federal Register notice, published Oct. 1; reported Oct. 4).[10]
- Elon Musk said on Sunday that SpaceXAI would be renamed SpaceXSI after an X user proposed replacing “AI” with “super intelligence”; the statement was reported as an overnight catalyst, with shares up more than **1%** overnight late Sunday (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 5, 00:34 ET).[14]
- Morgan Stanley reportedly described SPCX shares as “unusually cheap” ahead of Starship Flight 15 and cited an unchanged **$300 price target**; the report was based on an excerpt shared on X and is **[report]** (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 5, 00:34 ET).[14]
- SpaceX had not announced a date for its first full-quarter public-company earnings report; the report could affect the release of up to **1.3 billion locked-up shares** beginning on the second full trading day after earnings (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 3, 00:01 ET).[2]
