## Infosys Limited (INFY) `[Software & SaaS]`
- Infosys shares were reported down more than 1% on Monday, October 5, as Indian IT stocks traded ahead of the sector’s earnings season; the report gave no new Infosys-specific corporate announcement for the session (Upstox, Oct 5, 00:22 ET).[1]
- Accenture reported fourth-quarter revenue of **$18.68 billion** versus an analyst estimate of **$18.03 billion** and forecast fiscal-year revenue growth of **3%–6%**; the update put Infosys and other Indian IT exporters in focus when Indian markets reopened on October 5 (Economic Times, Oct 5, time not stated).[2]
- Infosys’s Monday move was reported as a decline despite Accenture’s outlook; one market report cited Infosys down **1.79%**, while the verified tape records a **2.73%** decline for the session (Univest, Oct 5, 03:58 ET).[3]
- Infosys had announced on October 1 a collaboration with **Columbia University** to establish the **Infosys Topaz–Columbia University Enterprise AI Center**, focused on generative and agentic AI applications; the announcement was outside the requested October 2–5 news window but was reported as relevant background (Stock Titan, Oct 1, time not stated).[4]
- Infosys’s next reported catalyst is its **Q2 FY27 results on October 23, 2026**; the same report cited an existing analyst view that the company could reduce the upper end of its **1.5%–3%** revenue-growth guidance range to **2.5%** (Univest, Oct 5, 03:58 ET).[3]
