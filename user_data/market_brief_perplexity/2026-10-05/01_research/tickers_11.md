## Intel Corp. (INTC) `[Semiconductors]`
- Intel shares closed at **$119.33 on October 2, 2026**, down **0.56%** from $120.00; the session range was $118.96–$126.00. (Ad-hoc-news.de, Oct 4 18:45 ET)
- A Yahoo Finance report attributed October 2 intraday interest to Intel’s **High-NA EUV** program entering production on selected layers of Panther Lake processors; ASML’s September 8 update said more than **1 million wafers** had been processed across testing, research, and production. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2; updated Oct 4 18:16 ET)
- Intel’s latest reported quarter included **$16.13 billion revenue**, up **25.42%** year over year, and **$0.42 non-GAAP EPS** versus a reported **$0.2175 consensus**; Q3 2026 earnings were scheduled for **October 22, 2026**. (Ad-hoc-news.de, Oct 4 18:45 ET)
- Data Center and AI revenue was reported at **$6.26 billion**, up **59%** year over year, while Intel Foundry revenue was **$5.76 billion**, up **31%**. (Ad-hoc-news.de, Oct 4 18:45 ET)
- Intel Foundry reported a **$2.1 billion quarterly loss** on **$293 million external revenue**, and CFO David Zinsner said the end-2027 breakeven target could move to **2028**. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2 08:02 ET)
- Intel was reported to be in talks with **SK hynix** about producing memory chips in the United States.[report] (Ad-hoc-news.de, Oct 2 15:22 ET)
- Intel traded at **€106.48** in European after-hours trading at **15:12 ET on October 2**, down **0.08%** from the prior €106.57 quote. (Ad-hoc-news.de, Oct 2 15:39 ET)
