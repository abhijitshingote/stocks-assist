## Wabash National Corporation (WNC) `Transportation Equipment`
- WNC closed at **$14.04**, up **12.68%** on Friday, October 2, 2026; the quoted after-hours price was **$14.03**, down **0.07%**, at **16:01 ET**. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2 16:01 ET)[5]
- A market-mover report attributed WNC’s **6.4%** intraday rise to weaker-than-expected September U.S. employment data: payrolls increased by **29,000** versus the **84,000** consensus, unemployment rose to **4.2%**, and Treasury yields fell after the release. - Wabash’s Q3 2026 outlook called for revenue of **$440 million–$460 million** and adjusted diluted loss per share of **$0.40–$0.50**. (Ad Hoc News, Oct 1 2026; updated Oct 2)[4]

- Wabash declared a quarterly cash dividend of **$0.08 per share**, with an ex-dividend date of **October 8, 2026**. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 3 12:24 ET)[5]
