## World Labs / Strategic rationale `[Products and AI capabilities]`

- AMD announced a definitive agreement on **September 28, 2026** to acquire World Labs for approximately **$8.2 billion in an all-stock transaction**, with closing expected by **year-end 2026**, subject to regulatory approval and customary conditions. (GlobeNewswire/AMD, Sep. 28, 2026; reported Oct. 5)
- World Labs develops **spatial-intelligence models** that generate, reconstruct, and simulate interactive **3D environments** from **text, images, video, and 3D data**. (AMD company release, Sep. 28, 2026)
- World Labs’ **Marble** platform generates persistent, explorable 3D worlds from text, images, and video. (Barchart, Oct. 1, 2026)
- World Labs’ **Atlas** is a multimodal world model pretrained from scratch for text, images, video, and 3D data; World Labs added Atlas on **September 1, 2026**. (Barchart, Oct. 1, 2026)
- World Labs is developing technology for **robotic learning and simulation**, including applications involving robotics, autonomous systems, cinematic production, and physical-world simulation. (AMD company release, Sep. 28, 2026; Barchart, Oct. 1, 2026)
- AMD said World Labs’ researchers and model experts would help AMD develop **AI hardware, software, and systems** around emerging models and applications, including **reasoning, robotics, simulation, and physical AI**. (AMD company release, Sep. 28, 2026)
- AMD CEO **Lisa Su** said access to frontier-model research would help AMD anticipate future computing requirements and shape its hardware, software, and system roadmaps. (Benzinga/TradingView, Oct. 1, 2026)
- World Labs founder **Fei-Fei Li** is expected to join AMD as **executive vice president and chief scientist**, reporting to Lisa Su, after closing. (AMD company release, Sep. 28, 2026)
