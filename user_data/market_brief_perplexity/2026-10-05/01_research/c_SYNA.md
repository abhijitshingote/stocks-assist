## Synaptics Incorporated (SYNA) `Semiconductors`
- ON Semiconductor and Synaptics amended their merger agreement on October 1, replacing the original all-stock transaction with an **all-cash acquisition at $123 per SYNA share**, valuing Synaptics at approximately **$5.7 billion** versus roughly $7 billion under the prior structure. The change followed an unsolicited competing proposal for Synaptics.[report] (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2, 2026, 08:27 ET).[1]
- Synaptics closed October 2 at **$121.10**, up **14.08%**, after closing October 1 at **$106.15**; its weekly gain was **17.9%**. ON Semiconductor rose approximately **6.0%** to **$84.89** on October 2.[1][6] (Insider Monkey, Oct 3, 2026, 23:30 ET; UOB Kay Hian, Oct 5, 2026, 09:00 ET).
- SYNA traded at **$120.76** in premarket trading on October 2, up **13.8%** from the October 1 close of $106.15.[3] (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2, 2026, 09:22 ET).
- The revised consideration represents approximately a **16% premium** to Synaptics’ October 1 closing price; the transaction is expected to close by **mid-2027**, subject to Synaptics shareholder approval and remaining regulatory approvals.[1][3] (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2, 2026, 09:22 ET).
- The revised transaction removes the stock consideration and associated dilution exposure for ON Semiconductor shareholders; ON Semiconductor said the transaction is expected to be immediately accretive to adjusted earnings per share after closing.[2] (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2, 2026, 08:27 ET).
- The amended agreement retains previously identified annual run-rate synergies of **$200 million** and includes potential revenue synergies and plans to bring part of Synaptics’ production in-house.[8] (RTTNews, Oct 2, 2026, 08:24 ET).
- The U.S. Federal Trade Commission had cleared the transaction; shareholder approval and other regulatory approvals remained outstanding.[3] (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2, 2026, 09:22 ET).
- Bank of America analysts said the revised terms could be received more favorably because they remove the dilution overhang while preserving the strategic rationale; they wrote that Synaptics fills key gaps in ON Semiconductor’s portfolio.[1][10] (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2, 2026, 08:27 ET; Investopedia, Oct 2, 2026, 13:27 ET).
- Rosenblatt Securities raised its Synaptics price target from **$115 to $123** while maintaining a **Neutral** rating.[4][15] (MarketBeat, Oct 2, 2026, 08:24 ET).
- ON Semiconductor’s acquisition rationale includes combining Synaptics’ chips for **autonomous driving and robotics** with ON Semiconductor’s automotive and data-center semiconductor portfolio.[1] (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2, 2026, 08:27 ET).
- The revised merger terms were the identified catalyst for the October 2 moves in both companies: SYNA gained **14.08%** and ON gained approximately **6%**.[1][6] (Yahoo Finance, Oct 2, 2026, 08:27 ET; UOB Kay Hian, Oct 5, 2026, 09:00 ET).
