## Super Micro Computer, Inc. (SMCI) `[AI Infrastructure]`
- Super Micro shares closed at **$43.69 on October 2, 2026**, up **4.22%**; a report attributed the move to the company’s shipment of **NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72** systems and attention to its AI-server order pipeline (MarketBeat, Oct 2; Yahoo Finance, Oct 3).
- Supermicro began shipping Vera Rubin NVL72 racks on **September 23, 2026**; each system combines **72 Rubin GPUs**, **36 Vera CPUs**, and direct liquid cooling through the company’s Data Center Building Block Solutions (TradingView/GuruFocus, Oct 2).
- The company reported more than **$60 billion of fiscal fourth-quarter 2026 orders** and forecast fiscal 2027 revenue of **$65 billion to $72 billion**; its manufacturing network includes the United States, Taiwan, Malaysia, and the Netherlands, with monthly capacity expected to exceed **6,000 racks** (TradingView/GuruFocus, Oct 2).
- Fiscal fourth-quarter 2026 EPS was **$1.70** versus a **$0.92 consensus estimate**, while revenue was **$11.12 billion**, up **93.2% year over year** and below the **$11.60 billion consensus** (MarketBeat, Oct 2).
- Super Micro’s fiscal 2026 revenue was reported at **$39.1 billion**, up **78% year over year**, and adjusted EPS at **$3.63**, up **76%** (Ad-hoc-news.de, Oct 3).
- NetApp announced a strategic collaboration with Super Micro on **September 29, 2026**, pairing Supermicro rack-scale AI systems and liquid cooling with NetApp’s data-management platform for AI factories, enterprise customers, neoclouds, and sovereign-AI projects (Simply Wall St, Oct 4).
- A California federal judge dismissed a shareholder lawsuit against Super Micro on **October 1, 2026**, according to a report citing Bloomberg Law; the suit alleged that the company misled investors about internal accounting deficiencies (Ad-hoc-news.de, Oct 3).
- The reported after-hours indication was **€38.40 at 7:35 p.m. CEST on October 2**, equivalent to **1:35 p.m. ET**, up **3.20%** versus the prior Lang & Schwarz close; no separate Monday, October 5 pre-market catalyst through **12:20 ET** was identified in the gathered results (Ad-hoc-news.de, Oct 2).
