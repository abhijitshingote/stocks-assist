## Hewlett Packard Enterprise Company (HPE) `[AI Infrastructure]`
- HPE shares rose **7.36% on October 2** after investor-day disclosures, analyst price-target increases, and a broader technology-sector rally; the investor day included a **$1.2 billion order from Vultr** for Helios AI server racks powered by AMD chips and deployed at Vultr’s U.S. data centers. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2 18:34 GMT-5)
- HPE raised its fiscal-2027 networking-revenue growth outlook to **high-teens to low-20%**, from **14%-17%** previously; it projected data-center networking revenue growth in the **low- to mid-50% range annually through fiscal 2029** and networking operating margins in the **mid- to high-20% range**. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2 18:34 GMT-5)
- HPE reported fiscal third-quarter 2026 revenue of **$12.21 billion** and non-GAAP EPS of **$1.11**, versus consensus EPS of **$0.95**; fiscal-fourth-quarter guidance was **$13.9-$14.8 billion** of revenue and **$1.20-$1.30** of non-GAAP EPS. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2 11:30 GMT-3)
- HPE raised fiscal-2026 revenue-growth guidance to **34%-37%** and non-GAAP EPS guidance to **$3.75-$3.85**. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2 11:30 GMT-3)
- **Daiwa Securities Group** upgraded HPE from **Neutral to Outperform** and set a **$75** price target on October 2. (MarketBeat, Oct. 2; publication time not stated)
- **Citigroup** raised its HPE price target to **$92 from $76** while maintaining a **Buy** rating. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2 18:34 GMT-5)
- The September U.S. employment report showed **29,000** nonfarm payroll additions versus an **84,000** consensus estimate; the unemployment rate was **4.2% versus 4.1% expected**, contributing to the broader equity-market move cited in coverage. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2 18:34 GMT-5)
