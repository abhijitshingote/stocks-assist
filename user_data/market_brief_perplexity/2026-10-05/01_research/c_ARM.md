## Arm Holdings plc American Depositary Shares (ARM) `[Specialty Materials & IP Licensing]`
- ARM closed at **$307.49**, up **5.18%** on Friday, October 2, while the Nasdaq-100 semiconductor names **Teradyne**, **Monolithic Power Systems**, and **Marvell Technology** also gained more than **4%**; coverage linked the move to a broader chip rally after a weaker-than-expected September jobs report reduced expectations of an October Federal Reserve rate hike. (Investing.com, Oct 4 12:24 ET; Investors.com, Oct 5 02:09 ET)
- Nvidia disclosed that **Arm-based processors** support AI-agent workload execution and safety monitoring in its **BlueField-4** infrastructure platform; ARM reached an intraday high of **$318.36** before closing at **$307.49**. (Blockonomi, Oct 2 13:05 ET; Investing.com, Oct 4 12:24 ET)
- ARM rose alongside **AMD**, which gained approximately **3%**, and other semiconductor stocks as coverage cited AI-infrastructure demand and recent earnings beats from **AMD, Nvidia, and Broadcom**. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 4 18:35 ET)
- Arm’s fiscal first-quarter 2027 results, reported on **July 29**, included **$1.29 billion** of revenue versus **$1.26 billion** consensus and **$0.45** EPS versus **$0.40** consensus; revenue increased **22.4%** year over year. (MarketBeat, Oct 2 05:48 ET)
- Arm CEO **Rene Haas** has identified **$2 billion** of customer orders for the company’s AGI CPU and said manufacturing constraints involving wafers, substrates, test equipment, and memory were easing; the comments were originally reported on **September 16** and were cited in October 2 coverage. (Ad-hoc-news, Oct 2 04:48 ET)
- The stock traded above the reported Wall Street mean price target of approximately **$289** after the October 2 close; another consensus compilation listed a **“Moderate Buy”** rating and an average target of **$303.32**. (Yahoo Finance, Oct 4 23:24 ET; MarketBeat, Oct 2 05:48 ET)
- No separately reported **analyst rating or price-target change**, **offering**, **acquisition**, **lawsuit**, **FDA action**, or **new government restriction** involving ARM was identified in the stated window.
- No separately reported **after-hours or pre-market ARM catalyst** before **12:20 ET on Monday, October 5** was identified; available coverage attributed the move to Nvidia/BlueField-4 exposure and the broader semiconductor rally.
