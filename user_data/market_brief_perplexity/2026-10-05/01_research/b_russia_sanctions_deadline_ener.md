## Graham Act / Trump deliberations `[Policy]`
- President Donald Trump had **not decided** by October 4 whether to impose tariffs on major Russian-energy buyers **China and India**; Energy Secretary **Chris Wright** said Trump would “evaluate the trade-offs” and make decisions at the last minute. (CBS News, Oct. 4; published Oct. 5 05:04 ET)
- The administration’s decision deadline was identified as **October 18, 2026**, under the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act signed by Trump on **September 18, 2026**. (CBS News, Oct. 4; published Oct. 5 05:04 ET)
- The Act authorizes tariffs of up to **100%** on imports from countries that are among the largest purchasers of Russian crude oil or natural gas and continue buying Russian energy; it separately permits tariffs of up to **500%** on Russian goods. (Davis Polk, Oct. 2; published Oct. 3 00:00 ET)
- U.S. Trade Representative **Jamieson Greer** said on October 1 that the administration must issue a report within **30 days**, would “name names” during October, and that the type of action would be Trump’s decision. (Semafor, Oct. 1; published Oct. 2 12:02 ET)
- Greer said India’s Russian oil and gas purchases were “really kind of a wartime-discount situation” and that the European Union had largely shifted to American oil and gas. (Semafor, Oct. 1; published Oct. 2 12:02 ET)
- As of the October 5 12:20 ET cutoff, no reported administration decision imposed secondary tariffs on China or India.
