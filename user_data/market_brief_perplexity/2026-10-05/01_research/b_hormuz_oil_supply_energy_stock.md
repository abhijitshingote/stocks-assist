## Strait of Hormuz proposal [Iran–US diplomacy]

- Iranian Foreign Minister **Abbas Araghchi** said on **October 4, 2026**, that Tehran’s “seven-day plan” could reopen the Strait of Hormuz within **seven days if the United States accepts it**; he said Iran had communicated its conditions for reopening the waterway. (Reuters, Oct. 4; 13:24 ET)[1]

- Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister **Kazem Gharibabadi** said on **October 4** that Washington had sent its views on the seven-day proposal through intermediaries and that Iran was reviewing the response internally; Tehran had not yet announced its final position by the **October 5, 12:20 ET** cutoff. (Mehr News, Oct. 4; updated Oct. 5, 05:42 ET)[11]

- Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman **Esmaeil Baghaei** said the US response focused mainly on **nuclear issues**, while Iran’s immediate focus was reopening the Strait of Hormuz; he said Iran had conveyed its position to the Qatari mediator. (China Daily, Oct. 4; updated Oct. 5, 06:05 ET)[13]

- Iranian parliament speaker **Mohammad Baqer Qalibaf** said on October 4 that the Strait would not reopen until **seven Iranian conditions** under the June “Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding” were met. (Reuters, Oct. 4; 13:24 ET)[1]

- The US government had responded through intermediaries before October 4, but the sources available here do not identify a new US public acceptance of the seven-day plan by **12:20 ET on October 5**. (Reuters, Oct. 4; 13:24 ET)[1]
