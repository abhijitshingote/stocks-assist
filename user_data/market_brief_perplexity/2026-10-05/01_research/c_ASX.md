## ASE Technology Holding Co., Ltd. (ASX) `[Test & Advanced Packaging]`
- ASE Technology closed at **$47.45**, up **6.08%** on Friday, October 2, 2026; it reached an intraday record of **$47.93** and moved above the **$45.52** cup-base buy point. (Investor’s Business Daily, Oct. 2, 2026; StockAnalysis, Oct. 3, 2026)
- ASE Technology reported **August consolidated revenue of TWD 82.247 billion**, up **45.70%** year over year from TWD 56.466 billion; its assembly, testing and materials business generated **TWD 51.287 billion**, up from TWD 33.510 billion. (Ad-hoc-News/Markets Insider, Oct. 2, 2026)
- **Bank of America Securities analyst Haas Liu** maintained a **Buy** rating on September 30, 2026; no price-target change was reported in the October 2 publication. (Ad-hoc-News/Markets Insider, Oct. 2, 2026)
- Investor’s Business Daily reported that ASE’s second-quarter earnings were **$0.28 per ADR**, up **155%** year over year, while revenue rose **16%** to **$5.88 billion**; the publication’s FactSet forecast for the current quarter was **$0.32 per share** and **$7.21 billion** in revenue. (Investor’s Business Daily, Oct. 2, 2026)
- SinoPac analyst **Yen Chun Wang** rated ASE **Buy** and cited robust demand for LEAP services; ASE management’s 2026 LEAP-sales guidance was above **$3.5 billion**, with at least **$7 billion** expected in 2027. (Investor’s Business Daily, Oct. 2, 2026)
- ASE benefited from order spillover from **Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSM)** and volume production ramp-up for **AMD’s next-generation Venice CPU**, according to analysts cited by Investor’s Business Daily. (Investor’s Business Daily, Oct. 2, 2026)
- Semiconductor packaging and testing stocks moved higher alongside ASE as demand for **AI and high-performance-computing applications** supported outsourced semiconductor assembly and test providers; no separate company-specific regulatory, lawsuit, FDA, offering or acquisition announcement was identified in the supplied search results. (TradingKey, Oct. 2, 2026)
- ASE’s ADR traded at **$47.90**, up **0.95%**, in after-hours trading at **7:59 p.m. ET on October 2**. (StockAnalysis, Oct. 3, 2026)
