## NN, Inc. (NNBR) `Specialty Manufacturing`
- NN, Inc. announced a **$50 million net PIPE financing** on Friday, October 2, 2026, with closing expected on or about October 5, subject to customary conditions. (GlobeNewswire via Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, 08:00 ET)
- The financing covers **16.1 million common shares or pre-funded warrants** priced at **$3.30 per share** or **$3.29 per warrant**. (NN, Inc. release via StockTitan, Oct. 2, 08:00 ET)
- NN said most proceeds would redeem and eliminate its remaining **Series D Preferred Stock** held by funds managed by **Morgan Stanley Tactical Value**. (Investing.com, Oct. 2, 08:06 ET)
- The remaining proceeds are allocated to **capital expenditures for new equipment** and **working capital** supporting higher sales. (Investing.com, Oct. 2, 08:06 ET)
- CEO **Harold Bevis** said the next step is refinancing NN’s high-cost term loan, with the company targeting a **multi-million-dollar reduction in cash interest expense**. (MarketChameleon, Oct. 2, time not stated)
- NN reported **third-quarter 2026 sales at their highest level in eight years**, approximately **$130 million of annual new business secured over the prior 12 months**, and more than **200 programs won during 2026**. (Investing.com, Oct. 2, 08:06 ET)
- NN said it is preparing to enter the **cable-assemblies** market through a new plant startup in **Mexico**. (Investing.com, Oct. 2, 08:06 ET)
- NNBR closed Friday, October 2, at **$4.51**, up **24.24%** from **$3.63**; the reported intraday range was **$3.67–$4.79**. (Ad-hoc-news.de, Oct. 5, 06:24 ET)
- NNBR was reported at **$4.55 in post-market trading at 22:06 ET on October 2**, representing a **25.23%** increase from the $3.63 regular-session close. (MEXC, Oct. 2, 22:09 ET)
- On Friday, October 2, NNBR was also identified among industrial stocks rising after September U.S. payrolls increased by **29,000 versus an 84,000 consensus estimate**, while unemployment rose to **4.2%**; NNBR was reported up **8.1%** in that broader market move. (Yahoo Finance, Oct. 2, publication time not stated)
- NN’s September 22, 2026 sales guidance was reported at **$470 million–$490 million**, raised from **$460 million–$480 million**. (Ad-hoc-news.de, Oct. 5, 06:24 ET)
- NN’s second-quarter 2026 results were reported as **$0.11 EPS versus a $0.06 analyst estimate** and **$128.74 million revenue versus a $116.09 million estimate**. (MarketBeat, Oct. 3, publication time not stated)
- A five-analyst consensus was reported as **Hold**, comprising **2 Buy, 2 Hold, and 1 Sell** ratings, with an average price target of **$5.67**. (MarketBeat, Sept. 27, outside the requested window; included only as direct context for the October 2 move)
