### 🧭 Thematic / Sector Weakness

#### Semiconductor / memory / wafer-fab equipment selloff → `Sector`

- **SKHY** -5.2%, **INTC** -6.3%, **AMD** -5.9%, **STX** -5.6%, **LRCX** -5.3%, **AMAT** -4.9%
- SK Hynix’s Seoul-listed shares fell ~11% on Oct 15, and the KOSPI dropped ~7.6%; memory makers lost roughly **$1.09 trillion** in combined value since Oct 2.
- Macro data in ticker articles: September **PPI +6.3% y/y**, hottest since Dec 2022; September **CPI +3.7%**; Fed hike odds near **33% for Oct 28**.
- Broadcom and Intel CEOs flagged **capital and cost of capital** as AI’s biggest bottleneck; Broadcom’s Hock Tan: “The biggest constraint is capital.”
- China supply fears: **CXMT** DRAM progress and **YMTC** becoming third-largest NAND maker; Elon Musk: “China will be making a LOT of RAM in 2028.”
- TSMC beat Q3 with **$46.7B revenue**, guided Q4 to **$52.3B–$54.3B**, maintained 2026 capex **$60B–$64B**, but shares still fell.
- Equipment group: LRCX closed down for **9 straight sessions**; Applied Materials declined to give a WFE forecast on investor day; Evercore cut AMAT PT to **$590**.
- **Link**: These names share the same semiconductor supply-chain catalysts—AI financing cost, China memory competition, hot inflation, and the chip-sector rout.

#### AI neocloud / debt-funded infrastructure weakness → `Sector`

- **CRWV** -11.9%, **NBIS** -11.9%
- CoreWeave’s 2031/2032 bond yields neared **15%**, 5-year CDS briefly topped **1,140bp**; debt and lease obligations ~**$52B**, Q2 net interest expense **$640M vs $267M** a year earlier.
- D.A. Davidson’s Gil Luria said CoreWeave’s equity “is going to be zero” in a downside scenario; company borrows ~9% vs ~1% returns.
- **Gartner** projected **80% of neoclouds** will cease independent operations by 2030, with **>$3.5T** AI infrastructure spend exposed to impairment; report named CRWV, NBIS, IREN.
- Nebius article cited AI-infrastructure debt pressure; stock traded **22% below its 20-day SMA**.
- **Link**: Both are capital-intensive AI-infrastructure providers under pressure from the same debt-financing fear; CoreWeave is the focal point, Nebius trades in sympathy.

*No other shared sector/theme driver identified from ticker articles.*
