### 🧭 Thematic / Sector Weakness

**AI Capex Shock / Enterprise Software** → `Technology`

- **ORCL** (-9.1%), **SAP** (-4.2%), **APP** (-3.5%), **CRM** (-2.9%), **NOW** (-2.3%), **ADSK** (-5.5%), **ADBE** (-4.9%), **WDAY** (-4.4%), **PTC** (-10.6%), **BSY** (-7.1%)
- Oracle reported Q4 revenue of $19.18B (beat estimates) and adjusted EPS of $2.11, but guided FY27 gross capital expenditure to $90‑95 billion vs. ~$61B consensus.
- The company plans to raise approximately $40 billion in debt and equity to fund the AI data‑center buildout, leaving free cash flow deeply negative.
- The iShares Expanded Tech‑Software ETF (IGV) fell 1.3% on June 11, its worst industry‑group performance; BofA flagged Oracle’s capex hike intensified worries about funding duration.
- Multiple ticker articles explicitly link the Oracle print to a brutal software session: Salesforce, GoDaddy, PTC and others fell in sympathy as the sector repriced AI spending risk.

- **ORCL**: Execution of the $40B debt/equity raise and FY27 capex buildout may further pressure the stock. Gushenheim’s $400 PT contrasts with Stephens’ $164, reflecting deep valuation uncertainty. All eyes on whether OpenAI revenue catches up to the compute it has ordered.
- **ADBE**: Q2 beat but CFO Dan Durn leaves June 15; the strategy shift to freemium with deferred Creative Cloud price hikes lowers H2 ARR growth. Analysts slashed price targets (Stifel to $200, Evercore ISI to $225), and the stock is near 52‑week lows. Next earnings report in ~3 months.
- **MSFT**: Xbox layoffs expected shortly after June 30 fiscal year‑end. Additional risk from the increasingly competitive AI landscape and ongoing CEO succession at Adobe/others.
- **ZG**: Google’s nationwide home‑listing expansion is now live; further traffic erosion could materially impact Zillow’s agent‑advertising revenue.
- **DAN**: Integration planning for the Eaton Mobility deal will continue through 2027; cost synergies are targeted at $250M but execution risk remains.
