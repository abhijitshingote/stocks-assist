### 🧭 Thematic / Sector Weakness

**Information Technology Services — Accenture Guidance Cut Ripples** → `Technology`

- **ACN** -17.6%, **IBM** -4.3%, **INFY** -9.6%, **CTSH** -8.8%, **EPAM** -11.6%, **GLOB** -10.1%
- Accenture reported Q3 revenue $18.700B (miss $18.745B est.) and narrowed FY26 sales guidance to $71.763B-$72.460B from prior $71.763B-$73.157B, below the $74.006B Street view.
- IBM, INFY, CTSH, EPAM, and GLOB all fell on the Accenture read-across; no separate company‑specific news triggered those moves.
- The Benzinga article explicitly named CTSH, GLOB, and EPAM as declining on the Accenture read-across; IBM was called out in its own article as falling after Accenture lowered guidance.

**Energy — Oil Decline on Iran Peace Deal** → `Energy`

- **BP** -2.7%, **COP** -2.6%, **CVX** -2.2%
- WTI crude fell ~2% to $75 after the US‑Iran 14‑point MOU committed to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, lifting the naval blockade and promising Iranian crude waivers.
- The Iran deal’s oil‑supply implications directly pressured energy stocks; the sector was the worst performer in the S&P 500 that day.

**Crypto / Bitcoin — Hawkish Fed Sinks Digital Assets** → `Technology`

- **MSTR** -5.8%
- Bitcoin slid below $63,000 after Fed Chair Warsh kept rates unchanged but signaled a hawkish shift, with the dot plot pointing to a potential hike in 2026.
- MSTR’s STRC preferred stock hit record lows ($85.32 intraday), as the company’s Bitcoin‑treasury model came under renewed scrutiny.
- Broader crypto market fell ~4%, dragging down MSTR as a high‑beta Bitcoin proxy.

### 🎯 Idiosyncratic Movers

**PFE** (-2.6%, mega) → Idiosyncratic
- CFO Dave Denton will step down on August 15 to take a role outside healthcare. Interim CFO Cecile Guegan named. The departure overshadowed Pfizer’s reaffirmed FY26 guidance.

**PLTR** (-2.3%, mega) → Idiosyncratic
- Michael Burry highlighted that Ukraine’s defense minister stated the country built its own Delta software “no worse” than Palantir and is increasingly using it instead, adding weight to Burry’s bearish thesis.

**STLD** (-7.9%, large) → Idiosyncratic
- Steel Dynamics issued Q2 EPS guidance of $3.51‑$3.55 (vs. Q1 $2.78) after the close on June 17; B of A and Wells Fargo cut price targets. The market reacted negatively to the outlook and analyst actions.

**KR** (-7.9%, large) → Idiosyncratic
- Q1 adjusted EPS $1.58 missed the $1.59 consensus; gross margin contracted on higher transportation costs, egg deflation, and price investments. Management guided Q2 ID sales (ex‑fuel) roughly in line with Q1, and FY EPS reaffirmed at $5.10‑$5.30.

**NVCR** (-18.8%, mid) → Idiosyncratic
- Phase 3 TRIDENT trial in newly diagnosed glioblastoma did not meet its primary endpoint of improved overall survival (median OS 17.7 vs. 17.5 months, p=0.519).

**AXTI** (-12.7%, mid) → Idiosyncratic
- A stock lockup expiration of 8.6 million shares is set for June 21, 2026, creating near‑term selling pressure. (Separately, AXT’s subsidiary secured a $25.4M InP wafer supply agreement, but that did not offset the lockup overhang.)
