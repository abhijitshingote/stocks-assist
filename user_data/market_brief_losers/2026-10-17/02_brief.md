### 🧭 Thematic / Sector Weakness

**AI Infrastructure / Neocloud Debt Fears** → `Technology`

- **CRWV** (-11.9%, large) and **NBIS** (-11.9%, large) fell on overlapping neocloud credit stress and a Gartner shakeout warning.
- CoreWeave five-year credit default swaps hit a record **1,194.5 bps** on Oct 15, implying **>60% five-year default probability** per standard market math (ICE/Bloomberg). Its **9.75% notes due Oct 2031** traded at **81.80 cents on the dollar**, yielding **15.08%**; the **9.625% 2032 notes** yielded **>15.4%**.
- Gartner warned **>80% of neocloud providers** could cease independent operations by 2030; it estimates AI data center owners need **$8.5T cumulative revenue through 2030** vs. **$3.6T forecast**, with **>$3.5T of AI infrastructure spending exposed to impairment by 2030**.
- Ticker articles explicitly linked the selloff to CoreWeave debt costs and Gartner’s forecast for **CRWV, NBIS, and IREN**; NBIS was down roughly **20% for the week** and tested its **200-day SMA near $174.10**.

**Semiconductor and AI Hardware Rollover** → `Technology`

- **AMD** (-5.9%), **INTC** (-6.3%), **STX** (-5.6%), **LRCX** (-5.3%), **SKHY** (-5.2%), **MRVL** (-5.0%), **AMAT** (-4.9%), **ARM** (-6.0%), and **CBRS** (-9.3%) moved together on memory/China competition plus AI financing-cost pressure.
- China’s **CXMT** reported progress on a denser **4F-squared DRAM architecture**; Citi (via WSJ) said mass production is unlikely before 2028 but the oversupply risk hit memory names. Counterpoint reported **YMTC became the world’s third-largest NAND maker**.
- **SOXX fell for a ninth straight session**, its longest streak since 2006; the PHLX Semiconductor Index sits **~22% below its June 22 record**; six memory/storage makers lost **~$1.09T combined since Oct 2**.
- **SK Hynix ADR** fell **24.3% for the week**; South Korea’s KOSPI logged its **worst week since Oct 2008 (-14.46%)**, with SK hynix **-57% from its summer peak**.
- Broadcom CEO **Hock Tan** said capital may be the biggest AI constraint; Intel CEO **Lip-Bu Tan** echoed financing/ROA concerns. Hot PPI (**6.3% Y/Y**) and CPI (**3.7% Y/Y**) raised Fed-hike bets, per ticker articles.

**Software / Cybersecurity AI Debt Fears** → `Technology`

- **SNOW** (-8.2%), **PANW** (-6.9%), **FTNT** (-6.8%), **CRWD** (-6.4%), **TWLO** (-10.9%), **OKTA** (-10.7%), and **NET** (-5.9%) sold off under the same AI-financing scare.
- Benzinga’s midday Oct 16 article stated: **TWLO** led Russell 1000 decliners **-10.7%**, **OKTA** **-8.9%**, **DDOG** **-8.7%**, **CRWD** **-7.4%**, **PANW** **-7.1%**, and **SNOW** **-7.1%**, as software and cybersecurity names got slammed while utilities, staples, and healthcare led.
- **Broadcom CEO Hock Tan** comments on capital costs and **Gartner’s neocloud warning** fed the rotation out of high-multiple software, per ticker articles.

**RKLB** (-8.0%, large) → Idiosyncratic
