### 🎯 Idiosyncratic Movers

**AZN** (-5.4%, Mega) → Idiosyncratic  
- Phase 3 CARDIO‑TTRansform trial of eplontersen for ATTR‑CM missed primary endpoint vs placebo.  
- Pre‑specified subgroup showed nominally significant HR 0.71 with monotherapy, but no benefit in patients already on stabilizers.  
- Full data to be presented at ESC Congress in August 2026.

**COST** (-4.2%, Mega) → Idiosyncratic (with broad staple overhang)  
- JPMorgan lowered price target from $1,110 to $1,100, Overweight maintained.  
- June sales update: net sales $29.24B (+10.6% YoY), US comps +10.6%, Canada +3.7%; gasoline deflation and FX flagged as headwinds.  
- An article on consumer staples ETFs (Warsh’s Collateral Victim) highlighted Costco as a top holding vulnerable to bear steepening, tariffs, and weakening consumer.

**PEP** (-4.1%, Mega) → Idiosyncratic  
- Q2 earnings: core EPS $2.20 missed $2.21 est; revenue $24.18B beat $23.96B.  
- Management cited high gas prices slowing impulse purchases in convenience channels.  
- Full‑year guidance reaffirmed (organic sales growth 2‑4%, adj. EPS $8.55‑$8.71).  
- Wells Fargo cut PT to $140, Citigroup downgraded to Neutral with PT $145.

**CRM** (-2.8%, Mega) → Idiosyncratic  
- KeyBanc downgraded to Sector Weight, citing difficult‑to‑find evidence of meaningful upside and soft channel checks.  
- Agentforce events feedback suggested product still needs development; recent quarterly results disappointing.

**IBM** (-2.6%, Mega) → Idiosyncratic  
- Bloomberg report: Starbucks building in‑house AI‑powered alternatives to replace IBM maintenance management tool, with potential rollout by end of next year.  
- Starbucks aims to cut $2B in costs, with $10M in software savings targeted this fiscal year.

**PLTR** (-2.4%, Mega) → Idiosyncratic  
- Rebound Capital warned Palantir remains a “trap” at ~80x forward earnings; forward‑deployed engineering costs mask low true gross margins.  
- Competition from Amazon ($1B) and Microsoft ($2.5B) embedding engineers, plus European data‑sovereignty headwinds (France migrating to ChapsVision).

**BP** (-1.6%, Mega) → Idiosyncratic  
- California drivers sued BP and other gas station operators, alleging use of AI‑powered pricing tool that coordinated fuel price increases in violation of state antitrust law.

**TW** (-3.5%, Large) → Idiosyncratic  
- Barclays lowered PT to $114 (Equal‑Weight), contributing to the selloff.

**ICE** (-2.7%, Large) → Idiosyncratic

**DVN** (-2.5%, Large) → Idiosyncratic

**RXT** (-32.9%, Mid & small) → Idiosyncratic

**CABO** (-13.1%, Mid & small) → Idiosyncratic

**GVA** (-11.5%, Mid & small) → Idiosyncratic
