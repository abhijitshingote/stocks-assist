### 🧭 Thematic / Sector Weakness

**Meta Muse agentic-AI / consumer-inertia disruption** → `Communication Services / Consumer Cyclical`

- **GOOGL** -4.0%, **GOOG** -4.0%, **BKNG** -4.2%, **ABNB** -6.8%.
- **Booking Holdings** commentary framed the threat as a “gatekeeper swap”: Booking spent ~$8.2B on marketing in 2025, ~30% of revenue, and the article flagged AI agents taking over travel search/booking. The same article noted **Airbnb** and Tripadvisor down >4% Wednesday and **Expedia** down as much as 6% after Morgan Stanley downgraded Expedia to Underweight; Booking was called lower in sympathy.
- **Alphabet** article stated shares fell as a broader selloff hit companies vulnerable to disruption from Meta’s Muse AI agent. Goldman Sachs’ “consumer inertia” basket includes Expedia and Booking; the concern is that agents handling tasks directly may reduce search/query activity or platform dependence.
- Link: All four are tied to the same documented Meta Muse agentic-AI threat: Booking/Airbnb on travel/customer inertia; Alphabet on search ad demand.

**Memory/semiconductor cycle doubt** → `Technology`

- **SNDK** -4.2%, **SKHY** -3.1%.
- **SanDisk** article: Martin Shkreli said he is short Micron and warned “if memory prices drop, it’s all over”; one trader on his desk was long ~$1M SNDK as a “momentum AI play,” while Shkreli separately called semiconductor stocks broadly expensive. A fund manager also warned memory earnings are cyclical and may be misleading near peak.
- **SK Hynix** article: Michael Burry doubled down on bearish memory bets, citing Acer CEO Jason Chen that memory inventories are building and Chinese suppliers are adding volume; CXMT advanced DRAM production and Samsung/SK Hynix/Micron capacity shifts to HBM were flagged.
- Link: Both are memory/semiconductor names under the same documented supply/cyclicality debate, with SNDK/SKHY caught in the same memory-boom-doubt trade.

### 🎯 Idiosyncratic Movers

**MCD** (-5.4%, Mega cap) → Idiosyncratic

- 2026 Investor Day: $8.5B franchisee support plan through 2036 (~$5B through 2030) spooked investors; stock hit a four-year low and was headed for a seventh weekly loss, the worst since July 2014.
- Management targets operating margin low-to-mid 50% by 2030 and free cash flow conversion mid-to-high 80%; admitted “falling short” on consistent execution.
- UBS PT $320, Citi $310, Morgan Stanley $308; Seaport Global initiated Neutral, saying improvement unlikely before mid-2027.

**BABA** (-4.7%, Mega cap) → Mixed

- Fell after unveiling its new AI chip (Zhenwu V900, described as 3x computing power of M890) and outlining cloud expansion in Turkey, Finland and Netherlands over 12 months; 20 GW data center target by 2032; $53B three-year AI investment plan.
- Ticker article also cited caution on large-cap technology and China-linked stocks, with the stock ~15.6% below its 200-day SMA after an April death cross.

**BX** (-4.0%, Mega cap) → Idiosyncratic

- Blackstone’s $77.2B Blackstone Private Credit Fund capped redemptions at 5% after investors sought ~10% of shares; BCRED received $4.3B repurchase requests in Q3.
- Article also flagged Apollo and Morgan Stanley private credit funds facing elevated redemption pressure, but Blackstone’s own fund action was the direct BX catalyst.

**APP** (-4.0%, Mega cap) → Idiosyncratic

- Edgewater Research analyst Joe Wittine said MAX share reached a “functional ceiling”; Q4 revenue growth seen at 8-9% q/q, versus Q3 guidance $2.055B-$2.085B on $1.92B prior quarter.
- Called it a “show-me story”; Unity flagged as competitive threat compressing net revenue spreads.

**ORCL** (-3.8%, Mega cap) → Mixed
