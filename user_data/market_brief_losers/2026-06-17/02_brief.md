- The Nasdaq fell 1.15% on Tuesday as investors rotated away from technology stocks that had surged the prior session on the U.S.-Iran peace deal and the Strait of Hormuz reopening.

### 🎯 Idiosyncratic Movers

**HUN** (-18.5%, mid & small cap) → Idiosyncratic  
- Announced an all-stock merger-of-equals with Olin Corporation (OLN). Huntsman shareholders receive 0.5476 OLN shares per HUN share. The combined entity, OlinHuntsman, expects $400M+ in synergies. Market reacted negatively to the dilutive structure; HUN shares fell sharply while OLN shares rose.

**GIL** (-18.2%, mid & small cap) → Idiosyncratic  
- Jehoshaphat Research published a short-seller report alleging channel stuffing, off-balance-sheet factoring, and an $800M revenue gap for 2H26. The report also highlighted governance concerns including recent executive departures from the Ethics committee.

**NFLX** (-3.3%, mega cap) → Idiosyncratic  
- Semafor reported Netflix lost a bidding contest for Roku to Fox, which offered $22B. The article also noted Netflix’s earlier pursuit of Warner Bros. Discovery, raising M&A strategy and integration concerns. Co-CEO Ted Sarandos previously acknowledged building “M&A muscle.”

**PDD** (-2.3%, mega cap) → Idiosyncratic  
- BNP Paribas initiated coverage with an Underperform rating and $89 price target, citing concerns about the Chinese e-commerce firm’s growth outlook.

**KLAC** (-6.2%, mega cap) → Idiosyncratic  
- Cantor Fitzgerald maintained Overweight but drastically cut its price target to $250 from $2,500. (The prior target may reflect a data error, but the reported cut served as the day’s only ticker-specific catalyst.)

**BLSH** (-11.0%, mid & small cap) → Idiosyncratic  
- JP Morgan maintained Neutral and lowered its price target to $26 from $43. No other ticker-specific news.

**BOT, AXTI, VCX, ALTO, ELVR, QUBT, WOLF** (mid & small cap) → Unknown  
- No material ticker-specific news in the window. Declines may be technical, sympathy moves, or linked to broader sector pressure.
