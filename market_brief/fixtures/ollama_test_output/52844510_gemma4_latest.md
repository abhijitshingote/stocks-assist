### ARM Holdings (NASDAQ:ARM)

**Sell-side Actions & Forecasts**
*   **Mizuho:** Raised forecast to **$360** with an Outperform rating (as of May 28).
*   **Bernstein:** Initiated coverage with an Outperform rating and **$300** target.
*   **Barclays:** Lifted forecast to **$250** while maintaining an Overweight rating.
*   Current consensus rating is Buy, with forecast targets ranging from **$120** (BofA Securities) to **$360** (Mizuho).

**Investment Thesis & Structural Drivers**
*   The AI market is shifting from chatbot applications toward agentic AI systems, structurally benefiting Arm's power-efficient CPU architecture (David Dai, Bernstein).
*   CEO Rene Haas noted that AI agents are increasing the importance of CPUs across cloud and data center environments.
*   Arm's AGI CPU targets a data-center CPU opportunity exceeding **$100 billion by 2030**.
*   The technology could reduce AI data-center capital spending by up to **$10 billion per gigawatt** through stronger rack-level efficiency versus x86 systems.

**Demand / Fundamentals**
*   **Q4 Fiscal 2026 Results:** Reported revenue of **$1.49 billion** (up **20%** YoY).
*   Licensing revenue climbed **29%** to **$819 million**, marking the strongest quarterly revenue performance in company history.
*   Committed customer demand for AGI CPU exceeded **$2 billion** across fiscal 2027 and 2028, more than doubling from previous disclosures.

**Long-Range Targets**
*   **Fiscal 2031 Targets:** CFO Jason Child maintained long-term goals of **$15 billion** in AGI CPU revenue and **$10 billion** in IP revenue.

**Risks and Constraints**
*   **Supply:** Current manufacturing capacity can satisfy only about **half** of the demand for AI-focused data-center CPUs.
*   **Valuation:** The stock is trading at a P/E of **356.1x**, indicating a premium valuation.
*   **Technical Risk:** The stock is pressing the top of its 52-week range, sitting just below the **$325** high, keeping "breakout versus fade" risk elevated.
